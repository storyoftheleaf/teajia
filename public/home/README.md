# Stand-in photographs for the home page's three plates

These are placeholders, not the shop's own pictures. They are here rather than
hot-linked because `scripts/check-china-dependencies.mjs` refuses Unsplash and
similar stock hosts anywhere under `src/` or `public/`: a customer in China
cannot load them, so a page that depends on one is a page that does not render
for them.

Source: Unsplash, free to use, converted to webp at 1400px.
Replaced the moment the real photographs exist, and this folder goes with them.
The open item is in TODO.md.

## Real photographs (2026-09-28)

`teapots-sepia.webp` (the opener, cropped to the pots and cups, light sepia and faded blacks baked in, no grain) and `pour-hands.webp` (Adrian pouring, cropped to
the hands and cups, the consult plate) are Adrian's own, from his library, 2000px webp.
`porcelain-bowl.webp` is the story's gold-mended bowl, cropped with the bowl
dead centre. The consult stand-in stays only because the Craft page still
borrows it.
