## Northern Pu’er country {#scope}

Lincang lies north of Pu’er Prefecture and against Myanmar to the west. Its tea geography includes Mengku, Yongde, Fengqing, Bangdong, Bingdao, and Xigui, with repeated place names such as Daxueshan making precise labels especially important. [cite:teadb-lincang]

## A broad regional tendency {#common-character}

Young single-origin Lincang sheng is often described as green, firm, and bitter. This reputation is useful as a starting point only: Lincang is a large prefecture, and material from its counties, mountains, and villages should not be collapsed into one tasting profile. [cite:teadb-lincang]

## History beyond single origins {#history}

Lincang has a smaller public archive of famous aged single-origin teas than Yiwu or Menghai. Older blended productions, especially those associated with Xiaguan, nevertheless provide a historical thread, even when the exact origin of their base material cannot be reconstructed.
