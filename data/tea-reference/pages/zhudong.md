## Where Zhudong is {#where-it-is}

Zhudong is a township in central Hsinchu County, in northwestern Taiwan, east of Hsinchu city. It sits where the plain begins to rise into the hills. It is a town more than a tea village, and tea is only one of the things grown in the hills around it.

## Zhudong and Oriental Beauty {#in-oriental-beauty}

Zhudong is sometimes named among the Hsinchu townships that grow Oriental Beauty, the honeyed, insect-bitten oolong of the county. Most places that write about the tea name Beipu and Emei as its heart, and some add Hengshan. Zhudong appears in only a few lists, and what makes its tea different from its neighbours is not established. A label reading Zhudong is a rare find rather than a known style.
