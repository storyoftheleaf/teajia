## Where Kunlu Mountain is {#where}

Kunlu Mountain is a spur of the Wuliang Mountains in Ning’er Hani and Yi Autonomous County, Pu’er City, Yunnan. The name is also written with other characters in older writing. Its old tea trees cover a little over 10,000 mu, about 667 hectares, and places give its height as about 1,410 to 2,271 metres.

## The trees {#trees}

The trees are tall and mostly unpruned. Wild, transitional and planted types grow together, with large, medium and small leaves mixed. The trade knows the mountain for these old trees.

## Tribute tea {#tribute-history}

In the Qing period, tea from Kunlu was made as tribute tea and carried to Beijing by horse caravan, about two months along the Tea Horse Road. Making Pu’er tribute tea in Ning’er is now listed as national intangible cultural heritage. Many sellers call the mountain an imperial tea garden, but the date and the details of that role are not settled.

## What people often notice {#common-impressions}

Sellers most often describe a light, graceful fragrance and a sweet aftertaste, with some bitterness and astringency that turn sweet.
