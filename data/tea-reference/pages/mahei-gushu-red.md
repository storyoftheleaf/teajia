## Reading the tea’s name {#tea-name}

Mahei names the village in Yiwu. Gushu means “old tree,” the description on this tea’s label. Hong cha, the last two words, means “red tea.” Together the words give a place, a description of the trees and a style of tea. [cite:teajia-mahei-red]
