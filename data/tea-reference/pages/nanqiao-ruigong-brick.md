## A brick on the Malaysian tea market {#malaysian-tea-fair}

In the catalogue for a Malaysian tea fair in 2022, a dark rectangle of pressed tea sits among four cream wrappers printed in red. The exhibitor, Quanyuxuan, offered five Ruigong bricks together at stall 27. The caption identifies that particular offering as ripe tea from 2008, with 250 grams in each brick. [cite:dahonghua-ruigong-brick]

## Where the Nanqiao name comes from {#nanqiao-name}

Nanqiao was once the name of the county around Mengzhe in southern Yunnan. Menghai County Nanqiao Tea Company, founded in February 2004, kept that old place name. The China Tea Marketing Association identifies Che Fo Nan as the company’s brand and records that it sold teas in Hong Kong, Taiwan and Southeast Asia. [cite:ctma-nanqiao-company]
