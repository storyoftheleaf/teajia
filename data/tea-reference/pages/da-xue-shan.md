## One name, several mountains {#where-it-is}

Da Xue Shan means Big Snow Mountain, and Yunnan has more than one mountain called that. In Lincang alone, trade writing lists four: the Mengku one on the Bangma ridge in Shuangjiang County, the Bangdong one east of Lincang County, the Yongde one in the south-east of Yongde County, and the Fengqing one near Sanchahu. The name on a label does not settle which of them the leaf came from.

## The one the trade usually means {#which-one}

When pu'er writing says Da Xue Shan, it most often means the Mengku mountain: the main peak of the Bangma ridge, on the border of Shuangjiang and Gengma counties. Teas from it are often sold under the Mengku name. The Yongde mountain is the next most common, and sellers of its tea name Yongde or the reserve there. A seller who writes only Da Xue Shan has not said which one, and the other three are not rare enough to rule out.

## Wild trees in the forest {#wild-trees}

People value Da Xue Shan for its wild tea trees. On the Mengku mountain a wild ancient tree community grows on the mid slopes in dense forest, at roughly 2,200 to 2,750 metres, and is described as large and well kept. The tea there is left to grow without pruning, fertilizer or managed picking paths. The core of that forest is now closed and protected, so it is no longer picked. Tea sold from the mountain comes from lower and managed ground.

## Wild tea is not the same as old garden tea {#wild-and-old}

Wild tree tea, ancient garden tea and ordinary plantation tea are three different things, and Da Xue Shan is sold under all three. A cake described as wild arbor may come from trees tens of years old rather than from the ancient forest itself. Sellers describe raw tea from the mountain as strong and bitter, with a lasting sweet return, but taste varies by which mountain and which trees.

## Da Xue Shan on a label {#on-a-label}

Treat Da Xue Shan on a label as a lead, not a source. Ask which county the leaf came from, whether the trees are wild, ancient or planted, and what the seller means by wild. Menghai is sometimes named in this context, but a Da Xue Shan in Menghai is not established by the places read.
