## A Menghai maker with a thin record {#maker-history}

Xinghai Tea Factory is a pu’er maker in the Menghai area of Xishuangbanna, Yunnan. It is not one of the big named state factories, so little is written about it, and what is written comes mostly from sellers and from the company itself. Sellers describe it as a ripe pu’er maker that took up wet piling early among the smaller Menghai factories. That is the reason its ripe bricks and cakes, including aged ones, turn up with collectors. The factory’s start date is not settled. The company’s own page says 2002, while one seller calls it an early private factory of the 1990s and sells a Xinghai brick dated 1997. Until that is cleared up, treat the name as a maker’s name only, and do not read a date or a style from it.
