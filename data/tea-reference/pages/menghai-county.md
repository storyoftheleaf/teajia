## Western Xishuangbanna {#scope}

Menghai County anchors much of western Xishuangbanna’s Pu’er landscape. Names such as Nannuo, Bulang, Mengsong, Hekai, Pasha, and Bada describe distinct mountain areas within the county rather than interchangeable synonyms for Menghai. [cite:teadb-menghai-county]

## A forceful reputation {#common-character}

Specialist writing often contrasts Menghai’s strength and bitterness with the gentler reputation of eastern Xishuangbanna. That comparison is a broad regional convention: tree material, craft, blend, age, and storage still create large differences within the county. [cite:teadb-menghai-county]

## Factory-era importance {#history}

The establishment of Menghai Tea Factory and the post-1950 shift toward large-scale production made the county central to twentieth-century Pu’er. Regional leaf increasingly entered factory blends, connecting Menghai with many benchmark raw and ripe productions. [cite:teadb-menghai-county]
