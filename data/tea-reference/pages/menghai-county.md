## Why people value Menghai {#why-people-value-it}

For its trees, above all. Menghai’s old gardens grow in and beside living forest, in a warm, wet climate, and many of the trees are centuries old. That old-tree leaf is what gives its mountains their name among buyers. On Bulang it makes a strong cup whose bitterness turns quickly into a sweet, lasting finish, the quality the trade calls huigan. Nannuo gives a softer, more floral tea. The factory history made the name famous, but the trees are why buyers seek out its mountains.

## Western Xishuangbanna {#scope}

Menghai County holds most of the tea mountains of western Xishuangbanna. Nannuo, Bulang, Mengsong, Hekai, Pasha and Bada are distinct mountain areas within it, each with its own tea, not interchangeable names for Menghai. [cite:teadb-menghai-county]

## A forceful reputation {#common-character}

Menghai’s forceful reputation belongs mostly to its Bulang side, where raw pu’er is strong and bitter at first, then turns sweet and stays. Nannuo is softer and more floral, so the county is not one taste, and a strong Menghai set against a gentler east is a trade convention more than a rule. Tree age, craft, blend and storage change the cup a great deal within every mountain. [cite:teadb-menghai-county]

## Why the name is so well known {#history}

Menghai’s fame also comes from its factory. Menghai Tea Factory was founded in the 1940s, and as pu’er grew into a large state industry the county’s leaf went into factory blends, so many benchmark raw and ripe cakes trace back to Menghai. [cite:teadb-menghai-county]

## Mengsong and Naka {#mengsong-naka}

Naka is a village in Mengsong, one of Menghai County’s tea-growing areas. [cite:teadb-menghai-county]

## Where ripe pu’er began {#ripe-origins}

Ripe pu’er was born in the early 1970s, when Yunnan’s state factories learned to speed up ageing by wet piling the leaf, and Menghai Tea Factory was one of the first to master it. Its 7572 cake became the benchmark ripe recipe, the cup other ripe teas are still judged against. The number reads as the recipe itself: 75 for the year the recipe began, 7 for the main leaf grade in the blend, 2 for the Menghai factory.
