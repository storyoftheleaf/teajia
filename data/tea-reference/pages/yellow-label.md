## What Yellow Label means {#definition}

Yellow Label is a name taken from the colour of the label on a pu’er cake or its wrapper. It is not the mark of one maker. Cakes sold as yellow label come from different factories and different years, in both ripe and raw tea, and in several cake forms. The colour alone does not say who made the tea, what leaf went into it or how old it is. For those, the maker, the recipe number and the year on the wrapper are what to read.
