## Where Jingmai is {#where}

Jingmai is a tea mountain in Huimin Town, Lancang Lahu Autonomous County, in Pu’er City, Yunnan. Its old tea forests and villages were listed together as a UNESCO World Heritage cultural landscape in 2023. The listing names five tea forests, nine villages and three protective forests, around the villages of Jingmai and Mangjing. Places give the height of the tea as about 1,000 to 1,600 metres.

## Forest tea {#forest-tea}

The tea grows under the shade of the forest, with little pruning, and the trees are tall. The trade values Jingmai for this forest setting and for its old trees. It is sold as Pu’er, both raw and ripe, under the mountain’s name.

## Who grew it and since when {#history}

The Bulang and Dai peoples have grown tea on the mountain for a thousand years or more. The date most often given for the start is about the tenth century, though one local source says thirteen hundred years. An oral story names an ancestor of the Bulang as the first to tend the wild tea, but it is a story, not a record.

## What people often notice {#common-impressions}

Sellers and drinkers most often name a strong flower or fruit fragrance and a sweetness that comes early and lasts. Some find the bitterness light and the astringency stronger. Spring tea is said to carry more fragrance than summer or autumn tea.
