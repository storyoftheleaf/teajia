## A product name, not a grade {#what-it-is}

Golden Bull, Jin Niu in Chinese, is the name of a ripe pu’er from Hengfengyuan. No ripe pu’er grade scale includes it, and no maker or seller describes it as a leaf grade. Read it as a name the maker chose for a tea, like a brand line, and not as a statement about leaf size or quality. What the name stands for beyond that has not been established.

## Who Hengfengyuan is {#maker}

Hengfengyuan is the ripe tea brand of the Changtai group in Yunnan. It is sold mainly as ripe cakes, bricks and tuo, and many of its teas are named by recipe number or by a place or line name. Changtai also trades under other brands, so Hengfengyuan on a label tells you the maker, not the grade of the leaf.
