## The geographic frame {#geographic-frame}

Pu’er is unusually place-bound for a broad tea family. Yunnan is the central geographic frame, while comparable processing outside the province does not by itself make a tea Yunnan Pu’er. [cite:teadb-yunnan-overview]

## A map of regions {#regional-map}

Reference maps commonly begin with Xishuangbanna, Pu’er Prefecture, and Lincang, then move toward counties, mountain areas, villages, and local names. Xishuangbanna is often divided into eastern and western tea areas, while other production extends through Yunnan beyond those best-known centers. [cite:teadb-yunnan-overview]

## Read labels with care {#reading-labels}

Regional schemes are editorial aids rather than a single settled ladder. Boundaries, spellings, and the prominence given to one place over another vary, and famous names also function as commercial language. A place label is useful context, not proof of a tea’s exact origin. [cite:teadb-yunnan-overview]
