## A regional grouping {#regional-grouping}

Xishuangbanna is a warm, humid southern prefecture whose tea history is often introduced through a group of old tea mountains. The familiar list joins places across the prefecture for orientation; it should not be mistaken for a single administrative level or one continuous origin. [cite:ctma-xishuangbanna-twelve-mountains-2025]

## Mountain names {#mountain-names}

One contemporary grouping names Youle, Gedeng, Yibang, Mangzhi, Manzhuan, Yiwu, Xiao Mengsong, Nannuo, Hekai, Mengsong, Bulang, and Bada. Some names denote mountains, others broader tea areas, and their scale should remain visible whenever they are connected to an individual tea. [cite:ctma-xishuangbanna-twelve-mountains-2025]

## Living tea culture {#living-culture}

Tea in Xishuangbanna is inseparable from the knowledge and daily work of communities including Bulang, Hani, and Dai peoples. Trade routes, tribute history, cultivation, processing, and contemporary protection efforts all contribute to the region’s place in Pu’er culture. [cite:ctma-xishuangbanna-twelve-mountains-2025]
