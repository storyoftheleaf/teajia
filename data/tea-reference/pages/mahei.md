## The road through Mahei {#village-road-and-harvest}

A section of the old Tea Horse Road passes through Mahei. Ma Zhefeng photographed it while tracing the route through Yiwu. In April 2024, local reporters met Mahei farmer Ding Wanchun picking spring tea with his crew. He said his family needed more than twenty pickers a day.
