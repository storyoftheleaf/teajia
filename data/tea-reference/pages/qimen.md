## Where Qimen is {#where-it-is}

Qimen is a county in the south of Anhui Province, in the hills near the Huangshan mountains and close to the border with Jiangxi. It belongs to Huangshan City. Tea is grown in other parts of the same hill country, but the name Qimen on a tea record points to the county itself.

## Why people know Qimen {#known-for}

Qimen is known for one tea, Keemun, the red tea named after the county. The land suits it: hilly, wooded ground with mild, damp weather, plenty of rain and frequent cloud and mist. Keemun is made mostly from a small-leaved local tea plant, and the trade prizes its scent above everything else. That scent is so closely tied to the county that it has its own name, Qimen aroma. People describe it as floral and fruity, sometimes with a faint smokiness, over a smooth, sweet cup with little astringency. It is one of the classic Chinese teas of the export trade, and it has long been used in English-style breakfast blends.

## How Keemun began {#how-keemun-began}

Before the 1870s Qimen made green tea. Red tea was made there from about 1875, using methods carried in from Fujian, by way of Jiangxi in most tellings, after green tea sales fell. Hu Yuanlong, a local man who set up the Rishun tea factory, is the name most often given as the founder. Another account gives the credit to the tea merchant Yu Gancheng, who opened buying stations in Qimen. The accounts disagree and the exact order of events is not established. What is agreed is the period, the late Qing dynasty, and that Western buyers were looking for the tea within a few years.

## Keemun on a label {#on-a-label}

Keemun is a style as well as a place. Tea made by the Keemun method also comes from nearby counties in Anhui, such as Dongzhi, Shitai and Yi County, and from Fuliang in Jiangxi, so Keemun on a label does not by itself mean the leaf grew in Qimen County. The name also sells well, and tea sold as Keemun varies a lot in quality. Qimen, named as the place of origin, is the stronger claim. Black teas from Hubei sold as Hubei Keemun are not Keemun at all.
