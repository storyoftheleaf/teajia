## Who Hengfengyuan is {#who}

Hengfengyuan is a pu’er brand name belonging to Changtai, a private Yunnan tea company based in Xishuangbanna. It is one of several brands Changtai sells under, alongside others such as Yichang Hao, which is the group’s better known Yiwu line. Little is published about Hengfengyuan beyond its place inside Changtai, so this page says only that.

## A name for ripe tea {#ripe-brand}

Within Changtai, Hengfengyuan is the brand for fermented, or ripe, pu’er. The trade knows it for ripe cakes, tuo cha and loose ripe tea, and sellers who handle it describe the style as thick and mellow. A tea sold under the Hengfengyuan name is therefore normally a shou tea, which is how this one is sold.

## Golden Bull is a separate mark {#golden-bull}
