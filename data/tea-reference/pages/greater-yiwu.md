## A name at several scales {#scope}

Yiwu can mean a township, a market center, or a much broader tea area in eastern Xishuangbanna. In trade, the name often reaches beyond the town itself, so a Yiwu label should be read as a regional claim whose scale still needs context. [cite:teadb-greater-yiwu]

## Shared reputation, varied tea {#common-character}

Greater Yiwu is often associated with a softer opening and a persistent sweet return when compared with the more forceful profiles linked to western Xishuangbanna. The area contains many growing places, however, and their material can vary substantially; this is a regional tendency, not a tasting promise for an individual tea. [cite:teadb-greater-yiwu]

## Trade history {#history}

Yiwu became an important collection and distribution point for tea from the old Six Famous Tea Mountains. Its trade connected local leaf, merchant networks, tribute routes, and later some of the best-known early twentieth-century tea firms. [cite:teadb-greater-yiwu]
