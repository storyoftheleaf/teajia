## Names in the tea market {#market-names}

Mountain and village names are prominent in the market for Yunnan Pu’er. A 2026 industry event published a purchase-price index spanning 108 named origins and highlighted names including Bohetang, Banzhang, Bingdao, Mansong, Fenghuangwo, Xigui, Jingmai, Yibang, and Manzhuan. [cite:ctma-yunnan-108-mountains-2026]

## What a list can tell us {#reading-the-list}

Such a list documents market attention at a particular date; it does not create a geographic hierarchy or authenticate the leaf in a finished cake. Prices can vary sharply by village, garden, tree category, and picking standard, so the names are best treated as research leads rather than interchangeable places. [cite:ctma-yunnan-108-mountains-2026]
