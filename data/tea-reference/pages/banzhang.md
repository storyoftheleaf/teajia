## Where Banzhang is {#where-it-is}

Banzhang is a group of tea villages inside Bulang Mountain, in the south of Menghai County in Xishuangbanna, Yunnan. The name covers more than one settlement. Lao Banzhang is the old village. Xin Banzhang is a separate village that grew out of it. Altitude is quoted differently from place to place, mostly between about 1,600 and 1,900 metres.

## Lao Banzhang and Xin Banzhang {#old-and-new}

Xin Banzhang means New Banzhang. It was founded by people who moved out of the old village. The two are priced very differently, with Lao Banzhang far higher. Sellers say Xin Banzhang is less bitter, but too few places agree on that to treat it as settled.

## The tea Banzhang is known for {#known-for}

Banzhang is known for raw pu’er from old trees. The trade describes it as strong and bitter at first, then quickly sweet, with a long aftertaste and a heavy body. It is one of the most expensive named villages in pu’er, and Lao Banzhang is often called the king of raw pu’er.

## A price that rose fast {#history}

Prices for fresh leaf and finished tea rose steeply from the early 2000s, and the village became one of the best known names in pu’er.

## Banzhang on a label {#on-a-label}

Banzhang on a label is a market name, and it is not proof that the leaf came from the village. The village makes a small amount of tea, and its name sells at a high price, so fakes and blends are widely reported. Some cakes sold under a Banzhang name blend in leaf from other Bulang villages. Lao Banzhang, Xin Banzhang and the wider Banzhang name are three different claims, and a label rarely says which one is meant.

## Banzhang in ripe pu’er {#in-ripe-pu-er}

Most of what the trade writes about Banzhang describes raw tea. Ripe cakes with Banzhang in the name do exist, but they are less common than raw ones, and the name is seldom backed by a stated leaf source. Reading Banzhang on a ripe cake as the village itself is not safe. It more often points to a Bulang style of ripe tea, thicker in body and stronger than a plain Menghai blend.
