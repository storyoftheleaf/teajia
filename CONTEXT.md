# Teajia Tea Knowledge

Teajia separates shared tea knowledge from the facts of a particular tea, the commercial relationship that supplied it, and a person's direct experience of it.

## Language

**Research Source**:
A website, book, institution, producer, or person cited as evidence for shared tea knowledge. A Research Source is not a Vendor unless Teajia actually purchased from it.
_Avoid_: Source, supplier

**Citation**:
The relationship between a Research Source and the exact Wisdom entry fields it supports.
_Avoid_: Reference when the supporting source is meant

**Wisdom Entry**:
A shared, non-account-scoped record for a region, cultivar, producer, style, mark, or named tea.
_Avoid_: Product, listing

**Sourced**:
Supported by usable citations and permitted for reference use, but not personally confirmed by Adrian.
_Avoid_: Verified, questionable

**Verified**:
An internal state meaning Adrian reviewed the complete current Wisdom Entry and agrees with it. Verification expires when the entry or its citations change.
_Avoid_: Approved, published

**Held Back**:
Insufficiently supported or contradictory material that cannot be used as public reference knowledge.
_Avoid_: Draft when the problem is evidence quality

**Potential Profile**:
Qualified sensory character derived from cited shared knowledge. It is not a claim that Adrian tasted a particular tea.
_Avoid_: Tasting notes, Adrian's tasting

**Tea Lot**:
The exact harvest, batch, cake, or other physical tea acquired or considered by an operator.
_Avoid_: Tea when the specific physical record is meant

**Vendor**:
The person or company from whom an operator actually acquires or considers acquiring a Tea Lot.
_Avoid_: Research Source, producer

**Producer**:
The person, family, cooperative, brand, house, or factory that made a Tea Lot, when known.
_Avoid_: Vendor, Research Source

**Personal Observation**:
An operator's direct tasting or sourcing observation about a specific Tea Lot.
_Avoid_: Potential Profile, common profile
