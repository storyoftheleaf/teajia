# Teajia

Teajia is a home for fine tea and the people who source it, study it and share it around a table. It grew out of Adrian’s twenty years of sourcing relationships. A tea can stay in a private collection, become something a shop offers, appear at a sit, or lead a reader to a grower’s story.

Follow one tea. Adrian can keep the supplier’s words, photographs and quote together before buying it. The person who receives the tea counts what arrived. A shop can offer part of that holding for sale. A host can name the tea they served, a guest can find it again, and an editor can connect it to a published edition. At each step, the person who owns the stock, the words or the photograph chooses what happens to it.

## The four words a visitor is given

| Door | What someone comes to do |
|---|---|
| **Read** | Open a story, learn a practice in Lessons, or look up a tea, mark, producer or place in Wisdom. Teajia has one Wisdom Base. A published tea can draw on the same knowledge as a reader studying it. |
| **Tea table** | Revisit what people have chosen to share after a sit. Tastings and recaps are ordered by time. A host or Adrian can hold a piece at the top by hand. A contributor can withdraw their piece. |
| **Tea spaces** | Discover where people sit, learn about Adrian’s work with tea houses, or arrange a visit. One house can hold several spaces. A person can host in a space without operating a shop. |
| **Shop** | Explore teas, teaware and collections, open a tea’s own page, and order from the person or shop offering it. An invoice, invitation or collection can lead back to the same tea with its original context. |

**Your Table** is the person’s private side of the site. **Manage** is where authorized operators and staff work for a selected shop. Each shop keeps its own holdings, prices, buyers and invoices. A public profile, a place to sit and permission to sell are separate choices.

## From a supplier to the shelf

Curate’s supplier-grouped Library holds the original name, quote, unit, currency and source evidence. Adrian can give the tea a working name, taste a sample or connect an AR Create transcript. Private notes, recordings and source photographs stay with the record. An operator selects the description and image that may appear in Shop.

Ordering creates an expected purchase. A parcel can reach a logistics company, travel onward and arrive in parts. Only a physical count adds the received quantity to the studio shelf. If 100 g of a 250 g order arrives, Stock gains 100 g and Purchases still expects 150 g. The operator and an authorized assistant act on the same purchase. ## How a purchase happens

The seller decides which counted holding and pack to offer. A personal seller can offer part of a private holding and receive their own proceeds. Public display and permission to order are separate decisions. Each price carries its unit and currency. The shop applies its markup to the leaf and passes inbound freight through at cost. Customer postage is a separate quote. [Money rules](claude/money.md)

The buyer’s request and invoice give the conversation a durable home. A reviewed order has a payment deadline. A split keeps the original deadline and records payments and refunds against the correct invoice. The buyer can use the eligible payment method, speak with the seller on WhatsApp, and return to the private order. Confirmed money, stock movement, shipping and delivery each record a different event. A blank shipping quote means the price is unknown. An explicit zero can mean pickup or free delivery. Saving a shared invoice grants access to that invoice alone. [Payment access](claude/pay-and-sign-in.md) · [Shipping notices](SHIPPING_PAYMENT_NOTIFICATIONS.md)

## Around the table

The host can invite guests beforehand and record the teas afterward. During an ordinary sit the app asks nothing of the people at the table. A photo or quick pour is optional. A host may choose a structured tasting when writing is part of the practice.

A casual sit can travel by one link. A scheduled private sit keeps its approval rule for directions and attendee material. A host may name a tea brought by someone else. Only the holder or someone authorized by the owning shop may move its grams. If two people bring 100 g each and one accounts for a 5 g serving, their holdings should read 95 g and 100 g. A reversal belongs to the original holding. [Roles and access](MEMBERS_AND_ACCESS_GUIDE.md)

## What a signed-in person gets

A guest can follow the teas from their thank-you, keep one, write privately, or choose a contribution to the Guest Book. The Journal holds what a person tasted and thought. Tea Chest holds what they own. Journey remembers where they sat. Favourites gathers what they kept. Signing in from a shared link should return them to the tea, invitation or edition they opened. Joining asks once whether Teajia and the hosts they choose may email them about teas and tables; the box opens empty for EU, EEA, UK and Swiss visitors. Order emails always come. The answer, with its time, lives in their account settings, host by host. [Email answers](../worker/src/emailConsent.ts)

Private directions, guest contacts, tasting scores and unpublished words each have a limited audience. Publishing a recap, naming a contributor or placing a tasting excerpt on a tea page takes its own permission. A guest can withdraw a shared contribution and keep the private original. A host’s record remains theirs after they stop hosting. A thank-you, a byline or a photo credit acknowledges a person without ranking them or prompting more activity.

## From a tea to a published edition

A contributor or editor can gather working material privately, agree on a byline and image rights, and submit a particular revision for review. Readers see the edition the editor approved. Work on the next revision stays private until the editor chooses to replace it. Publication and withdrawal apply wherever that edition is presented. Licensed study material retains its own access rules. [Contributor editing](CONTRIBUTOR_EDITOR.md) · [Tea Wisdom Base](TEA_WISDOM_BASE.md) · [Tea Atlas](TEA_ATLAS.md)
