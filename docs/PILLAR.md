# Teajia — the pillar

> What this site is and how it works, in one read. Written 2026-09-20 from a full survey of the code (four agents, every public and signed-in route, the worker API, the git history of the home page). Linked from the top of `CLAUDE.md` so every session sees it. When the site changes shape, change this file in the same commit; a pillar that lags the code is worse than none.

## What it is, in three sentences

Teajia is a home for fine tea: a place to source it, study it, and share it with the people who gather around the cup. It is one person's twenty years of sourcing relationships, turned into a shop, a magazine, a reference library, a calendar of sessions, and a set of professional tools that other tea people can run their own practice on. It is not a wellness app, not a social network, not a SaaS dashboard, and it never sits at the tea table.

## How a purchase happens

The flagship checkout ships to the United States, with Bali pickup available only when explicitly selected. A visitor supplies a name, reply contact and full US delivery address. When live PayPal is configured and stock and prices are known, checkout reserves the tea and offers immediate payment; orders needing review remain drafts until Adrian confirms them. The payment window is 24 hours from confirmation. Opening WhatsApp or pressing Send in another app is not required. Shipping is quoted and charged separately after the tea payment. In Sales, opening the saved order brings shipping and payment management together: enter the delivery charge, destination and tracking beside the payment ledger, with the balance refreshed after saving. A blank shipping quote is refused; an entered zero means pickup or free delivery. Shipping charges lock after dispatch, while tracking can still be corrected. Payment and shipment are separate: confirmed payment can allocate the purchased stock while the order still awaits dispatch, and adding shipping later must never deduct that stock twice. The visitor follows the request at `/order/:ref`. A customer can explicitly opt into a WhatsApp order confirmation from the shop’s existing business number. It is sent to that customer, with their private order-status link; Coexistence can mirror the outgoing conversation into the owner’s WhatsApp Business app. This is not an incoming alert to the owner. Automatic delivery stays disabled until the same-number provider connection and approved template are ready. Orders without consent still save normally. See [WhatsApp order setup](WHATSAPP_ORDER_SETUP.md) for configuration and delivery-state limits. Every order is a conversation. The shop copy should say so plainly, never apologise for it.

A website order can have several invoices after a Sales split. Each invoice keeps its own lines, payments, refunds and dispatch, while the private order link shows them together. Splitting or revising an accepted order preserves its original 24-hour payment deadline. Earlier payments stay on the invoice that received them. Pickup is in Bali. Delivery orders pay for tea first; shipping, including US, is quoted and charged separately later. US shipping remains quote-only rather than applying a preset at checkout. An unknown shipping price is not free shipping: it blocks dispatch until the operator enters the actual charge (zero included) and the remaining balance is paid. Bank transfers are confirmed by the operator in Sales. A [local-only PayPal sandbox](PAYPAL_SANDBOX.md) can verify a test capture and automatically confirm the disposable invoice ledger and stock once. It cannot activate on a deployed origin. The separate live PayPal integration uses production credentials and verified payment notifications, confirms captured money automatically, and keeps sandbox receipts separate. Customers pay the tea first and the later shipping balance through the same private order page. With shipping payment notifications enabled, saving a new positive quote queues the private order link through consented WhatsApp, otherwise email. Orders shows whether it is queued, sent or blocked. Unchanged quotes do not resend, and unknown recipients or unavailable private links remain explicitly unsent. Email can operate while WhatsApp approval is pending; the server automatically enables WhatsApp shipping notices only after fresh provider evidence confirms the dedicated utility template. No outbound US per-kilo rate is assumed.

**Agent-led purchases and arrivals (2026-10-28, no new migration).** An invoice finalized through MCP creates connected purchases and expected receipts by supplier; confirming a Curate order creates the same expected-delivery records. Recording physical arrivals through the agent or browser uses one guarded operation for stock, ledger, quantities and purchase state, including partial deliveries and safe retries. Purchases combines incoming tracking with compact supplier rows, Coming, Received and Needs attention filters. Opening a purchase reveals item quantities, Stock links, original invoice and arrival history. Manual receiving stays under Make a change. Partial delivery is normal; unfinished invoices, unresolved matches, older unlinked records and overdue deliveries remain visible for attention. Saved invoice evidence opens read-only in place; unfinished invoices retain a separate Review invoice action. The Stock Incoming entry opens this same tracking view. Invoice entry never implies that goods have arrived or that a private holding should be published.

**Supplier → logistics → studio (migrations `0052`–`0054`).** Purchases tracks each supplier's inbound courier and tracking number separately from a combined air or boat shipment to the studio. Each logistics company groups supplier parcels beside its onward shipments on wide screens, and in that same order on phones. Arrival checks and shipment preparation act within that company and the selected supplier; shipment contents are visible with a direct link back to the purchase. Parcel contents and paid costs, warehouse holdings, company rates, and outbound destination, ETA and freight calculations stay visible. Services, packing and corrections use focused task screens; there are no accordions. Chinese-and-English message drafts can be edited, copied or saved for the logistics company. Suggested packing volumes and freight estimates use the same calculation in the overview and message, without changing a confirmed bill. Pasted company messages are entered by agents into the matching purchase or shipment, retaining their source, reported time and history. Saved warehouse and destination addresses are reused explicitly and copied onto each route so later address-book edits cannot reroute an existing delivery (migrations `0053` and `0054`). This is message-based tracking, not a continuous courier feed. Goods arriving at logistics, being grouped, or being dispatched never increase shelf stock; only explicit verified studio quantities do. Logistics services keep reusable company rates in their quoted currency, per kg, per m³ or per shipment. A shipment retains its selected quote while its final bill and packing measurements can be corrected. Unknown costs remain unknown; planning volumes and freight shares based on them are labelled estimates. CSV and printable photo lists retain supplier costs, item quantities, reference links and inbound/outbound tracking. The browser also supports grouped hub-arrival checks, Mark selected ready, outbound tracking and direct Receive all at studio with editable quantities; these actions never wait for agent review. Actual shipment freight is accounting information, separate from the shop's existing retail freight policy. See [Purchases and logistics](PURCHASE_LOGISTICS.md) for the agent workflow and limits. Deployment proof is recorded by the coordinated release runner.

**Curate → Stock → sale (implemented locally, 2026-10-28).** A capture keeps its quoted amount, currency and quantity basis together. Its photo opens a reversible frame with square, portrait (2:3) and landscape (3:2) choices plus fine rotation; the original stays intact, and Delete sits beneath the thumbnail. New thumbnails default to square crops; chosen portrait or landscape thumbnails keep the same height and adjust their width. Source has one photo area, with documents and history available from Library. Buy saves the capture before proposing a receipt; Library resumes that same purchase. Stock receipt is an explicit, repeat-safe acknowledgement of physical possession, and publication is a separate choice. Intake keeps incomplete rows and purchase follow-ups until confirmed. Network Carry and wholesale receiving connect their listings to the same local Stock holding. Tea moves in grams, teaware in pieces; neither a missing cost nor a missing exchange rate becomes a zero price. Confirmed payment consumes stock once. Cancelling an unshipped paid order restores it once, reverses the sale settlement and leaves a visible refund due; recording an actual refund clears the due without erasing payments. Shipped orders cannot be cancelled through this path. Customer history follows the authenticated buyer across shops, with a private tracking-link claim for guest orders. The agreed invoice, original request and current refund state remain distinct on tracking. Migration `0031` and deployment are required before these local repairs are live.

## What a signed-in person gets

**One sign-in, everywhere.** `/signin` handles Google, password and email code for the public site, Your Table, event reservations and Manage. Entry points carry only a validated `returnTo`; without one, sign-in opens Your Table at `/account`. The account panel and RSVP sheet contain no separate login form. `/signup` owns account creation and email verification and keeps the same return destination. Google always returns through `/signin`; an event return reopens the unfinished reservation without submitting it, and admin returns retain filters, anchors and pending consent requests.

**Your Table** (the person glyph at the right end of the mobile bar, the same glyph in the rail's foot on desktop) is the person's own room, and since 2026-09-22 only that. A visitor sees Discover, Read, Shop, Find a Table. A member sees a day line (for someone who runs a shop it names what is waiting, with one line that opens Today in Manage, where the list lives), and tiles that are all theirs: journal (tasting journal), sessions, remember (favourites, and collections shared with them), cellar (tea they own, with grams, placeable at a location), discover, orders (what they bought, and samples sent to them), journey, profile (name, sign-in, appearance, and the public Tea Master identity for those who have one), and switch when they sit at more than one table. Since 2026-09-29 each of these things has one tile: samples, shared collections and account folded into orders, remember and profile, and the platform owner's walk-throughs into library. Anyone with a Manage room gets exactly one more tile, "manage", the door to the shop; it appears only once the token is confirmed for the active table. Nothing else from Manage is named here, so the shop's rooms have one name each, in the Manage column and the phone's site panel, both read from one list.

The personal records are deliberately separate and stay that way: **Journal** is what you tasted and thought, **Favourites** is what you want, **Cellar** is what you own, **Journey** is where you have been (sessions, teas met, seals). No streaks, no scores, no feed.

The standalone page for tea and teaware uses one [approved reading order](design/catalog-listing-hierarchy.md). The photos and account actions lead into a short introduction. An eligible lot tasting and its journal excerpt precede provenance, order and the historical reading. For an object, the opening introduces the piece and the compact facts prioritize form, capacity and condition. Cited style-level flavor context closes the tea reading. The Stock photo editor opens a private gallery proposal for the exact Stock item. The owner stages ordered JPEGs and reviews their main and additional views. The current listing and its gallery are changed only through their own publishing controls.

A member records their experience of a tea in the private tasting journal. The prompt responds to their verdict, and they can move between saved tastings from the editor. The post-event form uses the same experience field and lets guests move between the event’s teas. They can offer an exact excerpt with a public byline. A curator approves it and chooses the tea listing, the home page, or both. The listing shows the excerpt once, beneath the bottom border of the tasting descriptors box and before About. When the listing has no descriptors, the excerpt still appears before About. Withdrawal removes it from both public placements and preserves the private tasting. Member photos also start private. Only published listings can surface shared contributions to visitors.

A pot’s clay reading follows its introduction. Stock supplies the public material, while the owner preview distinguishes tentative clay families from confirmed decisions and links back to clay review.

The owner can open **Tea write-ups** from Drafts to read each intake introduction with its reviewed photos. Package-only images carry a private reference label. Verified Stock matches link to their records.

## How it is built

- **Accounts.** Every account is its own store: catalogue, stock, prices, currency, customers, invoices. Kinds are platform (Adrian), location (a tea room), master (a tea master with no address). One database, every table scoped by account id, one gatekeeper that resolves the acting account on every request.
- **Curate to stock.** A sourcing sample marked in Curate, in the app or through an agent, has one linked shelf portion in its vendor's open sourcing batch. The server owns sample lifecycle synchronization; a tasting never invents stock or a purchase decision. Reviewed supplier arrivals create private drafts with the tea's details and the exact order line's batch cost. Agents can also promote a Curate tea into a private, zero-stock inventory Draft before arrival, retaining its existing identity and samples. Vendor quotes alone never become batch costs. Recorded freight remains separate from the shop's freight setting.
- **Source details.** The Source header has one Context action for the capture group and optional sourcing trip/visit. Tea details opens a focused editor for tea-specific facts; supplier identity comes from the shared supplier record, shipping from the exact linked orders and receipts, and freight defaults from Store settings. These are not re-entered as free-text fields on every tea. Chosen tea names use the same naming control in Source and Library; historical chosen names remain available. Pack weight stays distinct from the weight covered by a supplier quote.
- **Curate workspace.** Supplier facts have typed tea, vendor and quote fields; private role-labelled files attach to those records and arrivals. Stock's Samples only filter shows received/tasted Curate portions without full stock, opens their editor and reviews tasting deductions or orders. Authorized shop managers and agents share account-wide saved records, with actor history and guarded undo of a selected change. Unfinished local captures and unsent local writes are kept per signed-in person and account. Older browser-only records with no trustworthy author remain in restricted local recovery storage; they are neither assigned to the next sign-in nor uploaded automatically. Photo unlinking preserves backups; agents can separately preview Drive trash or restore. [Operating details](CURATE_WORKSPACE.md).
- **Network.** A tea profile has an originator and a curator. A store carries a network tea by making its own listing over it. Cross-store movement routes through the platform account.
- **Money rules** (in full in `CLAUDE.md`): freight is a per-kilo rate on the account, inside the ×3 markup; one exchange-rate table refreshed daily; nothing entered is NULL and zero is zero; a tea is not created without its cost and its currency.
- **Events** move through draft, published, registration closed, completed and archived. A structured tasting can stand alone or retain its originating RSVP event. Group results show ratings and purchase interest, while personal prose and sensory notes are visible to their author. After an ordinary session, a verified attendee can save a private reflection in their session archive. Hosts deliberately share a separate text recap with confirmed guests marked attended. An existing public recap keeps its publication contract. A completed session can also become a magazine draft.
- **Received tea cards** keep a durable receipt per member, account and source. Save card reopens that exact received copy from Your Table → remember. An invitation saves a reference, and the member explicitly records tasting, favorites or physical ownership through their existing actions. Historical purchases and ambiguous old copies are preserved when an original invitation is saved again.
- **Agents.** Roughly seventy authenticated MCP tools (stock, sales, customers, intake, events, collections, articles) with preview-then-confirm on every write, plus four public read-only tools for the shopping public. `.mcp.json` connects a Claude session; the token lives in Infisical.
- **Stack.** React 19, Vite, Tailwind with CSS-variable tokens, Zustand for client state, React Query for server state, Cloudflare Pages for the site, a Cloudflare Worker on D1 for the API. Live at teajia.com (Pages project `teajiafinal`); the API at api.teajia.com.

## The boundaries, so nobody rebuilds them

No streaks, gamification, engagement notifications, algorithmic recommendations, social feeds, likes, followers, auto-replenish. Ordinary tea sessions offer notes after the event. Hosts can opt into a structured tasting under Sessions and Tastings, where guests rate each tea and the host sees live results for that tasting. Live PayPal activation requires sandbox acceptance and explicit owner authorization; both were provided on 19 November 2026. Curation is by hand. Journal, Favourites, Cellar, Curate stay separate. Nav labels and routes change only with explicit confirmation.
