## 2026-09

- 2026-09-01 — **`set_tasting` added to the agent/voice server.** The MCP had no path to a tea's structured tasting: `update_tea_pricing` carries `tasting_notes`, which is the legacy free-text tag array, so an agent could set loose taste words but never the taste, feel, body, finish and liquor colour the product page actually reads, and never the `tasting_source` that distinguishes the shop's own claim from a cited profile. The new owner-tier `catalog:write` tool takes taxonomy term ids per category through the same two-step preview/confirm every mutating tool uses, and the preview reads back written labels rather than ids so the person confirming can see the claim in words. It merges rather than overwrites, because that one column also holds the starred notes the page quotes, the teaser line and the brewing terms; only supplied categories change and an empty array clears one. Unknown ids are refused by name instead of dropped, since a dropped term would publish a shorter claim in silence, and a stored tasting that will not parse is refused rather than merged onto nothing. Validation and the merge extend `curateImportTasting.ts` so the taxonomy has one home in the worker. Verified: 8 new merge tests, the worker suite at 1164 passing with one pre-existing parallel-run race in `curate-imports.test.ts` that passes 170/170 on its own, plus TypeScript and colour lint.

- 2026-09-01 — **Product page interior redesigned around reading.** Where a tea is from left the facts ledger and now floats under the label as one block: the place in the display serif at reading size, harvest, elevation, forest and tree character following on a single tracked line. What the tea is sits in one keylined record card set into the page on a new `tea-sunken` surface, with taste on its own band, feel beneath a hairline at a smaller size in a quieter ink, and any starred note as the third band; the TASTE and FEEL labels are gone, because a 9px caps label inline in front of a 21px serif line collapsed into a ragged hanging indent whenever the terms wrapped on a phone. Your own tasting left that card and stands on its own directly above the reading, in its own keyline, closed by default and opening onto your note and the date, so the record states the shop's claim about the tea and the row states yours. Chapter labels became the centred rule-and-word mark at 12px rather than a 10px whisper, prose moved from 14px secondary to 16.5px at 1.82, and an author's own `## ` line inside the prose now sets as a display-serif heading. The quiet modal card and teaware are untouched; both keep the ledger and the inline plate. Verified locally: TypeScript and colour lint pass, and 18 shop, people and smoke browser checks pass against a dev server confirmed to be serving this worktree, with the product page rendered at 390 and 1440 with no horizontal overflow and no console errors.
