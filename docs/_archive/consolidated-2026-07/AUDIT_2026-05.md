## D. Intentional — do NOT "fix" these

Recorded so a well-meaning pass doesn't undo them. Per `OPERATIONAL_NOTES.md` and `VISION.md`.

- **WhatsApp checkout** — every order is a personal conversation. This is the model, not a gap. (The friction review *does* recommend adding a customer-facing order *confirmation* — that's a missing feedback state, not a checkout replacement.)
- **No on-site checkout / payment** — purchase exits to WhatsApp by design.
- **Human curation over algorithms** — at 139 products, Adrian's voice is the recommendation engine. No algorithmic recommendations.
- **The "DO NOT build" list** — streaks, gamification, engagement notifications, social feeds, auto-replenish.
