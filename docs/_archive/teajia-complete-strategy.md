# Part 1: Foundation

## Brand Identity

**Name:** Teajiā

**Tagline:** "A Living Space for Tea Culture"

**Core Promise:** Stories, wisdom, and community — wherever you are on the path

**Philosophy:** Tea as a way of being, not a commodity. Ancient wisdom meets modern accessibility.

**Tone:** Elevated but welcoming. Not pretentious. Grounded depth.

---

## Audience

| Audience | What They Seek |
|----------|----------------|
| Newcomers | Guidance entering tea |
| Experienced practitioners | Depth, community, connection |
| Collectors | Rare tea, antiques, quality |
| Space creators | Complete tea house design and curation |
| Businesses/Organizations | Tea integration, sourcing, events |
| Transformational travelers | Access to tea origins with a guide |

---

## Business Model

**Primary Revenue:**
- Tea House Design & Curation (highest value)
- Shop (The Collection + For Your Practice)
- Tea Sourcing (B2B and collectors)

**Secondary Revenue:**
- Sessions & Guidance
- Sourcing Journeys (on request)

**Audience Building (leads to revenue):**
- Magazine (Read)
- Learn (courses)
- Library (resources)

---

## Connection to Art Site

Teajiā and your Art site are two wings of your practice. They acknowledge and complement each other without confusing visitors.

**On Teajiā:**
- Link in Shop: "More from Adrian → Explore the full studio"
- Link in About: "Adrian is also a visual artist..."
- Link in Footer

**On Art Site:**
- Link to Teajiā: "Adrian also runs Teajiā — a living space for tea culture"

**Shared Products (appear on both, different framing):**
- Tea Tables
- Art (tea-relevant)
- Incense + Incense Boxes
- Oracle Cards (tea ceremony deck)

**Teajiā Only:**
- Tea, Teaware, Antiques, Sets

**Art Site Only:**
- Visual art (non-tea), Jewelry, Other oracle decks

# Part 4: Read (Magazine)

## Purpose

- Front door to Teajiā
- Community service — shares wisdom of tea world
- Audience acquisition — featured guests bring their audiences
- Authority building — positions you as curator and hub

---

## Editorial Approach

- Every piece carries your editor's voice
- AI structures interviews from real conversations
- ~60% guest features / ~40% your own pieces
- Each piece delivers tangible value — readers gain something

---

## Article Format

- Swipeable 20+ page mobile-first design
- Images and video integrated throughout
- Designed to be held and read like a physical magazine

---

## Read Tracking

**Hybrid Model:**
- Device-based by default (no friction)
- Account-based when logged in (syncs everywhere)
- Gentle prompts to create account after reading 3+ pieces

---

## Publishing Cadence

- At least one article per month
- Can release multiple when ready
- Monthly email announces what's new

- Pieces can carry multiple tags
- No new tag unless it applies to at least 3 pieces
- Tags power cross-linking and "Continue Reading" suggestions
