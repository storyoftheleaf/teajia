## Handoffs as they exist

1. **Vault Magazine workflow.** The author’s established sequence is Ideas → Source → Guidance → Develop → Review → publication → Subject feedback. The private archive has the handbook, quote checker, map template, editorial and blind-read briefs, and preview tools. Source grades constrain what can be written. The editorial team should reuse the existing quote, attribution, title, approval and feedback process. [Private handbook and tooling](https://github.com/technicianofthesacred/teajia-magazine)
2. **AR Create.** Library originals own their source identity and revision. An Article Piece owns its manuscript and exact audience edition independently of related carousels or other outputs. The project plan calls for delivery of reviewed tea articles and educational material to Teajia. Evidence of existing local Article composition, edition HTML export and source ownership does not establish an installed, revision-pinned send-to-Teajia action. [AR Create product](../../../ar-create/docs/PRODUCT.md) · [File and identity contract](../../../ar-create/docs/FILE-CONTRACT-MAP.md)
3. **i64 OS Wordforge.** The existing Wordforge sender has compile, sync and receipts in its article routes. Its draft port calls Teajia’s signed, account-bound Wordforge integration. This is a concrete donor to evaluate before adding another editorial receiver. [Wordforge routes](../../../i64os/apps/core/routes/wordforge.ts) · [Draft transport](../../../i64os/apps/core/lib/wordforge/teajia-sync.ts) · [Teajia receiver tests](../../worker/tests/wordforge-article-draft.test.ts)
4. **AR Create → Build this tea.** Teajia’s separate paired connector attaches a reviewed transcript or passage to an existing Curate tea. It can feed reviewed facts and description proposals. The selected tea retains identity and the original remains in AR Create. This private sourcing handoff ends in a tea record. [Tea link contract](../../docs/AR_CREATE_TEA_LINK_CONTRACT.md)
5. **Teajia editorial authority.** Placement, public byline, public media, SEO, and the publication/unpublication action belong to the Teajia editor. The existing curated issue and D1 draft deliveries have separate technical paths. A private source package should enter the chosen draft path with a durable receipt.

## Smallest coherent editorial delivery

- **Choose one real keeper.** Use one already-reviewed story, with an identified edition, accepted author words, verified claims, a quote-check record and licensed or owned public media. Run fixtures first.
- **Hand it across by revision.** From the existing owner select one Article edition and its approved media, title, contributor/subject identities, citation or evidence locators, layout intent and target issue. Keep source family, stable output key, exact revision, public URL and issue slot distinct. Retries return the same destination. Validate contributor and media scope and advance revisions atomically. Conflicting edits surface the two revisions and keep editor work. Only selected public material travels with the draft.
- **Review it in Teajia.** Open the returned draft, match on-page rendering and exact edits to the chosen format, and verify private data stays private. Add a public issue placement deliberately. Independently record author/source approval and Teajia editorial approval.
- **Publish and withdraw.** Bind approval to the exact reviewed revision across REST, MCP and issue placement. Verify the story from the issue entry point, direct URL, public attribution and social metadata. A new upstream draft preserves the already approved public edition while waiting for review. Test withdrawal separately for the issue index, route, API, governed modules, social metadata and media URLs. Retain shared media according to its own rights and dependency decision. Verify the candidate and affected older hosts. [Independent review](review.md)
- **Close the loop.** Record durable destination, public version and handoff outcome with the source owner. Stage subject feedback for Adrian after publication, with the approved text and intended recipient. A reply becomes a reported correction to review against the published revision.

Before choosing an owner for the new issue register, decide whether the existing private issue remains the editorial proposal and Teajia owns publication placement or whether a shared record can preserve both roles. Keep private planning fields out of public issue delivery. The unnamed reader-facing content of a protected archive should never become issue metadata.
