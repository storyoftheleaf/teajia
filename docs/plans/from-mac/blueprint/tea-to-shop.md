# A tea in hand to its Shop listing

**17 November 2026.** Start in the intended Teajia account. Use [the description playbook](tea-description.md) at step 8. The route and button names below come from the normal Source, Library, Purchases and Stock surfaces. The [controller's delivery register](DELIVERY.md) owns implementation and decisions. This walkthrough covers both starting records, the invoice alternative and the physical handoffs. Sandbox proof and live operation are separate gates.

| What you have | Phone start | What to keep linked |
|---|---|---|
| A new sample or a new tea | Open **Manage → Curate → Source** at `/admin/compass?tab=sourcing&capture=tea`, or **Library → Add to Library → New tea**. Make one encounter. | Shop account, encounter, supplier, quote, photos, tasting and any distinct sample portion. |
| A sample already in the system | **Manage → Stock → Show teas → Samples**. Find the supplier/tea and tap **Taste**. Select **Log a tasting** if recording sample consumption. | Original Curate encounter and exact sample portion. Start subsequent acquisition from the same tea, for example **Order this tea → Review order → Confirm order**. |
| A tea already in Stock or Library | **Stock → search name/supplier → Taste** for a personal tasting, or **Curate → Library → tea → Taste** for the Curate profile. Use the linked record for capture and writing. | Retain the existing product and encounter IDs. Personal Journal, shop profile and sample use are separate records. A retaste must preserve the shop profile unless deliberately changed. |
| A photo, supplier list or invoice | **Curate → Library → Add to Library → Import files**. Paste text or attach the original. Review and confirm the destination-specific action, such as **Hold 1 tea in transit** or **Save 1 Library record**. Use existing Stock intake at `/admin/intake` only where that intake route is the appropriate starting record. | Original import and vendor group, existing match or newly saved tea, purchase and receipt IDs where applicable. One invoice must create one purchase for its intended vendor group. |

## The phone walkthrough

1. **Identify and photograph.** Search before creating a new encounter. Capture the supplier's name for the tea and the shop's name for it separately. Select the supplier or store that supplied it and check the active Teajia account. Set the known type and harvest year only when you know them. Tap **Photo**, take or choose an image, and **Use this photo** when the capture review appears. Reopen the same Library row, check thumbnail and count, and use **Add another photo** in the viewer if you want the label, dry leaf or brewed liquor. Record company contact cards and original supplier images as private evidence. Select separate rights-cleared customer photos before listing.
2. **Say what you know, then taste.** Use the **Notes** microphone to record the supplier's shop, what they quoted, and your own observations with their speakers kept clear. Read back the transcript and correct names and numbers. If the audio saved but transcription failed, retry the existing recording and check it yields one note on that encounter. Enter the quote in the price control with its actual currency and basis, such as **CNY 200 per 500 g**, not the display currency. Tap **Taste**, note the brewing conditions and your own taste, then save. On a received sample enter any grams actually used through **Log a tasting** when you are recording consumption. Weigh it if needed. Leave unknown weight or cost unresolved and retain a real 0 when the item or freight was free.
3. **Check display and merge only confirmed matches.** In **Library**, reopen from supplier grouping, search or the reviewed import. Check name precedence, type/year, amount/currency/per-quantity basis, tasting, Notes, thumbnail/counter and the right holding link. If the import found a possible match, confirm it using supplier, item/packing identity, intended purchase and stock history. A name match is a clue. In invoice review, verify every included line, its cost and currency, pack weight where needed, vendor and whether the line is a reference, sample, ordered or physically received. Read the preview of saved records and move only evidence-backed items. Continue the existing batch on retry. If stock was recorded by the older intake, open **Purchases → Link recorded stock** and link the original movement. Match an unresolved purchase in **Match purchase items** before any arrival. A dated shipment or invoice can establish what was ordered or where it went. A physical count establishes on-hand stock.
4. **Place the supplier order.** From the tea, use **Buy**. Review or enter quantity plus **Inventory purpose** and **Acquisition**. For an ordinary purchase, choose **Working** and **Purchase**, then **Add to Ledger**. Open Curate **Orders** at `/admin/compass?tab=orders`, check supplier, line amount/weight, quote and total, and use **Purchase** on the draft to record it. The **Place order** basket is another existing route where available. Use **Mark as Sent** after the supplier order really went out. Saving a request, sharing a PDF and marking sent have different meanings. Do not add another order for an invoice already linked to this one. Confirm the order opens in **Purchases** with a server order ID, expected receipt line, linked private Stock draft and unchanged on-hand stock. Keep the same confirmation on an interrupted response. An already received non-purchase sample uses its distinct receipt proposal and **Accept into Inventory** after weighing. Do not route a future supplier parcel through that shortcut.
5. **Assign the logistics leg.** Open **Manage → Curate → Purchases** at `/admin/purchase-orders`. Find the same order and open its purchase task, then **Changes**. Choose the saved **Logistics company** where available, the verified **Warehouse address**, **Inbound courier** and **Inbound tracking**, then **Save route**. Record the supplier leg and keep original supplier invoice cost. Use **Mark at logistics** for a confirmed warehouse receipt, or **Check arrivals at logistics** for the grouped workflow. The warehouse is custody, with its own timestamps and locations. Stock still has the previous on-hand quantity.
6. **Group the shipment, record freight, dispatch.** At the forwarder, choose **Combine purchases** (or **Prepare shipment** where the current view uses it). The **Send together** sheet supports **Company**, **By** (**Air** or **Boat**), saved **Service**, **Deliver to** and editable line quantities. Choose the confirmed service and quantities and **Mark selected ready**. On the shipment enter **Outbound courier**, **Outbound tracking**, **Expected at studio** and freight under **Changes → Shipment freight → Save shipment**. Retain the quoted service, the actual charging basis (**kg**, **m³** or fixed), measured billable size, actual total and currency; use **Bill confirmed** only for the real bill. The final bill overrides a quote; a blank remains unknown and 0 means free. Estimated sizes remain estimates. Check allocation shares sum to the bill. Keep supplier cost and freight by currency. Actual logistics charges do not silently replace the retail freight setting. **Boat** selects sea transport. The form stores no dedicated vessel or voyage value. Save the confirmed vessel and voyage verbatim in the existing shipment tracking source report, alongside official outbound tracking and the ETA, pending a structured vessel decision. Do not put vessel name in the tracking number. Use **Mark sent to studio** after dispatch, confirm any outbound tracking demanded by the form, and verify the state moved to **To studio** while stock stayed unchanged.
7. **Count at the studio, then confirm.** For forwarded goods, open the exact shipment under **To studio → Receive**, whose accessible control is **Record studio arrival**. In **Receive at studio**, enter only physically checked quantities per line. Use **Receive all** only after verifying every outstanding line. The person who counted may confirm a routine receipt if they have Stock access. Route uncertainty about a match or a quantity discrepancy to Adrian in **Needs attention** before writing the affected line. **Confirm received** creates the movement. After an interrupted save use **Retry arrival → Retry save** for the same quantities. A direct order uses **Record arrival / manage delivery → Receive** through its receipt task. Forwarded purchases use the shipment arrival action. Check actual quantity, remaining amount, movement ID, actor and same Stock holding after reload. Cancel an agreed outstanding remainder explicitly; handle an already accepted receipt correction as a deliberate stock movement. Keep the listing private at this point.
8. **Review the product with Adrian.** Open the linked **Stock** holding. Check name, supplier, tea type, stated acquisition cost and currency, on-hand quantity, incoming remainder, holding purpose, selling price/unit and catalog rights to the images. Open the linked tea details in **Curate → Library**, then **Build this tea** and follow [the description playbook](tea-description.md). Review the exact wording and claimed facts, and keep Adrian's tasting observations in the tasting/profile fields. Check the public copy, approved photo and intended price on the actual product record before considering publication. Saving an accepted description updates the Curate record. Compare the existing product's **Introduction** and apply the approved copy there if it did not carry through. A source review save and the public listing switch are different events; editing an already visible product can change live content, so inspect current visibility before writing.
9. **Approve the listing and read it as a customer.** Keep the product in the intended private draft until Adrian approves the product copy, photos and listing. Verify the existing first-publication conditions (name, type, valid selling price and counted stock) and that sample or transit stock is not mistaken for shop stock. Confirm the intended existing Stock/Catalog and visibility permissions, evaluate the phone listing control available to that role (the expanded Stock control is **List in shop**), and verify release-authorized publication for that exact product. Open a new signed-out browser context at the right storefront and `/shop/product/:productId` (include the shop selector if required by that storefront). Check the same public ID, correct title/type, hero and gallery, **About This Tea**, tasting profile where approved, sale unit, price/currency and quantity state. Try browse and direct link. Private notes, raw sources, costs, supplier contact details and pending quantities should be absent. Later sellout must keep its approved direct page while disabling buying and removing it from default browsing.
