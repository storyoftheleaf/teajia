# Read, Magazine and editorial production

The public Reading Room, the private issue archive, the owner’s story workshop and the D1 Magazine room serve different steps in one editorial workflow. The proposed connection gives an approved edition one public home and a deliberate place in Read. The existing hand-built readers retain their interaction, address and publication controls.

## The Reading Room as delivered

## Consolidation decisions and work order

1. **Use one public home and one editorial work queue.** Keep Read navigation and routes. The Magazine room can list current curated and D1 references under their existing permissions, with an item opening its appropriate editor. Use explicit issue placement, order, optional feature and reader destination. Show published state separately from placement. The first approved public edition should be accessible from Read and by its own canonical URL. Linking curated pieces to a placement does not require migrating their source or renderer.
2. **Write down the issue decision at placement.** Record source owner/ID and edition, credited authors and subjects, reviewed format and media, approved public relations, theme/group, feature position, publish time, public URL and observed receipt. Keep primary review actions visible and give image, attribution and preview focused screens on phones. The public issue lists only placed and live items. Draft, scheduled/held, approved, live and withdrawn states each need clear action and error feedback.
3. **Prove native publication first with a representative story.** Fixture and editor checks need signed-out placement, correct byline and share/related routes, clear withheld/removed behavior, array preservation, mobile preview and direct URL. Choose candidate and placement with Adrian. Show issue readers one authored promise, one cover and a few pieces selected by the editor. Keep the index legible without loading a full gallery or competing cover rails.
4. **Repair the external lane as one slice.** Align the versioned import with supported renderers; make externally managed prose visible and immutable locally, and provide revision diff/review versus the currently live version. Keep the live edition available until an editor explicitly replaces or withdraws it. Keep same-revision replay idempotent and verify stale/conflicting input, wrong account, blocked placement, scoped media, successful exact revision proof and live/unpublish behavior. Connect one real author-controlled edition after synthetic proof.
5. **Triage the draft portfolio under the real movement of issue-making.** Establish which private stories and photographs are approved, who can publish, and which media can sit in a public folder. For each bespoke or database format, mark current support and preview fidelity before promoting a template. Clarify the four conceptual Templates Room cards or connect them to the vetted starter set. Preserve public affordances until replacement behavior is proven. Establish what happens to seeds, the reading memory, post-publish subject feedback and historical-host access through separate explicit decisions.
