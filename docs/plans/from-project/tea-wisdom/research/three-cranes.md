# Three Cranes  (entry-id: three-cranes)
Kind: leaf grade (a mark)     Part of: liu-bao     Related: san-he-liu-bao

## Chinese names
- 三鹤 · sanhe · three cranes

## What is commonly agreed
1. Three Cranes / San He is a trademark of Wuzhou Tea Factory, founded 1953, the largest Liu Bao maker.
   Applies to: the brand, not a style or place.  Agreement: 4 of 5 read
   Places: Sohu article (promotional, zh); Xin An Chu (seller, en); Chawang Shop (seller, en); Global Tea Hut (educator, en)
2. Labels carry a harvest year and a later packing or repack year, and a grade number (e.g. third grade).
   Agreement: 2 of 5 (two sellers) plus Global Tea Hut on grades one to five. Held lightly in the page.

## Where places disagree or are unclear
- Registration year 1991 rests on Sohu and the existing San He page's source; not independently repeated.
