# Yellow Label            (entry-id: yellow-label)
Kind: leaf grade (a mark)
Part of: shou     Related: none

## Chinese names
- 黄标 · huang biao · yellow label (label colour)

## What is commonly agreed
1. The name refers to a yellow label or wrapper mark and is used by several different makers and products, not one maker.
   Applies to: the phrase "yellow label" in pu'er trade naming.
   Agreement: 4 of 4 listing titles seen (titles only; pages themselves not opened)
   Places: eBay listing, Kunming Tea Factory 8281 Big Yellow Label iron cake, raw, 2007 (seller, en); Camellia Sinensis, Pu er Shou 2008 Yellow Label 7572 (seller, en); tuochatea.com, 2005 Haiwan Lao Tong Zhi Yellow Label ripe cake (seller, en); LKY Tea, Pu Erh Tea Cake IV Yellow Label 2006 (seller, en).

## Where places disagree or are unclear
Chinese results (19lou, chinaemail.com.cn, yanggu.tv) discuss yellow label versus white label and the CNNP yellow seal, but their content could not be read.

## What people commonly notice when drinking it
Not applicable: the name is a label colour, not a taste category.

## Not established
- Which maker or makers use the name, and from what year.
- Whether the iron cake in the shop is ripe, and its factory. The record states the factory is not given.
- Any grade meaning, leaf quality or blend tied to the colour.
