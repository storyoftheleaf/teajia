# Blue Seal Green Label (entry-id: blue-seal-green-label)
Kind: leaf grade (a mark)
Part of: sheng     Related: china-national-tea-corporation

## Chinese names
- 蓝印绿标 · lányìn lǜbiāo · blue seal, green label
- 蓝印 · lányìn · Blue Seal/Mark; 绿印 · lǜyìn · Green Seal/Mark

## What is commonly agreed
1. The trade names old Zhong Cha raw cakes by wrapper colour (red, blue, green, yellow seal). Applies to: collector naming of China Tea cakes. Agreement: shown by titles of 9 search results, but only 1 page could be opened. Not met at the 3-read bar.
2. Blue Seal wrappers carry "grade A / grade B" (甲乙级) text and a dark blue stamp. Applies to: Blue Seal wrappers. Agreement: 1 of 1 read.
3. Blue Seal followed Red Seal and was made by China Tea. Agreement: 1 of 1 read.

## Where places disagree or are unclear
- One page treats 绿印 as another name for 蓝印; others (by title) discuss them as separate. Unclear.
- The combined phrase 蓝印绿标 was not found as a trade term in any result. Only the shop record uses it.
