# Da Xue Shan (entry-id: da-xue-shan)
Kind: growing area  Part of: yunnan  Related: lincang

## Chinese names
- 大雪山 · dàxuěshān · Big Snow Mountain

## What is commonly agreed
1. Several Da Xue Shan exist in Lincang (Mengku/Bangma, Bangdong, Yongde, Fengqing). Agreement: 4 of 8 (umiteasets notes Yunnan has many and Yongde; jiemian lists four; thepaper names three; yunnanexploration Fengqing page and Yongde mention). Fengqing is on one page only.
2. Mengku Da Xue Shan is the one usually meant, on Shuangjiang/Gengma border, wild forest 2,200 to 2,750 m. Agreement: 3 (thepaper, jiemian, umiteasets).
3. Mengku wild forest is protected and sealed. Agreement: 2 (jiemian, thepaper says rare/protected) - stated softly on page.
## Where places disagree or are unclear
Altitudes and tree ages vary. Yongde: peak 3,504 m (KBA, sevencups). Yunnan Sourcing places the cake 45 km west of Lincang City without a county.
## Not established
A Da Xue Shan in Menghai (no place read mentions one). Tree ages of the Mengku forest (claims of 1,000+ years from one or two places only). Taste differences between the mountains.
