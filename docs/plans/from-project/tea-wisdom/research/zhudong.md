# Zhudong  (entry-id: zhudong)
Kind: village / township
Part of: hsinchu     Related: beipu, emei

## Chinese names
- 竹東 · Zhudong (also Chutung, Jhudong) · Bamboo East

## What is commonly agreed
1. Zhudong is a township in central Hsinchu County, east of Hsinchu city.
   Applies to: geography only
   Agreement: 2 of 12 read stated it directly (Wikipedia, Hsinchu County culture site); not contradicted anywhere. Below the 3-place bar, kept as plain geography.
2. The main Oriental Beauty townships of Hsinchu are Beipu and Emei; Zhudong is not among those usually named.
   Applies to: Oriental Beauty in Hsinchu County
   Agreement: 6 of 12 (MOA food education site, Hsinchu County farmers' association, MOA knowledge portal, BESTEA, Taiwan Tea Research Station site, Business Today)
   Places: see table

## Where places disagree or are unclear
- Zhudong is named as an Oriental Beauty area by tastetaiwantea.com (with Beipu and Emei) and by teafromtaiwan.com (as Chutung, within a Bai Hao area listing Hsinpu, Kuansi, Chiunglin, Hengshan, Chutung, Peipu, Paoshan, Emei). Only 2 places: not agreed.
- Hsinchu County site notes Zhudong as a camphor production site in the Japanese era (1 place).

## Not established
- Any tea style, cultivar, altitude or maker particular to Zhudong.
- Volume of tea made in Zhudong.
