# Qimen (entry-id: qimen)
Kind: growing area (county)
Part of: anhui     Related: red

- 祁门 · Qímén · Qimen (county)
- 祁门红茶 / 祁红 · Qímén hóngchá / Qíhóng · Keemun
- 祁门香 · Qímén xiāng · Qimen aroma
- 胡元龙 · Hú Yuánlóng; 余干臣 · Yú Gànchén; 槠叶种 · zhūyèzhǒng (local cultivar)

## What is commonly agreed
1. Qimen is a county in southern Anhui, in Huangshan City, near the Huangshan mountains and the Jiangxi border. Applies to: the county. Agreement: 3 of 8 (Wikipedia Qimen, China Visit Guide, Yaza).
2. Keemun is the red tea named after Qimen. Agreement: 8 of 8.
3. Qimen made only green tea before red tea began about 1875, with methods from Fujian (via Jiangxi in some tellings). Agreement: 5 of 8 (Vicony, Yaza, China Visit Guide, Sohu, Anhui News Chinese).
4. Hu Yuanlong is most often named founder; Yu Gancheng is the alternative. Agreement on Hu as common name: 4 of 8 (Vicony, Yaza, Sohu, Anhui News Chinese); Yu named in 3.
5. Setting: hilly wooded land, mild damp climate, rain, cloud and mist. Agreement: 3 of 8 (Vicony, Sohu, Anhui News Chinese).
6. The Qimen aroma (floral, fruity, sweet, sometimes slight smoke) is the defining trait. Agreement: 6 of 8.
7. Small-leaved local cultivar (zhuye zhong). Agreement: 4 (Vicony, Sohu, Wikipedia Keemun via China Visit Guide, Yaza).
8. Keemun-method tea also comes from Dongzhi, Guichi, Shitai, Yixian and Fuliang (Jiangxi). Agreement: 3 (Sohu, China Visit Guide, Yaza).
9. Keemun is used in English-style breakfast blends. Agreement: 3 (Vicony record context, Yaza, China Visit Guide mentions Earl Grey), phrased loosely.

## Where places disagree or are unclear
- Founder and order of events (Hu Yuanlong, Yu Gancheng, Chen Lieqing; factory location; Hu's name written as Hu Yunlong in one place).
- Altitude: 200 to 400 m (Anhui News Chinese) versus 300 to 800 m (shop record). Left off the page.
- Whether the aroma comes from cultivar, place or both.

## Not established
Exact founding year (1875 is "about"), who first made it, altitude range, any claim of Qimen green tea older than Tang sources (Sohu cites Lu Yu, single place), prize and output figures (single places).
