# Red Seal            (entry-id: red-seal)
Kind: leaf grade (a mark)
Part of: sheng     Related: china-national-tea-corporation

## Chinese names
- 紅印 · hóngyìn · red seal / red mark

## What is commonly agreed
1. Red Seal is a colour-named cake from the state era (1950s-60s), sold under the China Tea (Zhong Cha) mark, with Blue and Yellow.
   Applies to: the original cakes only
   Agreement: 3 of 4 read (teadb, Global Tea Hut, house record); dates loose
2. Zhong Cha trademark registered 1951, logo 1952; Zhong Cha wrappers reused later; licensing 1996-2003 brought many counterfeits.
   Applies to: the Zhong Cha mark
   Agreement: 3 (Sohu, teadb, Global Tea Hut)
3. Red Mark cakes are still made and sold today under modern labels (sellers' listings seen in search results: 2003 CNNP Red Mark iron cake, 2021 Xiaguan Red Mark). Title-level only, not read in full.
