## What is commonly agreed
1. Liu Bao comes from Liubao town in Cangwu County, under Wuzhou, eastern Guangxi. Agreement: 5 of 7 (heychina, People's Daily 2023, Xinhua 2025, Global Tea Hut, China Daily 2020/2023, Wikipedia for county).
2. Wuzhou has a humid subtropical monsoon climate, linked by writers to Liu Bao's character. Agreement: 3 of 7 (heychina, Global Tea Hut, China Daily 2023).
3. Hengzhou is China's largest jasmine area. Agreement: 3 of 7 (heychina, China Daily 2025, People's Daily 2026).
4. Production has spread from Cangwu to nearby counties. Agreement: 1 (Global Tea Hut) so worded softly as "has since spread"; flag.
Applies to: place words on Liu Bao and jasmine from Guangxi.

## Where places disagree or are unclear
Output and value figures differ by year and source, so none are on the page. Liu Bao "1,500 years" is repeated by Chinese news but is a claim, left out.
