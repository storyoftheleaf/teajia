## What is commonly agreed
1. Phoenix Mountain oolong (dancong) is Guangdong's best known tea. Agreement: 5 of 9 (heychina, Dominion Tea, Rishi, SCMP, Hey China-independent summary; ycwb listed it in title).
2. Yingde black tea is the province's other known tea. Agreement: 3 of 9 (Dominion Tea, Wikiwand, ycwb title/heychina mention).
3. Guangdong's role is trade more than tea volume; Guangzhou/Fangcun is the main southern tea market, Hong Kong and Taiwan the long-time pu'er buyers. Agreement: 4 (Dominion, gz.gov.cn, Jiemian, World of Chinese).
4. Hong Kong, Guangzhou are hot humid, traditional pu'er storage places. Agreement: 3 (teadb, Global Tea Hut, Guide to Puerh blog).
5. Chaozhou gongfu tea as local brewing. Agreement: 3 (heychina, SCMP, Rishi-adjacent daily-life; asianews not read).
Applies to: Guangdong as a place word.

## Where places disagree or are unclear
Fangcun's share of the pu'er trade (one source says over 80 percent; not used). Whether "Hong Kong storage" tea is stored in Hong Kong (one blog says most now is mainland-made; one source only).
