# Greater Yiwu, new sections   (entry-id: greater-yiwu)
Kind: growing area   Part of: yunnan

## What is commonly agreed
1. Yiwu is in (north) Mengla County, Xishuangbanna, east of the Lancang River, one of the Six Famous Tea Mountains. Agreement: 6 of 7 (jiemian gives no location detail). Places: teadb, orientaleaf, tea-7n9, yunnanexploration, sevencups, Yunnan Sourcing-style local page.
2. Soft, sweet, low bitterness, long sweet aftertaste. Agreement: 7 of 7.
3. Floral and honey fragrance. Agreement: 4 of 7 (orientaleaf, tea-7n9, chinatea-house, sevencups).
4. The name Yiwu is broad and ambiguous; buyers should want a village or plot named. Agreement: 5 of 7 (teadb, chinatea-house, jiemian, orientaleaf, sevencups).
5. Taste varies between subareas. Agreement: teadb, chinatea-house, yunnanexploration.
