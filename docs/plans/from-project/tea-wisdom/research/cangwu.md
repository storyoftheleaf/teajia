# Cangwu            (entry-id: cangwu)
Kind: growing area (county)
Part of: guangxi     Related: liu-bao, guangxi

## Chinese names
- 苍梧 · Cāngwú · Cangwu county
- 六堡镇 · Liùbǎo zhèn · Liubao town (Six Forts, a common reading)
- 梧州 · Wúzhōu · Wuzhou city
- 茶船古道 · chá chuán gǔdào · tea boat ancient road

## What is commonly agreed
1. Liu Bao is named for Liubao town in Cangwu County, Wuzhou, Guangxi; Cangwu is its original home.
   Applies to: the style's origin. Agreement: 10 of 10. Places: all listed below.
2. Liubao town is in the mountainous north of the county, with forest, frequent mist and a subtropical monsoon climate near the Tropic of Cancer.
   Applies to: Liubao town. Agreement: 5 of 10 (Guangxi town database, rmzxw, gxipo, Sina, Global Tea Hut).
3. Tea went by boat from the Hekou landing down the Xijiang to Guangzhou, then Hong Kong, Macau and Southeast Asia (the "tea boat road"); Malaysia and Singapore workers were the main drinkers.
   Applies to: the historical trade route. Agreement: 7 of 10.
4. The 1697 Cangwu gazetteer is the earliest cited record; the Jiaqing-era (1796-1820) listing among famous teas is also cited.
   Applies to: records. Agreement: 5 of 10 (cited, not independently checked).
5. The trade collapsed in the war years, again in the 1990s, and revived from the 2000s; national geographical indication 2011.
   Applies to: the industry. Agreement: 5 of 10.
6. Liubao and Heishi villages are named for the best leaf.
   Applies to: village naming. Agreement: 3 of 10 (rmzxw, sohu, xinanchu in part).

## Where places disagree or are unclear
- Age: "over 1,500 years" is repeated from one scholar's reading of an early text; the Jiaqing date is given as Qianlong in one place.
- Climate figures differ between pages (about 20.5 vs 21.2 C mean, altitude 1,000 to 1,500 m vs peaks under 1,000 m); left out.
- Historic use of the name Liu Bao was wider (nearby counties) in one place only.
- Hong Kong made imitation Liu Bao before and after the war; one place only, so used only to say the word on a label needs care.

## Not established
- Exact founding date of the tea; any tea tree variety grown in Cangwu; whether the protected area includes all of Cangwu; village-level quality rankings beyond Liubao and Heishi.
