# Phoenix Mountain  (entry-id: phoenix-mountain)
Kind: growing area
Part of: none (no Guangdong page exists)     Related: dancong, wudong-mountain-chaozhou

## Chinese names
- 凤凰山 · Fènghuáng Shān · Phoenix Mountain
- 乌岽 · Wūdōng · Wudong, the high ridge
- 大庵 · Dà'ān · Da'an old garden
- 畲族 · Shēzú · She people

## What is commonly agreed
1. Range lies in Fenghuang town, Chao'an, Chaozhou, eastern Guangdong; top peak about 1,500 m. Agreement: 5 of 11 (Wikipedia, Xinhua, 163, goguangdong, teaguardian).
2. Cloud and mist, cool nights, slow growth give thick, aromatic leaf. Agreement: 5 of 11 (Jing, Xinhua, 163, Verdant, Sohu).
3. Wudong is the core high-mountain area. Agreement: 6 of 11 (Wikipedia, Verdant, Sohu, 163, ifeng, Chaozhou gov PDF).
4. Da'an is the largest old garden. Agreement: 3 (163, goguangdong, Jing).
5. Old trees of hundreds of years, some said 600 to 700. Agreement: 6 (Wikipedia, teaguardian, Verdant, gov PDF, goguangdong, 163).
6. Phoenix Mountain as She homeland; gongfu tea tied to Chaozhou. Agreement: 3 (Wikipedia, esgreen, goguangdong).

## Where places disagree or are unclear
- Tea history length: 700, 900, over 1,000 years.
- Soil: yellow granite soil above 800 m (Jing, 163) vs red soil (Xinhua).
- Top peak 1,497.5 or 1,497.8 m.
- Wudong peak 1,391 to 1,397 m.
