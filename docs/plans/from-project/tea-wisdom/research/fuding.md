# Fuding (entry-id: fuding)
Kind: growing area (major region)
Part of: fujian     Related: white, fujian-white

## Chinese names
- 福鼎 · Fuding · county-level city; 太姥山 · Taimu shan · Taimu Mountain; 白琳 · Bailin

## What is commonly agreed
1. Fuding is on Fujian's north-east coast beside Zhejiang, mountains on three sides, sea to the east. Agreement: 5 of 8 (FAO GIAHS, EU GI register, orientaleaf, teavivre, cnipa). Applies to: place.
2. Mostly hills and mountains, heavy forest cover, humid mild monsoon climate with mist, acidic red/yellow soil. Agreement: 5 of 8 (EU GI, FAO, orientaleaf, slideshare, thetea, cnipa). 
3. Home of white tea; with Zhenghe and Jianyang the white tea places; Fuding named as origin. Agreement: 6 (EU GI, bjnews, FAO, fujian.gov.cn, thetea, orientaleaf).
4. Fuding Da Bai and Fuding Da Hao are the main bushes (FAO: nearly 99%; PMC; thetea; EU/others). 4 of 8.
5. Natural withering technique is national ICH (FAO, fujian.gov.cn, chinadaily gov page). 3.
6. Fuding white tea is a protected origin name, GI protection 2009 (EU register states 4 May 2009; cnipa; fujian white page). 3.
7. Taimu and Bailin named as places inside Fuding: bjnews, slideshare, thetea, orientaleaf/teavivre. 4.

## Where places disagree or are unclear
- Founding dates (1796 per orientaleaf, 1800s elsewhere, Tang Classic of Tea reference, tomb finds): not agreed; legend of Langu kept out.
- Altitude of gardens: 500-800 m (orientaleaf, teavivre), 400-800 m (record); left out as data.
- Differences between districts: thetea says they differ but no source says how.

## Not established
- Distinct character of Diantou, Panxi, Guanyang, Taimu, Bailin; founding date; first-use claims; number of townships (17 in one source only).
