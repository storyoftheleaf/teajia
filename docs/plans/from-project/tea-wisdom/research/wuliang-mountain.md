# Wuliang Mountain            (entry-id: wuliang-mountain)
Kind: growing area
Part of: yunnan     Related: kunlu-mountain, lincang, xishuangbanna-tea-mountains

## Chinese names
- 无量山 · Wúliàng Shān · Wuliang Mountain, "without measure"
- 古茶树 · gǔ chá shù · old tea trees
- 困鹿山 · Kùnlù Shān · Kunlu Mountain, a spur at the south end
- 哀牢山 · Āiláo Shān · Ailao Mountains, the neighbouring range

## What is commonly agreed
1. A long range in west-central Yunnan from Nanjian through Jingdong, Zhenyuan, Jinggu toward Ning'er, beside the Lancang River and parallel to Ailao.
   Applies to: the range as a whole
   Agreement: 6 of 9 read
   Places: chinatea.net, yunnanexploration, smzdm, storm.ski, sina blog, teadb
2. Monsoon climate, wet summer, dry misty winter, wide day/night range; tea differs between parts of the range.
   Applies to: the range
   Agreement: 3 of 9 (chinatea.net, yunnanexploration, storm.ski); "differs between parts" also in smzdm and teadb
3. Old tea trees, wild and planted, with Jingdong and Zhenyuan most named; gardens remote.
   Applies to: the range
   Agreement: 5 of 9 (chinatea.net, yunnanexploration, smzdm, sina, yunnansourcing)
4. Name means "without measure", for a mountain too big to measure.
   Agreement: 3 of 9 (chinatea.net, yunnanexploration, smzdm)
5. Label covers a whole range; Kunlu is a spur; Qianjiazhai usually counted to Ailao.
   Agreement: range wide, 6 of 9; Kunlu in range, 3 (smzdm, sina, kunlu page here); Qianjiazhai to Ailao 3 (smzdm, yunnanexploration, verdanttea/trip.com titles from search results only)

## Where places disagree or are unclear
- Main peak: Maotou Mountain 3,306 m, Bijia Mountain 3,374 m, "3,371 to 3,376 m". Not reconciled.
- Qianjiazhai: sina and 163 put Zhenyuan "between Ailao and Wuliang" without assigning the grove; others say Ailao.
- Which counties count: smzdm lists Weishan and Jingdong in the north; chinatea.net names six famous mountains as eastern branches. Lists differ.
- Old name: chinatea.net says Mengle Mountain; smzdm says Dawei Zi or Mengle. Only 2 places.

## What people commonly notice when drinking it
- Fragrant, floral (orchid, chrysanthemum): 4 of 6 (yunnansourcing, helloteacup, smzdm, chinatea.net)
- Soft or low bitterness: 4 of 6 (helloteacup, chinatea.net, teadb, smzdm as light)
- Thin in some examples: 2 of 6 (smzdm, teadb)
- Full body: 2 of 6 (yunnansourcing, teadb buttery)
