# China National Tea Corporation            (entry-id: china-national-tea-corporation)
Kind: producer
Part of: none     Related: y562, menghai-tea-factory, xiaguan-tea-factory

## Chinese names
- 中国茶叶公司 · Zhōngguó Cháyè Gōngsī · China Tea Company
- 中茶 · Zhōngchá · short name, also the brand
- 八中 / 红八中 · bā zhōng / hóng bā zhōng · the eight-zhong mark

## What is commonly agreed
1. Founded 1949 as the state national tea company. Applies to: the company's origin. Agreement: 5 of 8 (news.cn, teadb, cha.com.ua, maigoo, yunnanexploration).
2. Mark is a green 茶 ringed by eight red 中, from the early 1950s. Agreement: 5 of 8 (news.cn 1951, maigoo 1951, chinatea.net 1952, hellotea cup 1951/52, cha.com.ua design). Exact year disagrees.
3. Kunming, Menghai and Xiaguan factories made pu'er under it in the state era. Agreement: 4 of 8 (teadb, cha.com.ua, hellotea cup, yunnansourcing).
4. Brand label does not identify one maker or ensure quality; later also used by smaller makers. Agreement: 4 of 8 (teadb, cha.com.ua, yunnansourcing, hellotea cup).
5. Now part of COFCO. Agreement: 4 of 8 (news.cn, maigoo, hellotea cup, yunnanexploration).

## Where places disagree or are unclear
- Trademark year: 1951 (registered 14 Sept per hellotea cup) vs July 1952 notice (chinatea.net).
- COFCO date: 2004 (news.cn) vs 2006 (hellotea cup).
- Ripe pu'er at Kunming: 1973 (hellotea cup) vs 1984 (news.cn).
- Yunnan origin company date: 1938 (chinatea.net, a Yunnan company, different entity) vs 1944 (hellotea cup).

## Not established
- Chain of company names (CNNP formed 1972, renamed 1988): 1 independent place (hellotea cup, copied by sohu).
- Four-digit code, last digit 1/2/3 for Kunming/Menghai/Xiaguan: 1 place.
- Licensing 1996 to 2003, then brand paused and restored 2006: 1 independent place (teadb mentions franchise-style licensing only).
- Red Seal and Blue Seal meanings: left to the tea pages.
