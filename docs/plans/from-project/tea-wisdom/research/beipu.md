# Beipu (entry-id: beipu)
Kind: growing area (township)
Part of: hsinchu     Related: taiwan, taiwan-oolong, oriental-beauty (style page, not written here)

## Chinese names
- 北埔 · Běibù · Beipu
- 東方美人茶 · Dōngfāng Měirén chá · Oriental Beauty
- 膨風茶 · Péngfēng chá · bragging tea
- 小綠葉蟬 · xiǎo lǜ yèchán · small green leafhopper
- 青心大冇 · Qīngxīn Dàmǎo · Qingxin Dapang cultivar

## What is commonly agreed
1. Beipu is a Hakka township in Hsinchu County, north-western Taiwan, in hill country.
   Applies to: the township. Agreement: 4 of 9 (en/zh Wikipedia, Xianchatea, Wikipedia Dongfang meiren). Places: Wikipedia en and zh, Xianchatea, Wikipedia Dongfang meiren.
2. Beipu is a core Oriental Beauty area with Emei and Zhudong.
   Agreement: 6 of 9. Places: TBRS, Xianchatea, Taste Taiwan Tea, Hanyi, Wikipedia, Hsinchu-area blogs.
3. The tea is made from leafhopper-bitten shoots, picked in early summer, grown without sprays.
   Agreement: 8 of 9.
4. Qingxin Dapang is the main or best-suited cultivar.
   Agreement: 4 of 9 (TBRS, Xianchatea, Taste Taiwan Tea, Hanyi).
5. Other areas also make Oriental Beauty (Miaoli, Taoyuan, New Taipei).
   Agreement: 5 of 9.
6. Cup is honeyed, fruity, heavily oxidised, low bitterness.
   Agreement: 5 of 9 (used in description of the style only, not as a sensory claim).

## Where places disagree or are unclear
- Oxidation quoted as about 60-70% (two places) or about 70% (one).
- Peak picking: May to July, June to July, or Dragon Boat Festival period.
- Beipu as production centre (most) versus "collection hub" (one).

## Not established
- Origin story and who first made it (Jiang A-xin legend appears in 2 places, vague; British queen naming in 3 but is legend).
- Emei overtaking Beipu in output after the 1980s (1 place).
- Basin altitude about 90 to 150 m (1 place); no agreed tea-garden altitude.
- Lei cha (Hakka pounded tea) as a Beipu product (1 place).
- Founding dates of the settlement and tea (1835, 1866, 1867 in different places).
