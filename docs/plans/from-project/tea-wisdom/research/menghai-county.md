# Menghai County (entry-id: menghai-county)
Kind: growing area   Part of: yunnan   Related: banzhang, menghai-tea-factory

## What is commonly agreed
1. Different peoples tend different mountains: Bulang on Bulang Mountain, Lahu in Mengsong, Hani (Aini) on Nannuo with Bulang credited as earliest planters. Agreement: 4 of 7. Places: yunnanexploration Bulang page, GoKunming, Sohu, Google Arts Slow Food.
2. Menghai County and Menghai Tea Factory are different things; the factory blends leaf from several county mountains and some ripe uses Lincang leaf. Agreement: 3 of 7. Places: TeaDB, yunnanexploration factory page, Wikipedia factory mirror (location only).
3. Old gardens grow tall with little pruning and little chemical input, low yield against hedges. Agreement: 3 of 7. Places: GoKunming, yunnanexploration Bulang, Google Arts Slow Food.

## Not established
Founding date (1938/1940 differ), Bulang tea age (sources contradict), which village is "most" Lahu or Hani, tree counts and altitudes (left out as data).
