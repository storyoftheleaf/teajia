# Ya'an   (entry-id: ya-an)
Kind: growing area (major_region)
Part of: none   Related: sichuan-dark, mengding-mountain-ya-an

## Chinese names
- 雅安 · Ya'an · city name
- 雨城 · Yucheng · Rain City, nickname
- 蒙顶山 · Mengding Shan · Mengding Mountain
- 雅安藏茶 · Ya'an Zangcha · Ya'an Tibetan tea

## What is commonly agreed
1. Ya'an is a city at the western edge of the Sichuan Basin, ringed by mountains, on the route to Tibet. Agreement: 4 of 7. Places: Wikipedia, World of Chinese, Chinese essay (thepaper), Teavivre.
2. It is rainy, misty and mild, nicknamed Rain City, and this suits tea. Agreement: 4 of 7. Places: Wikipedia, China Daily govt page, Chinese essay, Teavivre.
3. Mengding Shan (Mingshan) is its famous tea mountain, with green teas (Ganlu, Mao Feng, Shihua) and yellow Huangya. Agreement: 4 of 7. Places: Teavivre, CNIPA, China Daily 2024, Chinese industry summary.
4. Wu Lizhen is credited with first planting tea there (tradition). Agreement: 4 of 7 (Teavivre, CNIPA, China Daily 2024, China Daily govt).
5. Ya'an is the source of tea carried to Kangding and Tibet; known today as Ya'an Tibetan tea. Agreement: 4 of 7 (World of Chinese, Chinese essay, Teavivre, Wikipedia).

## Where places disagree or are unclear
Mengding annual rainfall quoted about 1,400 mm (Teavivre) vs about 1,650-1,700 mm for the city (Wikipedia). Elevation of Mengding 557 to 1,456 m.

## Not established
Tribute start year (742) and Tea-Horse Office year (1074): one place. Ya'an share of Tibetan tea sales (over 80 percent) and Qing share (70 percent): one unsourced essay. Production and brand value figures: one or two places, not used. Age of Wu Lizhen story: tradition only.
