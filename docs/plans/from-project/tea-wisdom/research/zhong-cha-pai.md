# Zhong Cha Pai            (entry-id: zhong-cha-pai)
Kind: leaf grade (a mark / brand; not a leaf grade in the strict sense)
Part of: shou     Related: china-national-tea-corporation

## Chinese names
- 中茶牌 · zhōngchápái · Zhong Cha brand
- 内飞 · nèifēi · inner ticket; 内票 · nèipiào · insert

## What is commonly agreed
1. Zhong Cha Pai is the brand of the China National Tea Corporation (CNNP), used on pu'er cakes, bricks and loose tea.
   Applies to: tea sold under the mark. Agreement: 5 of 5 read (plus the existing company page).
   Places: teadb, sohu, teasenz, thetea.app, madmonktea.
2. Until the 1980s state factories made pu'er under the mark; afterwards the large factories moved to own brands and smaller makers used the Zhong Cha name.
   Agreement: 3 of 5 (teadb, sohu, madmonktea; teasenz and thetea.app say factories vary).
3. The wrapper does not identify the factory, year or quality; recipes and makers differ widely.
   Agreement: 4 of 5 (teadb, madmonktea, thetea.app, teasenz implicit).
4. The mark is among the most copied; check inner ticket, insert, recipe against year.
   Agreement: 2 of 5 (thetea.app, teadb on faked age). Phrased cautiously on the page.

## Where places disagree or are unclear
- Whether Kunming or Menghai first made wet-piled ripe tea (madmonktea credits Kunming; not used on page).
- Whether licensing without quality control has ended (madmonktea says yes; teadb describes it as a cause of 2007 crash; one place each). Not stated on page.
- Description of the logo: sources agree eight zhong around a central cha; the marks.csv description ("eight-pointed zhong ringed in red") does not match and should be corrected by the lead. Logo is not described on the page; it lives on the company page.

## What people commonly notice when drinking it
Not established: taste varies by factory and era.

## Not established
