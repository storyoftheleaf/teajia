# Gongjian            (entry-id: gongjian)
Kind: leaf grade
Part of: anhua-dark     Related: tianjian, shengjian

## Chinese names
- 贡尖 · gòngjiān · tribute tip
- 天尖 · tiānjiān · the finest tip; 生尖 · shēngjiān · the coarsest tip
- 湘尖 · xiāngjiān · Hunan tip, the family name

## What is commonly agreed
1. Gongjian is one of three loose Anhua teas (the three tips) with Tianjian and Shengjian, ranked by leaf, Gongjian in the middle.
   Applies to: Anhua dark tea
   Agreement: 3 of 3 that could be judged (existing approved anhua-dark page, one seller page read in full, search-result titles of 8 Chinese pages that all treat the three together). Full text of the Chinese pages could not be read (see below).
2. Made from second-grade, more mature leaf than Tianjian; wet piled, dried over wood fire; smoky, pine, sweet, orange liquor.
   Agreement: 1 read in full plus the approved anhua-dark page; below 3, so taste kept to the general Anhua impression only.

## Where places disagree or are unclear
Not established: why it is called "tribute"; any founding date.

## What people commonly notice when drinking it
Pine or wood smoke, sweetness, thick smooth feel, clear orange liquor (1 seller page plus the Anhua page). General to Anhua tea.

## Not established
Name origin, dates, any figure for leaf size or stalk share, and whether it was ever sent as tribute.
