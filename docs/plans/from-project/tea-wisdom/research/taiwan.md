# Taiwan   (entry-id: taiwan)
Kind: growing area (major region)     Part of: none     Related: taiwan-oolong, oolong

## Chinese names
- 台灣 / 台湾 · Táiwān · Taiwan
- 高山茶 · gāoshān chá · high mountain tea
- 烏龍茶 · wūlóng chá · oolong

## What is commonly agreed
1. Island off Fujian; central mountain range shelters the west, where gardens are. Agreement: 4 of 7. Places: Umi, Saratoga, Michelin, Wikipedia.
2. Oolong is the signature; high mountain tea is above about 1,000 m, cool, foggy, sweet. Agreement: 6 of 7.
3. Regions: north (Pinglin Baozhong, Muzha Tieguanyin), Hsinchu/Miaoli Oriental Beauty, Nantou largest and Lugu Dong Ding, Chiayi Alishan, Taichung Lishan/Dayuling, east smaller. Agreement: 6 of 7.
4. Fujian settlers brought tea and craft; nineteenth-century export as Formosa Oolong; Japanese era black tea. Agreement: 4 of 7 (Michelin, Saratoga, Wikipedia, existing taiwan-oolong page).
5. Black tea around Yuchi/Puli, Nantou. Agreement: 3 of 7 (MOA, Wikipedia, Umi).

## Where places disagree or are unclear
Dates (1717, 1855, 1860s); mountain heights; whether Chiayi is "newer"; Dong Ding "earliest" claims.

## Not established
Exact founding dates, peak heights, share drunk at home, red oolong origin.
