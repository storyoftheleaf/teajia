# Zhenghe  (entry-id: zhenghe)
Kind: growing area (county)
Part of: fujian     Related: white, fujian-white, yunnan-white

## Chinese names
- 政和 · Zhenghe · county name (Song-era reign title)
- 政和大白茶 · Zhenghe Dabaicha · Zhenghe Da Bai cultivar
- 政和工夫 · Zhenghe Gongfu · Zhenghe gongfu black tea
- 铁山 · Tieshan · township where the cultivar is placed

## What is commonly agreed
1. Zhenghe is an inland mountain county in Nanping, northern Fujian, forested, misty, cooler with larger day-night swings than coastal Fuding.
   Applies to: the county. Agreement: 5 of 11 (Wikipedia en, Valley Brook, Tearoom SG, China Culture Pro, tesegu).
2. With Fuding, Zhenghe is one of two main white tea homes; it has its own cultivar Zhenghe Da Bai, tied to Tieshan, found late 19th century.
   Agreement: 7 of 11. Places: Valley Brook, Wikipedia Baihao Yinzhen, Tearoom SG, China Culture Pro, Laenggasstee, Lapsang Store, tesegu.
3. Zhenghe Da Bai buds later than Fuding bushes; much Zhenghe leaf withered indoors.
   Agreement: 4 of 11 (Valley Brook, Laenggasstee, China Culture Pro, Lapsang Store).
4. Compared with Fuding, Zhenghe is called fuller, rounder, mellower, thicker.
   Agreement: 5 of 6 that compare (Valley Brook, Wikipedia Baihao Yinzhen, Tearoom SG, China Culture Pro, Lapsang Store). Disagrees: teas.co.uk.
5. Zhenghe Da Bai is used for white, black (Zhenghe Gongfu) and scented green.
   Agreement: 4 (Laenggasstee, tesegu cultivar page, Teapottravel, tesegu gongfu page).

## Where places disagree or are unclear
- Altitude: about 800 m average (Valley Brook), 200 to 1,000 m (tesegu), record 400 to 900 m. Left out.
- Teas.co.uk rejects the Fuding light / Zhenghe deep contrast. Reflected in the page.
- Gongfu taste: teapottravel (winey, full, peppery), tesegu (violet scent, mellow sweet). 2 places: worded loosely, "some sellers".

## Not established
- Huizong renaming the county in 1115 after tribute tea: repeated by 6 pages that copy one another, no record seen. Kept as story. Wikipedia en says the county was Guanli before.
- Gongfu black tea begun 1874 by Jiangxi merchants; ranked first of Min congou in 1896; exports to Russia; Panama-Pacific medal: 2 places or fewer each.
- Cultivar discovery year (1880, Wei Nian family courtyard): 1 place. "Late nineteenth century": 3.
- Zhenghe white tea GI in 2009, 1984 certification as Hua Cha 5: 1 to 2 places.
- Three famous Fujian congou (Bailin, Tanyang, Zhenghe): 2 places read, third source (Baidu) not reachable.
- "South Road Silver Needle": 1 place.
