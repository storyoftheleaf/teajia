# Hsinchu   (entry-id: hsinchu)
Kind: growing area (county)
Part of: taiwan     Related: beipu, zhudong, taiwan-oolong

## Chinese names
- 新竹 · Xīnzhú · Hsinchu; 峨眉 · Éméi; 北埔 · Běipǔ; 橫山 · Héngshān; 竹東 · Zhúdōng; 關西 · Guānxī
- 東方美人 · Dōngfāng měirén · Oriental Beauty; 膨風茶 · péngfēng chá · braggart's tea; 白毫烏龍 · white tip oolong; 小綠葉蟬 · small green leafhopper; 擂茶 · léichá

## What is commonly agreed
1. Hsinchu County is the home of Oriental Beauty. Agreement: 7 of 10 (Wikipedia Dongfang meiren, NYCU paper, MOA report, Yam article, Johor Kaki blog, In Pursuit of Tea, Taiwan Plus).
2. Emei and Beipu are the main townships for it. Agreement: 5 (Wikipedia, NYCU, MOA, Yam, Taiwan Plus for Beipu). Hengshan and Zhudong: MOA, UDN (Hengshan). Guanxi: Yam, NYCU. Stated as "also named".
3. Leafhopper bite, summer, no pesticide, hand picking. Agreement: 5 (Wikipedia, NYCU, Yam, In Pursuit of Tea, Johor Kaki).
4. Names bai hao and pengfeng cha. Agreement: 5 (Wikipedia, MOA, Yam, Johor Kaki, In Pursuit of Tea for bai hao).
5. Hakka trade. Agreement: 4 (NYCU, Taiwan Plus, Wikipedia Beipu, Wikipedia Hsinchu County).
6. Geography and Hakka majority: Wikipedia Hsinchu County (1, plain geography, basic).

## Where places disagree or are unclear
- Origin legends (queen, Dodd, 1933 competition, Ming immigrants, 200 years) differ widely: left out, stated unsettled.
- Emei larger than Beipu: NYCU only; stated as "described".
- Production spread to Miaoli, Taoyuan, Guanxi: NYCU only; stated softly.

## Not established
- Other teas in quantity (only UDN lists six teas served at an Emei event); cultivars; altitude; output (Yam gives 385 ha, 390 t, one place); lei cha as tea tradition (one place).
