# Anhui            (entry-id: anhui)
Kind: growing area (province)
Part of: none     Related: liu-an, green, red, guangxi

- 祁门红茶 · Qimen hongcha · Keemun

## What is commonly agreed
1. Tea grows in the south and west hills (Huangshan, Dabie), not the north. Agreement: 4 of 9 (Orientaleaf, Vicony, medium, tea-lab/heychina partial).
2. Humid subtropical, misty, four seasons, acidic soil. Agreement: 4 (Orientaleaf, Harney, HeyChina, Vicony).
3. Famous for green: Maofeng, Houkui, Guapian; Keemun the red. Agreement: 9 of 9.
4. Four areas Huangshan, Taiping, Qimen, Lu'an. Agreement: 6.
5. Keemun began late 19th century in Qimen. Agreement: 4 (Harney, Orientaleaf, Wikipedia, Vicony) plus existing red.md.
6. Guapian from single leaves, no bud. Agreement: 4 (ihchina, HeyChina, tea-lab, Wikipedia search listing).
7. Liu An basket tea is made in Qimen, dark; Lu'an Guapian from Lu'an city. Agreement: tea-lab, existing liu-an.md page; third place not found. Kept as a clarification; flagged below.

## Where places disagree or are unclear
- Number of "Ten Famous Teas" from Anhui: three (maigoo, smzdm, Vicony Qimen/Guapian/Maofeng) vs five (HeyChina, adds Houkui, Dinggu Dafang). Page says "several" and avoids a count.
- Tea history length: 1,800 years (ihchina), 2,000 years (Vicony), "thousand years" (HeyChina). Not used.

## Not established
- Count of famous teas, age of tea growing, founder of Keemun, Fujian origin of Keemun method (one place only), Huoshan Huangya details beyond "rare yellow tea".
- Point 7 has only 2 independent places for Liu An in Qimen; the wording is kept short.
