# Wuzhou Tea Factory            (entry-id: wuzhou-tea-factory)
Kind: producer
Part of: none     Related: san-he-liu-bao, liu-bao, wuzhou, three-cranes

## Chinese names
- 梧州茶厂 · wúzhōu cháchǎng · Wuzhou Tea Factory
- 三鹤 · sān hè · Three Cranes (San He), the brand

## What is commonly agreed
1. Opened 1953 in Wuzhou under the national tea company, later cooperative/state ownership, now a company.
   Applies to: the factory only.   Agreement: 3 of 5 (founding date); ownership years differ (1955 / 1956).
   Places: Sohu 556303346, Sohu 622979907, egoll (all Chinese; the first two read like factory-sourced material).
2. Made mainly for export to Hong Kong, Macau, Southeast Asia; one of two Liu Bao firms in 2002; 2003 standard drawn from their practice.
   Agreement: 3 of 5 (Global Tea Hut 811, Jiemian, Sohu 556303346).
3. A maker's name is not a grade or age (trade reading, not a quoted claim).

## Where places disagree or are unclear
- Year the factory passed to the supply and marketing cooperative: 1955 or 1956. Renaming year: 1958 or 1965 (after 1958 per egoll). Trademark year: 1990 or 1991 (the san-he-liu-bao page uses 1991).
- Year of the move to Lijiazhuang and the 1975 export split: one source each.

## Not established
- Meaning of six-digit product codes; the big-basket numbering of the 1990s (one source).
- Output, prices, awards (promotional, one source each). Leaf sourcing.
- "Cleaner than village Liu Bao": seller claim only, left out.
