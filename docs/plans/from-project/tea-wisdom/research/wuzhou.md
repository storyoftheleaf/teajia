- 西江 · Xijiang · Xi River

## What is commonly agreed
1. Wuzhou is a city in eastern Guangxi on the Guangdong border, where the Xi, Gui (and Xun) rivers meet. Agreement: 4 of 9. Places: Britannica, Xinhua 2024, China Daily CN, China Daily govt.
2. It is a river port and gateway to the coast; opened as treaty port 1897. Agreement: 4 of 9 (1897: Britannica, Xinhua 2024, Global Tea Hut/Umi; 3 of 9).
3. Liu Bao is named for Liubao town in Cangwu County, Wuzhou; the tea went by small boat to the river, then to Guangzhou and overseas. Agreement: 6 of 9 (CGTN, People's Daily, Global Tea Hut, Umi, Xinhua 2024, Xinhua 2025).
4. Trade fell after 1937 and with the planned economy and farmers leaving tea; revival as a large local industry. Agreement: 3 of 9 (Umi, Global Tea Hut/Umi, CGTN, People's Daily).

## Where places disagree or are unclear
- Output figures: 1,500 t down to 197.5 t (CGTN); 17,000 t (2019) to 35,000 t (2023) (People's Daily, Xinhua 2024); over 40,000 t (Xinhua 2025); over 20,000 t (CGTN). Not used.
- Age of Liu Bao: "over 1,500 years" (People's Daily, Global Tea Hut) against Tang/Song emergence (CGTN). Not used.
