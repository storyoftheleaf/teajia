# Tongqinghao   (entry-id: tongqinghao)
Kind: producer (historic house, revived brand)
Part of: none     Related: yiwu (greater-yiwu)

## Chinese names
- 同慶號 / 同庆号 · Tóngqìng hào · Tongqing house (hao = trading house)

## What is commonly agreed
1. A pu'er house from Yiwu, Qing era origin. Applies to: the historic house. Agreement: 4 of 4 read (not fully independent: all trace to brand telling). Places: Xin An Chu, Yunnan Craft, Tea Urchin, CNPP.
2. The brand was revived in the 2000s and is sold today as a modern Xishuangbanna brand. Applies to: modern brand. Agreement: 3 of 4 (Xin An Chu, Tea Urchin, CNPP).
3. Antique cakes sell at auction for very high sums. Applies to: surviving old cakes. Agreement: 2 of 4 read plus the shop's record; used with the record only for the general point.

## Where places disagree or are unclear
- Ownership: Xin An Chu reports two companies (Xishuangbanna and Yiwu) with conflicting court rulings; CNPP lists only the Xishuangbanna company. One detailed account only.
- Closure: 1948 per two places (Xin An Chu, CNPP); worded here as "around the middle of the twentieth century".

## Not established
- Founding in 1736 and the founder's name (Liu Hancheng, one place); "six selections, six discards" as earliest standard; royal tribute status; Dragon and Horse mark dates; descendants holding recipes; quoted auction prices; state gifts.
- What year or maker the shop's tea has: only the record, which says uncertain.
