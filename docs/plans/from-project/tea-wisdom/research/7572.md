# 7572   (entry-id: 7572)
Kind: leaf grade (recipe mark)
Part of: shou     Related: gong-ting, y562, menghai-county

## Chinese names
- 唛号 · màihào · mark number, the trade word for a recipe code

## What is commonly agreed
1. The first two digits are the year the recipe was first made (1975).
   Applies to: the 7572 recipe. Agreement: 3 of 3 read (digit pages).
   Places: White2Tea (seller blog, English); puerh.app (educator reference, English); Teasenz (seller listing, English).
2. The third digit is the leaf grade of the main blend, 1 finest to 9 coarsest.
   Agreement: 3 of 3 read (Teasenz says only "leaf size, relatively large").
3. The last digit 2 is the Menghai factory code (1 Kunming, 3 Xiaguan, 4 Pu'er listed by two places).
   Agreement: 3 of 3.
4. The number is the recipe's, so it does not date a given cake. Follows from 1; White2Tea and Teasenz both sell cakes from other years under the same number.
5. Menghai Tea Factory made the original, now under Dayi/Taetea. Agreement: 2 read plus search listings.

## Where places disagree or are unclear
- puerh.app calls the blend grade 5 at the core while the third digit is 7; the same page gives two pile lengths (52 and 60 days). Its other figures (10 tonnes, 4.82 yuan/kg, 2,000 tonnes a year) are single-place and left out.
- White2Tea says other makers (Kunming, CNNP Zhongcha) also produce a 7572; search listings (sculx.cn title "中茶7572") hint the same. Only one place read, so not used on the page.
