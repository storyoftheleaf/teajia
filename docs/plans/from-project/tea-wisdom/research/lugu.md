# Lugu (entry-id: lugu)
Kind: village / township
Part of: nantou   Related: taiwan-oolong, dong-ding (style, on taiwan-oolong)

## Chinese names
- 鹿谷 · Lùgǔ · deer valley (township)
- 凍頂 · Dòngdǐng · frozen peak
- 林鳳池 · Lín Fèngchí · man credited with bringing bushes
- 大崙山 · Dàlùnshān · higher tea mountain in Lugu

## What is commonly agreed
1. Lugu is a mountain township in the south west of Nantou, home of Dong Ding oolong.
   Applies to: place. Agreement: 6 of 8. Places: Lugu township site, Wikipedia, MOC, Kembel, Lugu special products page, Foodnext.
2. Bushes came from Fujian's Wuyi area in the 19th century; Lin Fengchi story (36 seedlings).
   Applies to: place history. Agreement: 5 of 8 on Fujian origin and 19th century; the Lin Fengchi detail in 3 (Kembel, Foodnext, special products page). Year differs: 1851 (Lugu township site), 1855 (MOC-linked travel page, Foodnext, special products).
3. Cool, cloud and mist setting. Agreement: 3 (MOA, travel in Taiwan, Wikipedia Dong Ding).
4. Bamboo is the other famous product. Agreement: 3 (Lugu township site, special products, MOA).
5. Tea became the main local livelihood. Agreement: 3 (Lugu township site, MOC, special products).

## Where places disagree or are unclear
- Year: 1851 or 1855. Altitude: Dong Ding mountain about 750 m (MOA); Dong Ding tea 600 to 1,800 m (Kembel); Dalunshan 1,250 to 1,500 m (travel page). Reason for move to Dalunshan (soil fertility) from one place only.

## Not established
- Exact year and planter of first bushes; township altitude range; grade-mark system status beyond the farmers association (one place); anything about Xitou as a tea area (travel page says it is not described as one).
