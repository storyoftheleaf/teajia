## What is commonly agreed
1. Nantou is Taiwan's largest tea county, more than half of area and output. Applies to: county. Agreement: 3 of 8 (gvm, umi, teafromtaiwan).
2. Tea country spans low, middle and high ground (Mingjian low; Zhushan, Sun Moon Lake, Dong Ding middle; Shan Lin Xi, Yushan, Hehuanshan, Ren'ai high). Agreement: 3 (gvm, umi, vocus/foodnext).
3. Lugu is in Nantou and is home of Dong Ding. Agreement: 5 (wikipedia Lugu, wikipedia Nantou, teafromtaiwan, umi, gvm).
4. Shan Lin Xi is Nantou high mountain tea near Dong Ding. Agreement: 4 (gvm, vocus names Zhushan, medium, umi).
5. Yuchi/Sun Moon Lake makes black tea (Assam type). Agreement: 5 (teafromtaiwan, umi, gvm, wikipedia Nantou, thenewslens).
6. Lishan is in Taichung (umi, vocus: 2, plus Taichung implied by foodnext not stated). Alishan is Chiayi (umi, vocus). Ren'ai Township is Nantou high mountain tea (thenewslens, vocus, foodnext: 3).

## Where places disagree or are unclear
- Shan Lin Xi altitude: 600-1950 m (vocus), 1600-1800 m (another article), up to 1650 m (umi). Shown as disputed.
- Dayuling county: Nantou vs Hualien/Taichung border. Disputed.
- Lishan county: only 2 places state Taichung; stated as "sources place it in Taichung". Wikiwand says only that Nantou and Chiayi ranges grow high mountain tea.
- Whether Sun Moon Lake black tea cultivar TTES 18: one place only; left out.

## Not established
- Exact tea area or output figures (quoted differently); first tea planting dates; Xitou; Wuling and Cuifeng counties.
