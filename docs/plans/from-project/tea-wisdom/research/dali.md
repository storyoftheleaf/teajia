# Dali (entry-id: dali)
Kind: growing area (prefecture)   Part of: yunnan   Related: lincang, xiaguan factory page (parallel)

## Chinese names
- 大理 · Dàlǐ · Dali
- 下关 · Xiàguān · Xiaguan
- 沱茶 · tuóchá · bowl tea

## What is commonly agreed
1. Xiaguan, in Dali, was a trade and pressing hub for compressed tea on the routes to Sichuan and Tibet. Agreement: 4 of 9 (yunnanexploration, chinanews, umiteasets, visityunnanchina).
2. The bowl shaped tuocha is tied to Xiaguan trading houses (Yongchangxiang named by two). Agreement: 5 of 9 (yunnanexploration, visityunnanchina, businesstoday, teasenz, umiteasets).
3. Xiaguan tuocha leaf is blended from outside Dali (rest of Yunnan, Fengqing/Lincang, Menghai). Agreement: 3 (yunnanexploration, businesstoday, umiteasets on Lincang planning).
4. Dali grows little tea, mainly Cang mountains and Wuliang range (Nanjian); green teas common; transitional between large and small leaf zones. Agreement: 3 (ratetea, yunnanexploration, sohu).

## Where places disagree or are unclear
- Tuocha year: 1902 (yunnanexploration, visityunnan), about 1916 (businesstoday), 1951 or 1975 for the factory's own production (umi, chinanews).
- Factory founding: 1902 vs 1941; that is the factory's page, not this one.

## Not established
- Year tuocha first made; French export story (one place); local Dali tea tasting character; altitude; which counties produce how much.
