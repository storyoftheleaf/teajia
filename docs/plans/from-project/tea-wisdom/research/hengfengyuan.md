# Hengfengyuan   (entry-id: hengfengyuan)
Kind: producer
Part of: none     Related: golden-bull, shou

## Chinese names
- 恒丰源 · Héngfēngyuán · brand name (Heng Feng Yuan)
- 昌泰 · Chāngtài · Changtai, owner of the brand

## What is commonly agreed
1. Hengfengyuan is a brand of Changtai (Yunnan Changtai group, Xishuangbanna), one of several it sells under.
   Applies to: the brand name on Yunnan pu'er. Not any single tea.
   Agreement: 5 of 6 read (Changtai's own site, Yunnan Exploration, King Tea Mall, Sina blog, Sohu article; Steepster listing too)
   Places: see table
2. Hengfengyuan is Changtai's ripe (fermented) brand: cakes, tuo cha, loose.
   Applies to: the brand. Agreement: 3 (Changtai site, Sina blog, King Tea Mall blog; Steepster calls its tea ripe)
   The Sohu article does not mention ripe tea at all (silent, not contrary).

## Where places disagree or are unclear
- Origin of the name: Changtai's site says an old brand from 1906, registered by the group in 2000. King Tea Mall and the Sina blog say it was a tea house name in Jinggu, acquired about 2001 (the two probably copy each other). Steepster says it was one of two predecessors of Changtai from 1996 (one place). Not reconciled.
- Changtai's own founding year: 1998 or 1999 across pages.

## Not established
- Who founded Hengfengyuan, and when. Any date.
- That the "Golden Bull Hengfengyuan Shou Cha" in the shop is Changtai's: no place names Golden Bull with Hengfengyuan. The name match is plausible but unproven; page does not claim it.
- Where its leaf comes from (one seller says mainly Menghai; one place only).
- Heritage claims (1906), scale figures: single-source, left out.
