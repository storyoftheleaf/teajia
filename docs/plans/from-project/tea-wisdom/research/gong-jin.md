# Gong Jin            (entry-id: gong-jin)
Kind: leaf grade (mark); in fact a selling name
Part of: shou     Related: gong-ting, nanqiao-ruigong-brick

## Chinese names
- 贡金 · gòngjīn · tribute gold

## What is commonly agreed
1. It appears in product titles on pressed ripe pu'er, with no grade definition given.
   Applies to: bricks from Menghai makers.
   Agreement: 3 of 5 read (Yunnan Sourcing 2012 Ye Zhuang Shuang Li brick; ipucha Longyuan 2020 brick listing; existing nanqiao-ruigong-brick page and its catalogue source).
2. The established ripe grade names are Gong Ting and similar, and Gong Jin is not among them.
   Agreement: 3 of 5 (Yunnan Sourcing Gong Ting listings, Hojo Tea, existing gong-ting page); none lists Gong Jin as a step.

## Where places disagree or are unclear
Record text calls it a "tribute-grade classification"; no page read supports that.

## Not established
Meaning, start date, maker, any link to a particular leaf. The marks.csv "Qiaorui" producer is unconfirmed. Search snippets show 贡金 also names a Shaanxi fu tea (Jingwei); only titles seen, pages not read, so not used as a claim beyond "unrelated dark teas".
