# Xiaguan Tea Factory            (entry-id: xiaguan-tea-factory)
Kind: producer
Part of: none     Related: dali (place, written in parallel), shou, sheng, china-national-tea-corporation

## Chinese names
- 下关茶厂 · xiàguān cháchǎng · Xiaguan Tea Factory
- 康藏茶厂 · kāngzàng cháchǎng · Kangzang Tea Factory (1941 predecessor)
- 沱茶 · tuóchá · bowl-shaped pressed tea
- 铁饼 · tiěbǐng · iron cake
- 中茶 · zhōngchá · Zhong Cha, state tea company mark

## What is commonly agreed
1. Began in 1941 as the Kangzang Tea Factory in Xiaguan, Dali, to press tea for Tibet and border regions.
   Applies to: the maker. Agreement: 5 of 8 read (dushatea, meridiancha, teartchat, sohu, qifullife).
2. Tuocha is its signature; the shape predates the factory. Agreement: 4 of 8 (dushatea, meridiancha, teartchat, qifullife).
3. After 1949 under the state tea company, Zhong Cha mark in the early 1950s, private Xiaguan houses merged in the mid 1950s. Agreement: 4 of 8 (teartchat, sohu, dushatea, qifullife).
4. Reorganised as a joint-stock company in the 1990s. Agreement: 4 of 8 (dushatea, teartchat, sohu, qifullife).
5. Smoky edge, dense iron-press cakes, tends to soften with age. Smoke: meridiancha, qifullife, teaswelike, yunnansourcing (reviewer): 4 of 8. Dense iron cake: yunnansourcing, teaswelike: 2 read plus listing titles; Baidu Baike entry on the iron cake and the smzdm posts were seen as search results only. Softening with age: qifullife, dushatea, teaswelike, meridiancha (partly): 3-4.
6. Ripe pu'er made from the 1970s. Agreement: 3 of 8 (teartchat "1970s", sohu 1976 workshop, meridiancha 1970s xiaofatuo), plus existing shou.md section on 1973.

## Where places disagree or are unclear
- Date of private ownership: teartchat says 2004, qifullife "early 2000s", dushatea and sohu silent.
- Year ripe began: 1970s (teartchat), 1976 workshop (sohu), 1958 steam method for pressed jincha (meridiancha, and shou.md).
- Whether smoke fades: qifullife says matured tea develops smoky and woody notes; others say it softens.
