# Muzha  (entry-id: muzha)
Kind: growing area
Part of: taiwan     Related: tieguanyin, anxi

## Chinese names
- 木柵 · Mùzhà · Muzha (district of southeast Taipei)
- 貓空 · Māokōng · Maokong (tea hillside in Muzha)
- 木柵鐵觀音 · Mùzhà Tiěguānyīn · Muzha Tieguanyin
- 正欉鐵觀音 · zhèngcóng Tiěguānyīn · Tieguanyin from the original cultivar
- 張迺妙 · Zhāng Nǎimiào · tea master named for the 1895 introduction

## What is commonly agreed
1. Muzha/Maokong is a tea hill area in Wenshan District, southeast Taipei, in low hills.
   Applies to: the place. Agreement: 6 of 8. Places: Taipei City Gov, Maokong Gondola guide, taiwantea.jp, tastetaiwantea, Taipei Locals, Trident.
2. Tieguanyin came from Anxi, Fujian; 1895 Zhang Naimiao named as the introducer.
   Applies to: how Tieguanyin reached Muzha. Agreement: 6 of 8. Places: Taipei City Gov, Trident, Maokong Gondola, CommonWealth, Taipei Locals, Taipei agriculture site (zh).
3. Traditional Muzha Tieguanyin is a ball-rolled, part-oxidised, charcoal-roasted oolong, darker and heavier than lightly finished Fujian style.
   Applies to: the style. Agreement: 5 of 8. Places: taiwantea.jp, tastetaiwantea, Trident, Maokong Gondola, CommonWealth.
4. Notes: roast, ripe fruit, nuts, honey; amber liquor; sweet return (about 5 of 8).
5. Farms open to visitors, most grow Tieguanyin (Taipei City Gov, Taipei Locals, Maokong Gondola, taiwantea.jp: 4 of 8).
6. Authentic Tieguanyin cultivar is scarce; other cultivars (Qingxin) used (CommonWealth, taiwantea.jp, Taipei agriculture site hints: 3 of 8).

## Where places disagree or are unclear
- Later plant shipments: 1919 with a thousand plants (tastetaiwantea), 1926 a thousand (Maokong Gondola). Introduced plants: 12 (Trident, Maokong Gondola).
- Altitude: 300 to 350 m (agriculture site, Trident) vs 300 to 500 m (Maokong Gondola). Left out.
- Fermentation: 3 to 4 tenths (tastetaiwantea) vs 15 to 30 percent (agriculture site). Left out.
- Tourism dates: 1960s, 1980, 2007 differ.
- "Only place in Taiwan growing Tieguanyin": 2 places say so, not agreed. Left out.
- Founding story: "over 200 years ago" (tastetaiwantea) conflicts with 1895.

## What people commonly notice when drinking it
Roast, ripe fruit, nuts, honey, amber cup, full body, sweet return.

## Not established
Later shipment years; altitude range; oxidation percentage; Baozhong link (one place); whether Muzha is the only Taiwan Tieguanyin area; tourism dates.
