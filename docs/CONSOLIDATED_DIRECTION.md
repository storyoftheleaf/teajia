## Sequencing

1. Resolve and verify the reading-memory/saved-story discrepancy.
2. Use `LAUNCH_VALIDATION.md` as the sole launch sequence and completion record.
3. Feed evidence from real use into the three active engineering tracks.
4. Prefer content, operating proof, and small corrections over new platform foundations.

## Guardrails

- Preserve inquiry-led commerce; do not add conventional automated checkout.
- Preserve human curation; do not add rankings, review aggregation, or recommendation engines.
- Do not merge Journal, Favorites, Cellar, and Curate into one engagement model.
- Do not add feeds, follows, likes, streaks, badges, engagement notifications, or phone-at-the-table modes.
- Do not reopen route labels or navigation without explicit approval.
- Do not treat local fixtures as proof of deployed delivery, business-data safety, mainland reachability, operator usability, or authentic contributor voice.

## Definition of progress

Progress is demonstrated by one of four things:

- verified working behavior;
- a completed real-world validation item;
- approved editorial material published through the real workflow; or
- a retired piece of dead or contradictory surface area.

New roadmap prose, duplicate audits, and parallel launch checklists do not count as progress.
