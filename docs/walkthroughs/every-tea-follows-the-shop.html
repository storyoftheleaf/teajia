Every tea follows the shop

Your rule, made true on the whole shelf at once. No tea keeps a freight rate of its own, so the zeros and the gold dots both go, and the shop rate applies to everything.

85 yuan a kilo, air from China, is the rate. It is one number, in one place, converted to dollars live. Renegotiate it and every tea moves with it, because none of them carry a copy.

Why every rate goes, including the ones that look deliberate

Three separate doors wrote freight rates onto teas that nobody chose, and all three are now fixed but all three had already written rows:

The Add Product form pre-filled the shop rate as a value, so every tea you added by hand was stamped with that day's number and stopped following it. Clearing that field saved a zero, meaning ships free, so the only way to unpin a tea was also the way to make it free. And migration 0008 wrote a figure onto every tea that had none, which 0010 only partly undid.

After that, a rate sitting on a row is not evidence that anyone chose it. So the honest move is to clear all of them and let you re-pin the few that genuinely differ, which is a sentence now rather than a form.

No price can go down from this. A tea that was shipping free starts carrying freight, and freight sits inside the cost basis the markup multiplies. If a tea really does ship free, tell the shop and it goes back to zero.

Adjusting one tea, which is now a sentence

As you add it

create_tea takes a freight rate now. Leave it out and the tea follows the 85. Give a number only when that tea cost more or less to ship.

Afterwards

update_tea_pricing already did this. It can also hand a tea back to the shop rate, which is what clearing it means.

Say something like

“Add the 2019 Bulang, 400g, cost 1,200 yuan, and this one shipped at 110 a kilo.”

“The Bulang actually shipped at 95.”

“Put the Bulang back on the shop freight rate.”

The rate you give is in that tea's own buying currency, so a tea bought in yuan takes the yuan figure. A zero means that tea genuinely ships free, and leaving it out is not a zero.
