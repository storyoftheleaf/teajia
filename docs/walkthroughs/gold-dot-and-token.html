Teajia Two jobs About 12 minutes in total

The gold dot and the token

Both are yours because neither can be done from a session that cannot reach your shop. The first is a look. The second is what stops the second one being true.

Job one

Check the Y562 is following the shop's freight rate

Two minutes. Easier on a laptop than a phone, for one reason in step 3.

  1. Open your stock list

    It is at /admin/stock. The old /admin/inventory address still works, it just forwards here.

    Open the stock list

  2. Find the tea

    Type Y562 into the search box at the top, the one that says “Search tea or source”. If nothing comes back, try 1993, and that on its own tells us something: it would mean the tea is filed under a name with no Y562 in it, which is one of the two reasons the migration needed two clauses.

  3. Look at the “Ship $/kg” column

    On a laptop it is between Cost/g and Retail. On a phone you will have to swipe the table sideways, because that column is not one of the first ones on the mobile layout. That is the only reason this is easier on a laptop.

    The number is not the answer. A tea pinned to 85 and a tea following a shop rate of 85 print exactly the same figure. That is the whole reason the dot exists.

  4. Look for a small gold dot just after the number

    This is the entire test. Two cells, side by side, as they appear in that column:

    Following the shop rate, which is what you want

    11.89

    Pinned to this tea, which means the migration did not reach it

    11.89 •

    If you would rather read it than squint at it: hover the cell on a laptop, or press and hold it on a phone, and it says either “Following the shop rate” or “Pinned to this tea. Clear it in the edit panel to follow the shop rate.”

  5. Then one of two things

    No dot

    Done. The tea follows 85 ¥/kg and keeps following it when you renegotiate. Nothing else to do; go to job two.

    There is a dot

    Click the tea's name to open the edit panel, clear the Ship $/kg field so it is empty, and save. That now writes “follow the shop” rather than “ships free”, which is exactly the bug that was fixed yesterday afternoon. The dot should disappear. Tell me either way.

Job two

Connect a session to the shop, once, permanently

Ten minutes. Two halves, and it fails quietly if you do only one.

  1. Open the token page

    It is at /admin/mcp-tokens and the heading reads Voice & agent access. Owner only, so sign in as yourself.

    Open the token page

  2. Click “Mint a new token” and give it a label

    The label is only for you, so you can tell later which token is which and revoke the right one. Something like Claude on the web. It will not let you continue without one.

  3. Tick catalog:write. This is the step people get wrong

    You will see three groups of checkboxes. The read ones and the two operator ones are already ticked. The three under Write: Owner are not.

    Take the defaults and the currency backlog will not work. Reading the backlog needs inventory:read, which is on. But actually *fixing* a vendor's currency needs catalog:write, which is off. You would get a list you cannot act on, and the refusal would look like a broken tool rather than a missing tick.

    Tick catalog:write. The other two owner scopes are for customer records and account settings; leave them off unless you want them, they are easy to grant later with a new token.

  4. Copy the token. You get one look at it

    It is shown once and never again, because only its fingerprint is stored. If you lose it, nothing is broken, you just mint another and revoke this one.

    Do not paste it into a chat with me. Not this one, not any session. It goes straight into the settings field in the next step and nowhere else.

  5. Put it in the environment, as TEAJIA_MCP_TOKEN

    Go to claude.ai/code, open the settings for the environment these Teajia sessions run in, and add an environment variable named exactly TEAJIA_MCP_TOKEN with the token as its value.

    Open claude.ai/code

  6. In the same settings, allow api.teajia.com on the network

    This is the half that gets forgotten. The sandbox blocks outbound traffic to anything not on its allowlist, and your API is not on it by default.

    Without this the token looks broken and is not. The connection is refused at the proxy before it ever reaches your shop, so what comes back is a routing failure, not an authentication one. That reads exactly like a bad token, and you would spend the afternoon re-minting a token that was fine all along.

  7. Start a new session

    Environment settings are read when a session boots, so this one will not pick them up. Open a fresh session on the Teajia repo.

  8. Test it with a question, not a change

    Say, in the new session:

    Ask this

    “Which teas have a cost currency nobody ever stated?”

    It answers

    You get your vendors, with a count of unanswered teas under each and what any intake receipt recorded. You are connected. From there it is one sentence per vendor.

    It cannot reach the shop

    Almost always the allowlist rather than the token. Tell me what it said and I can tell which of the two halves is missing from the shape of the error.

Once job two is done, the currency backlog becomes a conversation instead of a spreadsheet: you say “everything from Yunnan Co was paid in yuan”, it shows you how many teas move and what their prices become, and you say yes. The other thirty commands come with it.
