# Members and Access Guide

Members belong to one account. Australia members operate only inside Australia unless they are also invited to another account.

## Roles

### Owner

Owners have all capabilities on their account. Use this for the person legally or operationally responsible for the store.

### Staff

Staff receive capability bundles. Start narrow, then expand when needed.

### Viewer

Viewers have no operational bundles. Use this for observation, training, or temporary review.

## Presets

### Sales

For people handling customers and orders.

Bundles: Sell.

### Inventory

For people entering products, correcting stock, and receiving stock.

Bundles: Catalog, Stock.

### Events

For hosts and people managing gatherings.

Bundles: Gather.

### Content

For people curating collections or editorial publishing.

Bundles: Publish.

### Manager

For daily store operators who should not grant access.

Bundles: Catalog, Stock, Gather, Sell, Publish.

### Access manager

For someone trusted to run the account with the owner.

Bundles: Catalog, Stock, Gather, Sell, Publish, Members.

### Viewer

For observation and training.

Bundles: none.

## Access rules

- Do not grant Members unless the person should invite or remove people.
- Do not grant Stock unless the person is allowed to change physical inventory.
- Do not grant Sell unless the person can work with customers, pricing, or orders.
- Review access after launch and after any staff change.

## Invite flow

1. Add member email.
2. Choose a preset.
3. Send invite.
4. If email delivery is not configured, copy the invite link.
5. The member opens the link, sets a password, and signs in.
6. Pending status clears after they accept.
