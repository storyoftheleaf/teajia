# Tea wisdom base: coverage audit against products/

Generated by `scripts/audit-wisdom-coverage.mjs`, which reads the real
`matchTeaVariety`, `matchCultivar`, `findRegion`, `normalizeTeaType` and
`normalizeTeaForm` from the app (bundled through esbuild, the same pattern
`scripts/export-wisdom-dataset.mjs` uses), so nothing here is a
reimplementation that could drift from what the app actually resolves.

Run it yourself: `node scripts/audit-wisdom-coverage.mjs` (add `--json` for
machine-readable output).

## Type and form vocabulary: what's in use vs. what's canonical

All 9 distinct `type` values in use (`Sheng` 43, `Oolong` 42, `Shou` 38,
`Dark` 24, `Red` 20, `White` 12, `Green` 6, `Teaware` 117, `Misc` 1) are
already covered: 7 by `TEA_TYPES`, 2 (`Teaware`, `Misc`) by `NON_TEA_TYPES`.
No gap.

Forms are a real, if small, gap. 9 distinct values in use:

| Form value | Count | Resolves to | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Loose Leaf | 92 | `Loose` | fine |
| Cake | 54 | `Cake` | fine |
| **Rolled** | **23** | **nothing** | every rolled oolong in the collection (23 products) carries a form the vocabulary can't normalize |
| Brick | 5 | `Brick` | fine |
| **Other** | 4 | **nothing** | generic placeholder on 4 products (aged sour citrus tea, mushroom-shaped puerh, yaobao buds, xiao qing gan), not one real form, four different unclassified shapes |
| Tuo | 4 | `Tuo` | fine |
| Ball | 2 | `Ball` | fine |
| **Basket** | **1** | **nothing** | the aged Liu An (老六安), a real and historically significant loose-tea packing format for Anhui dark tea |
| Bag | 1 | `Bag` | fine |

`Rolled` is the highest-value fix here: it is the standard shape for rolled
(ball-style) Taiwanese oolong short of a fully compressed `Ball`, and 23
products carry it. `Basket` is a real, distinct storage/packing form (loose
tea aged in woven baskets, historically how Liu An and some Liu Bao were
sold) and is worth its own vocabulary word rather than being folded into
`Loose`. `Other` is not a form gap so much as 4 products that were never
classified; each would need its own real form on inspection, not a
catch-all word added to the vocabulary.

I did not touch `src/wisdom/vocabulary.ts` (outside my file ownership for
this task); this is a recommendation, not a change.

### Why tea-name coverage didn't move, and what the 110 unresolved names actually are

Almost none of the 110 unresolved names are missing *places* (which this
pass could fix); they are missing or absent *identities*. Grouped honestly:

**~55-60 are Adrian's own naming for a specific lot, not a tea style or
cultivar name, and will never resolve no matter how big the wisdom base
gets:** `Courage`, `Passion`, `Mercy`, `Wisdom`, `Inspiration`, `Universe`,
`Galaxy`, `True Love`, `Miracle Fate`, `New Rainbow` / `Old Rainbow`, colour-
and grade-descriptive labels like `Dark Green Tie Bing`, `Light Green Tie
Bing`, `Yellow Label Tie Bing`, `Purple Bud Sheng` (×3), and bare
category-plus-year names like `Sheng Puerh 1980/1990`, `Shou Puerh 1990`,
`Hong Cha 1979`, `Yunnan Hong Cha` (literally just "red tea"). This is not a
gap in the wisdom base; it is the correct outcome of `matchCultivar`/
`matchTeaVariety` returning null rather than guessing on a name that isn't a
recognized tea identity in the first place.

**~10-15 are real historic puerh brands, factory recipes, or classification
marks that the wisdom base has no entity type for at all** (it models
varieties, cultivars, and regions, not brands or historic grading systems):
`7572 Menghai` (the famous CNNP/Menghai factory recipe number), `Tongqinghao
1980` (同慶號, one of the oldest recorded puerh trademarks), `Xinghai Aged
Sheng` (兴海, a named modern factory), `Zhong Cha Pai Yuan` (中茶牌, the
China Tea Corporation brand mark), `Red Seal Camphor Puerh` / `Blue Seal
Green Label` (紅印/藍印, the 1950s-60s colour-seal vintage classification
system collectors use to date puerh), `Golden Bull Hengfengyuan`, `Qiaorui
Gong Jin`, `Menghai Jia Ji Tuo`. These would need a new holding
("brand"/"historic mark"), not a fix to the existing three.

**~10 are genuinely missing tea styles or named entities that belong in
`src/data/teaVarieties.ts` or `src/wisdom/cultivars.ts` as real content
gaps:**
- `Xiao Qing Gan` (小青柑, mandarin-peel puerh), a well-known, widely sold
  style, absent from the variety list entirely.
- `GABA Oolong` (3 products: `EU GABA Oolong`, `Organic GABA Oolong`, `GABA
  Organic High Mountain`), a real, named processing style (anaerobic
  fermentation for gamma-aminobutyric acid), not currently in the vocabulary
  or variety list.
- `Ruicaokui` (瑞草魁), a historic, named Anhui green tea, missing from the
  Green section of `teaVarieties.ts` even though its Anhui siblings (Huang
  Shan Mao Feng, Tai Ping Hou Kui, Liu An Gua Pian) are present.
- `Bai Liang` / `Hua Juan` (花卷/百两, the "hundred-tael" rolled bamboo-wrapped
  Hunan dark tea), a distinct, famous format, missing.
- Aged `Liu An` (老六安, the Anhui dark basket tea) is easy to confuse with
  `Liu An Gua Pian` (an unrelated Anhui **green** tea already in the list);
  the dark-tea Liu An is a separate, real gap.
- `Ya'an Kang Brick` (雅安康磚), the Sichuan brick tea historically pressed
  for the Tibetan market, missing.
- `Anhua Huangye` / `Anhua Yi Ji` (荒野/一级, "wild-grown" and "grade one"
  Anhua heicha classifications), real grade terms used across multiple
  products, not represented.
- `Shuishalian Yuchi Red` (水沙連魚池紅茶), a real, named Taiwanese black
  tea from the Sun Moon Lake/Yuchi area, missing.

## Prioritised list: what would most improve coverage next

1. **Add a "historic brand / factory recipe / classification mark" holding.**
   This is the single biggest structural gap. ~10-15 of the 110 unresolved
   names are real, well-documented entities (7572, Tong Qing Hao, Red
   Seal/Green Seal, Zhongcha) that the wisdom base currently has nowhere to
   put, because it only models the plant (cultivar), the style (variety),
   and the place (region), not the brand or the vintage classification a
   collector actually uses to identify aged puerh. This would resolve real
   content, not naming artifacts.
2. **Add the ~10 missing named styles above to `teaVarieties.ts`** (Xiao
   Qing Gan, GABA Oolong, Ruicaokui, Hua Juan/Bai Liang, dark-tea Liu An,
   Kang Brick, the two Anhua grade terms, Shuishalian Yuchi Red). This is
   the highest-leverage, lowest-effort fix: each is a well-documented,
   real tea style, and adding them would resolve roughly 10-12 products
   immediately, more than doubling the marginal gain of this whole pass.
3. **Add `Rolled` and `Basket` to `TEA_FORMS` in `src/wisdom/vocabulary.ts`.**
   `Rolled` alone covers 23 products (all the collection's ball-rolled
   Taiwanese oolongs), the single largest resolvable gap of any kind found
   in this audit. `Basket` covers 1 but is a real, distinct, historically
   important packing format. Both are outside my file ownership for this
   task (vocabulary.ts is a hand-written module, not generated), so I'm
   flagging rather than changing it.
