# Write the brewing guides and build the pages they power

A set of QR sticker cards is already built. Each card links to a brewing guide page that does not exist yet. Two pieces of work close the loop: writing the guides, then building the pages.

## Step 1: Write roughly 20 brewing guide profiles

One profile per tea type or style, covering the full catalog. Styles to cover include: Gongfu Oolong, Grandpa-style Green, White, Raw / Ripe / Aged Puerh, Sheng, High-mountain Oolong, Roasted Oolong, Black gongfu, Black western, Yellow, Liu Bao, and the rest.

Each profile holds: water temperature, steep time in seconds, leaf-to-water ratio, vessel type, and infusion count.

Adrian provides or approves these. They can be drafted in conversation, then loaded.

## Step 2: Build the brewing guide pages

The `/learn/brew/:teaType` route and page do not exist yet, even though the brewing QR card component already links to them. Build the pages once the profiles above are written. They power both the QR sticker cards and the on-site brewing guides.
