# Host credit and personal assistants

A qualifying order can earn tea credit when the shop fills it, and spending requires shop approval. Keep one entry for each earn, spend or reversal and show the host their own balance. The host’s assistant can reach only that person’s host operations. Recheck authority on every confirmed write and on revocation.
