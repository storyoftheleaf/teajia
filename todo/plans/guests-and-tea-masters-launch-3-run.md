# Hosting, reach and private sits

A private historical sit keeps its owner and audience. Hosted sessions have an explicit host, an admission policy and a separately controlled public reach. A host may choose a structured tasting. A guest can leave the waiting list. Sharing a past sit or naming a co-pourer preserves the audience promised to each participant.
