# Invitations, private directions and host records

A host can invite someone by link, name the teas and send a private arrival walkthrough. Scheduled private directions are for approved guests. An operator may grant or withdraw hosting authority. A former host still owns their personal record. The membership or approval for one shop cannot open another shop’s guests, media or ledger.
