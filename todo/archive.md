## Orders and payments

- [x] Close the loop from an order request to being paid — done 2026-08-31, live at teajia.com. An order request now reaches Adrian by email instead of waiting to be noticed, becomes a priced invoice in one click instead of being retyped, and carries a pay link resolved to the right tea master with the amount and a reference to quote. Payments got their own records, so a customer can report a transfer and Adrian confirms it against his bank: a report is never money, part payments work, and the pay link asks for the balance outstanding rather than the full amount again. What the customer sees on their tracking page is now derived from the order itself instead of a status kept by hand, which was generating the "where is my tea" messages the page exists to answer. What needs Adrian gathers into one list in Your Table, oldest waiter first, and six voice commands reach all of it.
