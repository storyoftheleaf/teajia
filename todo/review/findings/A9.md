## What holds

- Two simultaneous requests for one modern seat produce one 201, one 409 and one confirmed guest.
- Two queue advances for one seat offer it to the first guest once. A claim exactly at its SQLite deadline is refused.
- Lowering capacity below confirmed seats is refused. Changing request to link leaves existing requests pending. An open door still needs the host’s yes.
- Cancelling between the confirmation read and write prevents confirmation. Draft, registration-closed, cancelled, completed and archived tables issue no new seat.
- Giving up a seat twice makes one offer. The next guest-page read omits the directions, and the mounted page removes its stored way and photo cache.
- Across midnight in Makassar, directions end at the stated instant. An unstated end defaults to four hours. A claim after the session end is refused even while its offer is live.
- With Launch 3 open, stopping a host closes new bookings but preserves the confirmed guest’s ticket, directions and public table link. Withdrawal of approval changes link bookings to requests.
- Deleting a guest removes their guest book entry and Tea Table tasting. The failed host deletion rolls back and the earned credit remains intact.

## Conflicts for Opus

- **Future tables when hosting stops or approval is withdrawn.** Launch 3 keeps confirmed guests’ directions and the public link. With only Launch 2 hosting enabled, withdrawing approval closes the public link and removes the directions from a still-confirmed ticket. Choose either to honor existing confirmed seats with directions until the end, or to close those tables and tell their guests that the booking has ended. A9-10 covers people still waiting for an answer.
- **Pending requests of stopped hosts.** Close the request and explain that the table cannot confirm it, or keep it paused and let the guest withdraw. Both choices can keep the moderation reason private.
- **Guest deletion.** Release future bookings and advance each queue, or let the guest explicitly retain selected bookings with their private link. State the outcome before they delete.
- **Host deletion.** Require the host to transfer or cancel future tables before deletion, or close the tables and circles as part of deletion. Both options need to preserve the credit ledger and specify who handles outstanding credit.
