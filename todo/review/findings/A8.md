## What holds

- Fill, Pending and Fill with unchanged lines produces exactly one award. Two simultaneous Fill requests also earn once.
- A paid Fill followed by repeated Void leaves one matching reversal. An unpaid Fill or a sale without a referring source earns nothing.
- A direct edit of a Filled order's lines or prices is refused. The reopening case is A8-2.
- Two orders competing for $10 reserve a total of $10. A browser-supplied $900 request still holds only the server-calculated $10.
- Approval twice spends once. Concurrent approval and decline produce one winning decision. A staff seller cannot approve a spend.
- Closing credit with a waiting request blocks both decisions. Reopening allows the owner to approve the existing request once.
- A linked host buying through their own thank-you earns nothing. A guest hosting a different session still earns credit only for the referring host.
- A Yuan display currency leaves the stored USD basis intact. Missing the catalogue currency rate refuses checkout before an invoice is created.
- A stored zero share earns zero. The migrated ledger refuses both update and delete. A historical award dated 2020 is still available to spend in full.
- Voiding the redemption order after approval currently leaves the full spend posted. The host gets no credit back.
- Stopping hosting leaves the old balance available. Orders with that host's recorded referral can still earn after the stop. The host can still request a spend.

## Conflicts for Opus

- **Self-orders.** Today a linked self-order earns zero. Question for Adrian: Keep the exclusion on one's own thank-you, or award the same share as for any other buyer?
- **Void after spending.** Today voiding the redemption order leaves the credit spent. Question for Adrian: Restore the approved credit once when its order is voided, or require a separate owner decision to return it?
- **Orders filled while closed.** Choose prospective earning or an explicit catch-up for all qualifying earlier Fills. An unrelated invoice edit currently decides the outcome (A8-5).
- **Stopped hosts.** Question for Adrian: Should existing referrals keep earning after a stop, or should the stop freeze new earnings while preserving the existing balance?
- **Who can verify a Fill.** Count an authorized seller's Fill or require the shop owner's verification for credit (A8-6). Keep fulfilment permissions separate from spend approval.
- **Debt after an earning is voided.** Preserve a signed ledger with host debt or have the shop cover the uncovered reversal (A8-7). The existing test named "repays debt from later earnings before making credit available to spend" documents the current choice.
- **Shared invoice arithmetic.** Post an invoice settlement explicitly identified as tea credit, or teach the shared invoice calculation to read approved credit as its own form of settlement (A8-1). Both must give the same amount due on every payment surface.
- **Replay identity.** Accept a scoped Idempotency-Key header or a persisted client request ID in the use payload. Keep the decision attached to the original request (A8-8).
- **Owner detail layout.** Reuse the decision controls on the host detail or give the detail a direct action that opens the confirmation on the matching request (A8-10).
- **Replaced sale lines.** Preserve an invoice-level award across later edits, or explicitly reverse and recompute the award as one correction (A8-2). Saving equivalent lines must leave the same net credit.
- **Price changes during a hold.** Release a stale hold when the order changes, or keep it pending and require the owner to review the revised eligible amount before approval (A8-3).
- **Void while closed.** Allow reversal of previously posted awards during closure, or reconcile those awards before the spend routes reopen (A8-4).
- **Void with a pending request.** Release the hold in the Void transaction, or make the spendable-balance calculation ignore holds on void orders and retire those requests during reconciliation (A8-9).
