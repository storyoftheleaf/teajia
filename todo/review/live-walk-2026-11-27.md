# Live walk after every switch went on (2026-11-27)

Walked https://www.teajia.com after PR 606 went live (worker 1f30859, version 0a12cbd2), with Adrian as the first and only approved host. Read only: nothing was created, changed or removed on the live site.

## How it was walked

- **Visitor:** a crawl after `tests/review/switch-crawl.spec.ts`, signed out, at 390 and 1280 wide. It started from the bar words, Home, Your Table, the tea table, tea houses, sessions, people, spaces, Wisdom, the MCP page, /account/host, Daniel's link and an unknown address, then followed every link found, 60 pages a size (120 in all). Each page was failed on the not-found page, an empty main area, a spinner still turning, side scroll, a console or page error, an API answer of 400 or more, or any 500.
- **Owner and host:** through the Teajia connector, read only (`whats_waiting`, `list_site_problems`, `list_events`, `list_venues`, `list_my_tables`), plus the public answers a guest's phone reads (`/api/events/:slug/public`, `/api/tea-table`).
- **Guest:** the public session page as a guest in another time zone (America/New_York). No guest account exists on the live site, and making one would be a real account, so the signed-in guest pages (`/m/:token`, `/after-sit`) were not walked.

## What holds

- Every page the visitor reaches loads, with no 500, no console error, no side scroll and no stuck spinner. The only not-found page is the made-up address.
- The bar reads read, tea table, tea house, shop. The Tea Table, Tea Houses, Find a tea house, People, Sessions, Your Table and the MCP page all open.
- /account/host sends a signed-out visitor to sign in. Daniel's link loads.
- A session shows its time in its own zone and names it ("2:00 PM, Taipei time" for a viewer in New York).
- A public session lists no guest names (none has opted in); the count is shown, never a full name.
- PR 614's four fixes are live: Lessons' words load (`/api/story-content/craft` 200), /account/host's tab says "Page not found", Home's sign-up block is for signed-out people only, and Manage rooms sit under Your Table's tiles.
