# The switch map

Done by Opus 5.5 (cloud), in place of Astra (A10). Read from `build/review` on 2026-11-26. Each switch is a constant in `src/lib/`, read by the site as `VITE_<NAME>` and by the worker as `<NAME>`. HOME_BAR is read by the site only. FIRST_TABLE (Launch 1) is open on the live site and is left out of the table.

"Needs first" means the code checks the other switch too, or the switch's doors lead somewhere only the other switch opens. "Gated in" names where the closed answer comes from: `launch4Closed.ts` (the router leaves the route out, so it answers exactly as an unknown address) or the handler's own check.

## The recommended opening order

The draft order in `tests/review/switch-sets.ts` is confirmed as it is. Every "needs first" in the table above falls earlier in it. Two conditions are added: HOST_CREDIT waits until A10-1 is fixed, and every step follows the deploy rule below.

FEW_HOSTS, ANYONE_HOSTS, FAVOURITES, YOUR_TABLE_PAGE, YOUR_TABLE_PLACE, TEA_TABLE, TOP_TEAS, HOST_ARTICLES, TEA_HOUSES, HOME_BAR, HOST_CREDIT, CIRCLES, HOST_TOKENS, TABLE_CARD, KNOW_THIS_TEA.

One rule for every step: **open the worker first and the site second; close the site first and the worker second.** The half-open walk found every site-open, worker-closed door shows an error sentence, and every site-closed, worker-open door is quiet (`tests/review/half-open.spec.ts`, findings A10-3). Until the shared "not here yet" helper lands, that order keeps the minutes between the two deploys quiet.
