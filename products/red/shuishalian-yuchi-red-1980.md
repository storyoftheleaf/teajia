A 1980 red tea from Shuishalian, sitting between the 1979 and 1981 vintages in the collection.

Three consecutive years of Shuishalian Yuchi red from Master Bo — 1979, 1980, and 1981. The 1980 and 1981 are priced equally at 6,000 NT, double the 1979. Whether the price reflects a different picking standard, a different cultivar selection, or simply a recognized quality jump between the two price tiers, having all three allows direct comparison of year-to-year variation from the same area.

## Terroir

Shuishalian, Yuchi Township, Nantou County. Sun Moon Lake basin microclimate. The same terroir as the 1979 and 1981 — warm, humid, fog-wrapped, with volcanic-origin soil. At forty-five years, terroir differences between single years are subtle but real.

## Processing

Full-oxidation red tea from the Sun Moon Lake basin, likely Assam or local large-leaf cultivar. Orthodox processing. Forty-five years of aging have developed the full Yuchi aged red character — the transformation from bright malt to dark, woody, honeyed depth.

## Mood

middle vintage

## Experience

A bridge between the lighter 1979 and the fuller 1981. The longan and malt are present, the wood has developed, and there is a cocoa note that adds richness. Drinking all three vintages in sequence would reveal the subtleties of how a single year's difference manifests across four decades of aging.
