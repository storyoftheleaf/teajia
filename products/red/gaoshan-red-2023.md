A high-mountain black tea from Taiwan.

Taiwanese high-mountain red tea represents a relatively new tradition that has quickly earned respect for producing some of the most aromatic and elegant black teas in the world.

## Terroir

High-mountain regions of Taiwan. The essential factor is elevation: above 1,000 meters, where temperature and humidity conditions produce leaf with higher amino acid content and more developed aromatics.

## Processing

Full oxidation of high-altitude Taiwanese cultivar. The characteristic elegance of this style comes from controlled oxidation that stops short of the heavy, malty profile of lower-elevation black teas.

## Mood

quiet brightness

## Experience

A clean, sweet red tea with a lightness that makes it easy to drink all day. The honey note is gentler than the named version, with more of a roasted sweetness underneath. Approachable and reliable.
