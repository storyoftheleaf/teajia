Zhengshan Xiaozhong is the original name for what the West calls Lapsang Souchong.

'Zhengshan' means 'true mountain,' distinguishing authentic Tongmu village production from imitations. This is a modern example of the world's first black tea, produced in the same village where the category was born over 400 years ago.

## Terroir

Tongmu village in the Wuyi Mountains nature reserve, Fujian. The cold, wet climate and the pine-smoke environment are inseparable from the tea's identity.

## Processing

Traditional Tongmu processing: withering and drying over pine wood fires. Modern Zhengshan Xiaozhong tends toward lighter smoke than historical versions, letting the cultivar's natural sweetness come forward.

## Mood

origin warmth

## Experience

The pine smoke is present but gentle, more backdrop than feature. What leads is a sweet, fruity character, longan and sweet potato, that shows why this tea defined an entire category. Warming without heaviness.
