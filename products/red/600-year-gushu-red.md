Ancient-tree red tea from 600-year-old trees in Yunnan.

Trees of this age are exceedingly rare and produce leaf with a depth and complexity that plantation tea cannot approach. The root systems of these ancient trees penetrate deep into old-growth forest soil, drawing minerals and compounds that younger trees simply cannot access.

## Terroir

Yunnan's old-growth tea forests. Trees of this age are found in protected forest areas, often in Xishuangbanna or Lincang prefectures. They grow in mixed forest canopy, their character inseparable from the ecosystem around them.

## Processing

Full oxidation of ancient arbor leaf from 600-year-old trees. The processing must be gentle to respect the quality of this exceptional raw material. Likely sun-dried or low-temperature dried to preserve the leaf's inherent complexity.

## Mood

profound respect

## Experience

Thick, sweet, and layered in ways that reveal the age of the tree. The body is substantial and the finish seems to go on indefinitely. This is not everyday drinking. This is a tea you share with someone who understands what 600 years means.
