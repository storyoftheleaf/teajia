Zhuangyuan Hong (Scholar Champion Red) is named for the highest rank in China's imperial examination system.

In Wuyi tea culture, the Zhuangyuan designation implies the finest quality. This is a premium Wuyi red tea, processed with the same care and attention to terroir that defines the region's oolongs.

## Terroir

Wuyi Mountains, Fujian province. The same rocky, mineral-dense environment that produces legendary oolongs gives Wuyi red teas a backbone and complexity that lowland red teas cannot match.

## Processing

Wuyi red tea processing: full oxidation of local cultivar in the strip-style. Wuyi red teas benefit from the same mineral-rich terroir as the oolongs, producing a depth and complexity that distinguishes them from other Chinese red teas.

highest regard

## Experience

Rich and layered with a mineral depth underneath the fruit and honey. The Wuyi terroir is unmistakable even in a red tea. Cocoa and dried apricot with a finish that stays. A serious red tea for serious drinking.
