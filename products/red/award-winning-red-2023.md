A 2023 competition-grade Taiwanese red tea.

Tea competitions in Taiwan are rigorous, with blind judging by panels of experienced assessors. An award-winning designation means this tea was selected from hundreds or thousands of entries for exceptional quality in processing, aroma, and flavor.

## Terroir

Taiwan. Competition teas may come from various regions, with the specific origin varying by competition and category. The award reflects processing skill as much as terroir.

## Processing

Competition-grade processing with meticulous attention to every stage: withering, rolling, oxidation, and drying. Award-winning teas represent the maker's best work, where small adjustments in timing and temperature create measurable differences.

earned distinction

## Experience

Clean, precise, and balanced. Nothing out of place. The honey and muscatel are vivid and the finish is unusually long. This is what Taiwanese red tea sounds like when everything goes right.
