A 1979 red tea from the Shuishalian area near Sun Moon Lake.

Shuishalian (水沙蓮) is a locality within the Sun Moon Lake basin in Yuchi. The name carries Thao indigenous heritage — this is traditional Thao territory, and the area around the lake was cultivated long before the Japanese established tea research stations there. A 1979 red tea from this specific area represents Yuchi production at a confident, mature stage.

## Terroir

Shuishalian, Yuchi Township, Nantou County. Within the broader Sun Moon Lake basin, Shuishalian sits at the interface of lake and mountain. The microclimate here benefits from both the lake's thermal regulation and the mountain slopes' drainage and fog. The soil carries volcanic and alluvial characteristics.

## Processing

Full-oxidation red tea, likely from Assam or a local large-leaf cultivar suited to the Yuchi basin. Orthodox processing with careful withering and oxidation. Over four decades of aging have transformed the bright malty character of young Yuchi red into something deeper and more honeyed.

## Experience

Smooth and round with a dried fruit sweetness and just enough malt to remember where it came from. The wood note is gentle, the finish is clean. A well-aged Taiwanese red that has found its equilibrium.
