A 1979 red tea from a personal collection. The name 'The Classic' was earned, not given at birth.

Over four decades, this simple red tea has developed into something that justifies the title. Aged red tea is uncommon because the category is typically consumed fresh.

A red tea that has thrived for forty-five years is genuinely exceptional.

## Terroir

Unrecorded. The 1979 vintage and personal collection status suggest Taiwan or mainland China origin. The tea has aged past the point where origin matters more than what is in the cup.

## Processing

Full oxidation processing from the late 1970s. The specific method is unknown but the era suggests traditional Chinese red tea production. Forty-five years of natural aging have transformed the original character completely, developing a depth that fresh red tea cannot achieve.

## Mood

classic grace

## Experience

The name is the review. It is smooth, warm, sweet, and balanced in a way that only decades can produce. There is nothing out of place and nothing missing. This is what red tea sounds like when it has had a lifetime to settle into itself.
