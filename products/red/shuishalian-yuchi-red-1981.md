A 1981 red tea from Shuishalian, priced at double the 1979 vintage from the same area.

The price difference suggests either a superior picking standard, a different cultivar, or a recognized quality distinction that the seller has maintained. At over forty years old, this Shuishalian red has entered the territory where Taiwanese red teas develop a character that has no equivalent in any other tradition.

## Terroir

Shuishalian, Yuchi Township, Nantou County. The Sun Moon Lake basin microclimate — warm, humid, fog-wrapped. The same terroir as the 1979 version, but the tea itself tells a different story at the higher price point.

## Processing

Full-oxidation red tea from the Sun Moon Lake basin. The higher cost relative to the 1979 may indicate a finer plucking standard or a select cultivar. Four decades of aging have developed the characteristic Yuchi red transformation — malt becoming wood, brightness becoming depth.

## Mood

deep lake

## Experience

Fuller and more resonant than the 1979. The longan note is pronounced, the malt has darkened into something closer to cocoa, and the wood note has real presence. The mineral finish is long. This is the more serious of the two Shuishalian vintages — a tea that asks you to pay attention.
