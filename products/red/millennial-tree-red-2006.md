A 2006 ancient-tree red tea pressed into cake form.

Pressing red tea into cakes is uncommon and suggests Yunnan origin, where the practice of compressing tea is deeply ingrained. The 'millennial tree' designation points to old-growth arbor tea trees, the same trees valued for pu-erh production, processed instead as red tea.

## Terroir

Yunnan province, likely from established old-growth tea forests. The 'millennial tree' designation is common for ancient arbor teas, typically trees over 100 years old growing in forest-garden environments at moderate to high elevation.

## Processing

Full oxidation of Yunnan large-leaf arbor tea, compressed into cake form. Ancient-tree red tea from Yunnan represents a relatively recent innovation, using the same exceptional raw material as pu-erh but with full oxidation. Nearly twenty years of aging have added complexity.

## Mood

deep sweetness

## Experience

Rich and full-bodied in a way that other red teas are not. The Yunnan large-leaf base gives it a thickness and depth that small-leaf red teas cannot achieve. Dark honey and rose with a malty warmth. A red tea that drinks like a meditation.
