Wuliang Mountain (无量山) sits in Jingdong County, Pu'er City, held between the Wuliang and Ailao ranges. The mountain is a living record of tea's evolution — wild types, natural hybrids, and old cultivars still standing side by side in the gardens.

The Yi people have tended these trees for centuries. By the Qing dynasty the Wuliang gardens covered more than 100,000 亩. This cake is ancient-tree leaf from those gardens, wo dui fermented and pressed. The old material gives a shou more depth than standard plantation ripe.

It drinks earthy and woodsy rather than heavy. Smooth body, a quiet mineral floor, a mellow finish that carries the faint dry-apricot sweetness the region is known for.
