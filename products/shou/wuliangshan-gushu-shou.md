Wuliang Mountain (无量山) is one of Yunnan's storied tea regions, lying in Jingdong County, Pu'er City — the county sits between the Wuliang and Ailao mountains.

The mountain is described as a natural museum of tea breed evolution: trees range from wild types through natural hybrids to modern group cultivars, each stage still present in the gardens. The **Yi people** have protected and cultivated these trees for centuries, and in the Qing dynasty the Wuliang Mountain gardens covered more than 100,000 亩.

## Terroir

Wuliang Mountain, Jingdong County, Pu'er Prefecture, Yunnan. Jingdong is bounded by the Wuliang and Ailao ranges. Ancient gardens and tea-horse roads across the county attest to its long tea history.

## Processing

Ancient-tree leaf from the Wuliang Mountain gardens, wo dui fermented and compressed into 357-gram cakes. The old-tree material gives a shou deeper body than standard plantation ripe pu-erh.

## Mood

quiet earth

## Experience

Region pu-erh from Wuliang is known for being light and woodsy rather than aggressive, ageing toward a dry apricot sweetness. As a ripened shou this translates into a deep, earthy, smooth profile with a mellow finish.
