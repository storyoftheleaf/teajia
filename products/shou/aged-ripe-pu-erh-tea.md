An aged loose ripe puerh, come to us from the Guangzhou tea trade. Dark in the cup, smooth and thick, with an earthy depth. An easy introduction to shou for a beginner.

## Terroir

Born in Yunnan, in the far southwest of China, from the large-leaf tea trees that grow across the province's warm subtropical hills.

## Processing

Ripe (shou) puerh. After picking and drying, the leaves are heaped in a warm damp pile so heat and moisture build and microbes slowly work through them. This wet-pile method was stabilized in Yunnan in the early 1970s. It shortens the long wait that raw puerh needs and gives a darker, smoother cup from the start. Then aged loose.

## Lore

Puerh is named for the old market town of Pu'er, the historical trading centre where Yunnan leaf gathered before moving out along the tea-horse roads.
