A 1998 shou pu-erh aged inside bamboo tubes.

Bamboo-tube tea (zhu tong cha) is a traditional preparation from Yunnan's Dai and Hani ethnic minorities, where tea is packed into fresh bamboo segments and roasted or dried. The bamboo imparts a distinctive fragrance that becomes part of the tea's identity over time.

## Terroir

Yunnan province. Bamboo-tube tea production is associated with the Dai, Hani, and other ethnic minorities of Xishuangbanna and the surrounding areas. The bamboo used is fresh-cut and green at the time of packing.

## Processing

Shou pu-erh packed into bamboo tubes, a traditional Yunnan minority technique. The bamboo creates a sealed aging environment while contributing its own aromatics to the tea. Twenty-five years of co-aging have unified the bamboo and tea characters.

## Mood

bamboo warmth

## Experience

The bamboo note is the first thing you notice and the last thing you forget. It weaves through the shou earthiness and adds a sweetness and freshness that standard shou does not have. A unique preparation worth experiencing at least once.
