Xiniu Tang (犀牛塘), meaning "Rhinoceros Pond," is a named micro-origin inside the greater Yiwu tea area of Xishuangbanna. Each of Yiwu's small places carries its own signature of soil, elevation, and microclimate.

Ancient-tree leaf from Xiniu Tang, wo dui fermented and pressed into cake. Old-tree material takes the shou process deeper than standard ripe.

The liquor is thick and honeyed, with the aged-earth character of a settled ripe. A cool camphor note runs through the middle. The finish is long and mineral, a sweetness that holds on the tongue between sips.
