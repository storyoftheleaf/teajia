Xiniu Tang (犀牛塘) means 'Rhinoceros Pond,' a poetic place name from the Yiwu tea region.

This gushu shou represents ancient-tree material from one of Yiwu's named micro-origins, fermented into ripe tea. Each named location within Yiwu carries its own terroir signature.

## Terroir

Xiniu Tang, within the greater Yiwu tea area of Xishuangbanna, Yunnan. Named micro-origins within Yiwu each carry slightly different soil, elevation, and microclimate conditions that create distinguishable terroir signatures.

## Processing

Ancient-tree leaf from the Xiniu Tang area of Yiwu, wo dui fermented and compressed into cake form. The high-quality old-tree material creates a shou with more depth and complexity than standard production-grade ripe pu-erh.

## Mood

deep honey

## Experience

Thick and honeyed with the unmistakable sweetness of Yiwu old-tree material. The fermentation is clean and the camphor note adds dimension. Another example of what happens when exceptional raw material meets shou processing.
