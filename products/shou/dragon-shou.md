A large-format shou pu-erh cake with dragon imagery.

Dragon-themed pu-erh cakes are common in Chinese tea production, often created for festivals or commemorative purposes. The 1,300-gram size suggests this was produced as a display or gift piece rather than standard retail packaging.

## Terroir

Yunnan province. Large-format commemorative pu-erh is produced by various factories across the pu-erh region.

## Processing

Wo dui fermented shou pu-erh compressed into a large-format commemorative cake. The larger format ages differently than standard 357-gram cakes due to the greater ratio of interior to exterior leaf.

## Mood

generous comfort

## Experience

A big tea in every sense. The large format means you can break off pieces for years. The flavor is straightforward shou: dark, sweet, smooth, and comforting. Not complex but reliably satisfying.
