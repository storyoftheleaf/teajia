Yiwu is pu-erh's most legendary region. 'Monkey Black' likely refers to a specific garden or processing batch designation.

Ancient-tree shou from Yiwu represents a premium category: the world's most famous pu-erh origin combined with old-growth tree material, fermented into ripe tea. The 2015 pressing has had a decade of post-fermentation refinement.

## Terroir

Yiwu township, Mengla county, Xishuangbanna, Yunnan. Yiwu is home to some of the most revered old tea tree gardens in the world. The combination of tropical moisture, high elevation, and centuries of cultivation creates leaf with unmatched sweetness and complexity.

## Processing

Wo dui fermented ancient-tree shou from Yiwu area, pressed in 2015. Using identified Yiwu old-tree material for shou production is relatively uncommon and represents a significant investment in raw material quality.

## Mood

forest depth

## Experience

This is shou pu-erh with the ghost of sheng underneath. The Yiwu sweetness and the camphor note survive the fermentation, giving this ripe tea a complexity and depth that is rare in the category. Worth drinking slowly.
