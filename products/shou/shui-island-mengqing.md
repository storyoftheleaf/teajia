Shui Dao (水岛) refers to an area in Shuangjiang county, Lincang, Yunnan. Lincang has emerged as an important pu-erh origin alongside the more famous Xishuangbanna and Simao regions.

The Mengqing designation likely refers to the specific tea garden or processing facility. Lincang shou tends toward a lighter, sweeter profile than Menghai production.

## Terroir

Shuangjiang county, Lincang prefecture, Yunnan. This area is home to some of the oldest cultivated tea trees in the world. The climate is slightly cooler and drier than Xishuangbanna, producing leaf with a different mineral and sweetness profile.

## Processing

Wo dui fermented shou pu-erh from Lincang region. Lincang processing tends to produce a lighter, sweeter fermentation profile compared to the heavier, earthier character of Menghai-region shou.

## Mood

light ease

## Experience

Lighter and sweeter than you might expect from shou. The brown sugar note is genuine and the body is clean without the heaviness that some shou carries. An easy drinker for people who find Menghai-style shou too dense.
