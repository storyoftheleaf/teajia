A 1980 shou pu-erh, over four decades old. At this age, the distinction between shou and aged sheng begins to blur.

The manufactured fermentation has been so thoroughly absorbed by time that what remains feels less like process and more like geology. This tea predates the modern pu-erh market by decades.

## Terroir

Yunnan province. Shou production in 1980 was centered in a handful of state-run factories. The leaf would have come from established Yunnan tea gardens, processed in the still-experimental early years of wo dui fermentation.

## Processing

Wo dui fermented shou pu-erh from 1980, an era when shou processing was still relatively new (the technique was developed at Kunming Tea Factory in 1973). Early shou production varied widely in quality and approach. Forty-plus years have refined whatever the original processing contributed.

## Mood

deep contemplation

## Experience

Time has done most of the work. What remains is smooth beyond smooth, dark and sweet and utterly without edge. The dates and walnut notes are vivid but gentle. A tea that asks you to sit still and pay attention to what quiet tastes like.
