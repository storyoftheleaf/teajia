Puerh comes from Yunnan; this is the ripe style, wet-piled. Huangpian are the more mature leaves, pulled out during sorting and once considered lower-grade.

## Terroir

Yunnan province, southern China, the historical heart of puerh production.

## Processing

Wo dui fermented shou puerh made from huangpian, the older, more mature leaves set aside during sorting. Once discarded, the trade came around: the same leaves are now valued for a rounder, sweeter body. Expertly stored in Guangzhou.
