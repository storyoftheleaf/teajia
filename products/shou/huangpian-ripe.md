A ripe puerh (shou) made from huangpian, the older coarse leaves set aside in sorting.

## Lore

Huangpian (黄片) are the older, coarser leaves pulled out during sorting. For a long time they were treated as low grade and even discarded; the trade eventually came around to them for a rounder, sweeter, softer body than young leaf.

## Terroir

Puerh comes from Yunnan, a large mountainous province in southwest China. Its highlands have long produced the broad-leaf tea puerh is made from.

## Processing

Loose-leaf ripe puerh, called shou. Ripe puerh is made by wet piling the leaf, a step developed in the early 1970s where heat and moisture let microbes transform it, turning it dark and smooth so it drinks well now rather than in decades.
