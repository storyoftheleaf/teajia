Yiwu small-leaf (xiaoye) shou is unusual.

Most Yiwu production uses the large-leaf varietal that defines the region, but small-leaf trees grow scattered among them, producing a different character entirely. Processing small-leaf Yiwu as shou creates something lighter and more aromatic than the typical Yiwu profile.

## Terroir

Yiwu township, Xishuangbanna, Yunnan. Small-leaf tea trees in Yiwu are less common than the large-leaf varietal but produce a distinctive, lighter profile that some drinkers prefer for its refinement and approachability.

## Processing

Small-leaf varietal from the Yiwu region, wo dui fermented and pressed into cake. The small-leaf material produces a lighter, more aromatic fermentation than the dominant large-leaf variety. Decades of aging from the 1980s have added depth while preserving the delicate character.

## Mood

melodic sweetness

## Experience

Lighter than you expect from Yiwu shou. There is a melody to it, a sweetness that rises and falls across steepings rather than hitting one sustained note. The small-leaf character gives it a grace that large-leaf shou rarely achieves.
