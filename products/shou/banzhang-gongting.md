Banzhang village material processed as gongting (palace grade) shou and hand-rolled into dragon pearls.

This represents an intersection of three prestigious elements: Banzhang's famous terroir, the highest grade classification in shou pu-erh (gongting uses only the smallest, most tender buds), and the artisan craft of hand-rolling each pearl individually.

## Terroir

Banzhang village in Menghai county, Xishuangbanna, Yunnan. Lao Banzhang (Old Banzhang) is among the most famous and expensive tea-producing villages in China, known for powerful, full-bodied tea with distinctive bitter-sweet character and intense qi.

## Processing

Palace-grade shou pu-erh (finest bud material) from Banzhang village, wo dui fermented and then hand-rolled into individual dragon pearl shapes. The small bud material creates a smoother, more refined fermentation than larger leaf grades.

## Mood

concentrated elegance

## Experience

Each pearl brews rich and sweet with surprising density for such a small piece of tea. The Banzhang character shows through the fermentation: a strength and depth that separates it from generic shou. Unfurling each pearl is a small ceremony in itself.
