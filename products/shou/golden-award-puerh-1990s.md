A 1990s gold award shou pu-erh.

Competition recognition for shou is less common than for oolong or sheng, making this designation notable. The award signals exceptional fermentation quality, and twenty-five to thirty years of aging have refined the result further.

## Terroir

Yunnan province. Competition-grade shou comes from carefully controlled fermentation using quality leaf material. The award reflects processing excellence as much as raw material quality.

## Processing

Award-winning wo dui fermented shou pu-erh from the 1990s. Competition-grade shou requires exceptionally clean fermentation with no off-notes or mustiness. Decades of post-fermentation aging have mellowed and deepened the profile.

## Mood

quiet distinction

## Experience

Cleaner and more refined than typical shou of similar age. The award-winning fermentation shows in the absence of any roughness or off-notes. Smooth, sweet, and deeply satisfying. A shou that earned its recognition.
