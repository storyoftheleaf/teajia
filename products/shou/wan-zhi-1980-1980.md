A 1980 shou pu-erh carrying the name Wan Zhi (万枝), meaning 'ten thousand branches.

' The poetic name suggests either a specific production batch or a reference to the diversity of leaf material blended into this cake. Over four decades of aging on a shou from this early era of ripe production.

## Terroir

Yunnan province. Early shou production in 1980 drew from established state-managed gardens across the pu-erh regions. Blended material from multiple sources was standard practice.

## Processing

Wo dui fermented shou from 1980, an early period for this processing method. The 'ten thousand branches' name may indicate a blend from multiple garden sources, a common practice that creates complexity through diversity. Forty-plus years of post-fermentation aging.

## Mood

branching depth

## Experience

Rich and layered, like the name suggests. The flavors multiply across steepings: earth, then dates, then walnut, then a dark sweetness that keeps finding new expressions. Forty years have woven the blend into something unified but multifaceted.
