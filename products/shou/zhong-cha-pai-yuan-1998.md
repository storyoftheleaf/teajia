Zhong Cha Pai (中茶牌) is the iconic brand of the China National Tea Corporation, marked by the eight-pointed 'zhong' character surrounded by a red circle.

This 1998 pressing in the classic Yuan Cha (round tea) format carries one of the most recognized labels in pu-erh history. The red mark (hong yin) designation has been used across decades of production.

## Terroir

Yunnan province. Zhong Cha branded teas have been produced by various state-run and later privatized factories across the pu-erh regions. The brand represents institutional Chinese tea production at its most established.

## Processing

Wo dui fermented shou pu-erh bearing the Zhong Cha brand, compressed into standard 357-gram cake form. The Zhong Cha label has appeared on teas from multiple factories across China. Nearly thirty years of aging have brought this into comfortable maturity.

## Mood

trusted heritage

## Experience

There is a reliability to teas bearing this mark. It brews clean and dark, tastes of dates and aged earth, and finishes with a sweetness that lingers. Not the most complex shou in the collection, but one that carries history in every sip.
