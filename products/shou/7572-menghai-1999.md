The 7572 is Menghai Tea Factory's benchmark shou recipe. The number encodes its identity: 1975 formula, grade 7 leaf, factory number 2.

This 1999 pressing arrived without its original paper wrapper, a common occurrence for cakes that traveled through informal storage channels in Southeast Asia. The tea itself needs no introduction.

## Terroir

Menghai county in Xishuangbanna, Yunnan, sits in tropical lowland terrain surrounded by old-growth forest. The factory has drawn from gardens across the region since the 1940s, blending leaf from multiple elevations to achieve the consistent profile that made this recipe famous.

## Processing

Wo dui (wet pile) fermented shou pu-erh, compressed into standard 357-gram cake form. Twenty-five years of post-fermentation aging have smoothed the manufactured edges into something that tastes less like process and more like time.

## Mood

familiar comfort

## Experience

Dark and smooth with no rough edges. It fills the cup with warmth and the room with the scent of old wood. A tea that asks nothing of you. You drink it when you want to stop thinking about tea and just drink tea.
