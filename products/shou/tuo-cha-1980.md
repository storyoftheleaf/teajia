Tuo cha (沱茶) is one of the oldest compressed tea forms, shaped like a bird's nest or bowl.

The format originated for ease of transport along ancient trade routes. This shou tuo represents the most traditional and utilitarian form of ripe pu-erh: compressed for storage, fermented for daily drinking.

## Terroir

Yunnan province. Tuo cha production is centered in Xiaguan and Menghai, the two most prolific pu-erh production areas.

## Processing

Wo dui fermented shou pu-erh compressed into tuo form. The concave shape creates different aging dynamics than flat cakes, with the interior aging more slowly. The result is a range of flavors from surface to center.

## Mood

steady ground

## Experience

Straightforward and grounding. It does what shou tuo cha has done for generations: brew dark, taste clean, settle the body. No pretension. A tea for daily drinking that has earned its place through centuries of service.
