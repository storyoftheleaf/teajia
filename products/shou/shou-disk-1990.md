A 1990 shou pu-erh cake of standard 357-gram format.

Shou disks from this era were workhouse teas, pressed for daily consumption and regional trade rather than collection. Thirty-five years of aging have elevated what was once ordinary into something worth paying attention to.

## Terroir

Yunnan province. Standard shou production from the early 1990s typically drew from gardens across the pu-erh growing regions, blending for consistency rather than showcasing a single origin.

## Processing

Wo dui (wet pile) fermented shou pu-erh, compressed into standard disk form. Over three decades of post-fermentation aging have fully mellowed the production character into a smooth, integrated profile.

## Mood

quiet reliability

## Experience

Dark, smooth, and completely without pretension. Thirty-five years have erased whatever rough edges this cake once had. What remains is simple and satisfying: warmth, sweetness, and the kind of depth that only comes from time doing its slow work.
