A 1980 shou pressed into small, round cakes (xiao yuan bing). The diminutive format suggests these were made for individual servings or gift-giving.

Over four decades, these small cakes have developed a character disproportionate to their size. The name says everything about how this tea enters the room.

## Terroir

Yunnan province. Small-format shou production from this era was less common than standard cakes, suggesting a specific purpose or market for these pieces.

## Processing

Small-format shou pu-erh cakes from 1980. The smaller size means more surface area relative to volume, which can accelerate certain aspects of aging while creating a more uniform transformation throughout the cake.

## Mood

soft arrival

## Experience

It does not announce itself. It arrives softly, warm and sweet, and settles without asking permission. The body is light for its age, the sweetness is gentle, and there is a comfort in the cup that makes you reach for another pour without thinking about it.
