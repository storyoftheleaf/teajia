A 1990 shou pu-erh.

Over thirty years of aging in warm, humid climate creates a different transformation path than storage in Yunnan or Hong Kong. This style of storage tends toward a cleaner, less musty profile with a brightness that dry-stored teas develop.

## Terroir

Yunnan province for production. The dual terroir of Yunnan leaf and decades of subtropical storage creates a profile distinctly different from mainland-stored pu-erh of the same era.

## Processing

Wo dui fermented shou pu-erh, stored loose for over three decades. Subtropical climate provides consistent warmth and humidity that accelerates and refines the aging process without the mustiness that can develop in more extreme storage conditions.

## Mood

clean warmth

## Experience

Clear and refined after three decades. The earthiness is present but clean, without any musty or storage-off notes. The fruit sweetness is a signature of well-aged pu-erh stored in warm, humid conditions. Reliable and satisfying.
