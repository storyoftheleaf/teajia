A 2010 sheng from Elephant Village (象村), a named origin within Yunnan's pu-erh producing regions.

The village name evokes the wild elephants that still roam parts of Xishuangbanna. Fifteen years of aging have brought this into early maturity.

## Terroir

Elephant Village, Yunnan province. The name suggests a location within or near areas where wild Asian elephants still inhabit the forest, typically in Xishuangbanna or Pu-erh prefecture.

## Processing

Sun-dried Yunnan maocha from Elephant Village, stone-pressed into cake. Fifteen years is the early edge of interesting aging for sheng: the raw youth is fading but the deep aged character has not yet fully developed.

## Mood

quiet unfolding

## Experience

In transition. The astringency of youth is softening and sweetness is emerging, but the tea has not yet arrived at its destination. There are glimpses of camphor and mineral depth that suggest good things ahead. A tea to revisit in five years.
