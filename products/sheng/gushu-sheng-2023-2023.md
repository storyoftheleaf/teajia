A 2023 ancient-tree (gushu) sheng, the youngest raw pu-erh in the collection.

Fresh gushu sheng is where the pu-erh journey begins: bright, astringent, full of potential, and years away from becoming what it will eventually be. The Spring designation marks this as first-flush material, the most prized picking of the year.

## Terroir

Yunnan province. Gushu (ancient tree) sheng uses leaf from old-growth trees, typically over 100 years old. Spring picking captures the first flush after winter dormancy, when the leaf is most concentrated in amino acids, aromatics, and flavor compounds.

## Processing

Fresh spring-harvest gushu maocha, sun-dried and stone-pressed into 357-gram cake form. The ancient-tree material provides more complexity and body than plantation leaf even in its youth. This tea is designed to age for decades.

## Mood

fresh promise

## Experience

Bright and alive in the cup with a vegetal freshness and a tingling astringency that announces its youth. The sweetness is there but raw, not yet refined by time. This is a tea to drink now for what it is, and to store for what it will become.
