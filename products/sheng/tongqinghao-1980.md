A 1980 Tongqinghao (同慶號), one of the most legendary names in pu'erh.

Tongqinghao is among the oldest known pu'erh brands, with origins traced to the late Qing dynasty in Yiwu. The original Tongqinghao cakes from the early 1900s are among the most expensive teas ever sold at auction. A 1980 production carrying this name — whether from the revived brand or from old stock — connects to a lineage that defines what aged pu'erh means.

## Terroir

Yunnan province, historically associated with Yiwu. The Tongqinghao name carries specific regional associations — Yiwu was the center of the antique pu'erh trade, and the Tongqing brand sourced from the old tea mountains surrounding the town. Whether this 1980 production maintains that sourcing is uncertain but likely.

## Processing

Sheng pu'erh in loose form. The Tongqinghao tradition emphasized quality material and careful processing. Forty-five years of aging have developed the characteristic aged sheng profile — camphor, dark fruit, and aromatic wood — with the Yiwu-associated softness that the brand is known for.

## Experience

The name carries weight, and the tea earns it. Camphor and plum lead, followed by a honeyed sweetness and aromatic wood that unfolds over multiple steepings. The hui gan is complex — it shifts and develops as the session progresses. This is a tea with history built into its name and its leaves.
