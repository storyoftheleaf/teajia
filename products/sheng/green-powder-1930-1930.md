A 1930 loose sheng pu-erh in powdered form. Nearly a century old, this is among the oldest teas in the entire collection.

The powdered state suggests either intentional grinding for traditional preparation or natural breakdown of very old leaf over time. Tea of this age exists in a category beyond normal evaluation.

## Terroir

Yunnan province. A tea from 1930 predates the People's Republic, predates modern pu-erh production standards, and comes from an era when Yunnan tea was still being traded along ancient caravan routes.

## Processing

The original processing is lost to history. What remains after ninety-five years is the residue of transformation upon transformation. The leaf has passed through so many stages of change that the original processing is irrelevant. What you taste is pure age.

## Mood

whispered history

## Experience

There is almost nothing left but the ghost of tea. What you taste is not a flavor profile but a feeling. Sweet, faint, ancient. It is a meditation object more than a beverage. Handle it with the respect that ninety-five years demands.
