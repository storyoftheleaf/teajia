A 2000 sheng iron cake (tie bing).

The 'dark green' (墨绿) description refers to the leaf color at the time of pressing: deep, dark green material suggesting robust, mature leaf. Iron cakes are hydraulically pressed to extreme density, creating a tea that ages very slowly and maintains intensity over decades.

## Terroir

Yunnan province. The dark green leaf color and robust character suggest mid-elevation garden material with good concentration.

## Processing

Sun-dried Yunnan maocha, hydraulically pressed into an extremely dense cake. Twenty-five years of aging in this compressed format. The iron cake format preserves and concentrates flavors, aging much more slowly than stone-pressed cakes.

## Mood

compressed patience

## Experience

Breaking into this cake takes effort, and the tea rewards it. Dense, concentrated, and still carrying some youthful bitterness that is rapidly converting to sweetness after twenty-five years. The camphor and dark fruit notes are vivid. An iron cake doing what iron cakes do.
