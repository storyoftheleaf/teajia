White Needle Golden Lotus (白针金莲) is a poetic name for a 1980 sheng that evokes both the white bud material (bai zhen, white needle) and the golden lotus, a symbol of purity in Chinese Buddhist culture.

Over forty years old, this tea carries both its Buddhist-influenced name and the weight of four decades of transformation.

## Terroir

Yunnan province. The 'white needle' designation suggests a bud-heavy picking style, indicating higher-altitude or early-spring material where buds are more prevalent. The 'golden lotus' name adds cultural significance without specifying geography.

## Processing

Sun-dried sheng pu-erh from 1980, likely using bud-heavy material given the 'white needle' designation. Forty-five years of natural aging on what appears to be finer, more delicate raw material than typical sheng cakes of this era.

## Mood

lotus clarity

## Experience

Lighter and more delicate than the other aged sheng in the collection. The bud-heavy material produces a thinner body but a more aromatic, floral character. The camphor is gentle rather than forceful. A contemplative sheng that favors subtlety over power.
