A 1976 sheng pu-erh infused with ginseng. The year places this tea in the Cultural Revolution era, a period when tea production continued under state control but with minimal documentation.

Ginseng-infused pu-erh was a traditional preparation believed to combine the qi of both plants. Fifty years have merged the two into something unified.

## Terroir

Yunnan province for the tea, with ginseng root (likely from northeastern China or Korea). The combination of pu-erh and ginseng is a traditional Chinese wellness preparation that predates modern commercial tea culture.

## Processing

Raw pu-erh processed with ginseng root, a traditional Chinese medicinal preparation. The ginseng was likely added during or after pressing. Five decades of co-aging have fully integrated the two elements into a single unified character.

## Mood

renewed strength

## Experience

The ginseng and the tea have become one thing. You cannot separate them after fifty years together. There is a medicinal warmth that starts in the throat and spreads through the body, something neither ginseng alone nor pu-erh alone produces. A tea of renewal in the truest sense.
