A 2006 Yiwu sheng, twenty years into its journey.

At this age, Yiwu sheng begins to show the sweetness and depth that make the region legendary. The astringency of youth is fading, replaced by a growing complexity that will deepen for decades to come.

## Terroir

Yiwu township, Mengla county, Xishuangbanna, Yunnan. Yiwu's terroir produces sheng with a characteristic sweetness and smoothness that distinguishes it from the more bitter, powerful profile of Menghai teas.

## Processing

Sun-dried Yiwu maocha, stone-pressed into cake. Twenty years of natural aging have brought this past the awkward middle period where sheng can taste neither young nor old. It is entering its stride.

## Mood

warming light

## Experience

The sunshine in the name feels right. There is a warmth and brightness to this tea that twenty years of aging have not dimmed. The honey and apricot notes are vivid, the body is thickening, and the camphor adds depth without heaviness. A Yiwu sheng coming into its own.
