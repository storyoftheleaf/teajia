Blue Seal Green Label (蓝印绿标铁饼) is a designation from the era of state-controlled pu-erh production.

The colored seal and label system was used by the China National Tea Corporation to classify production batches. A 1990 iron cake bearing this designation carries both historical weight and the dense compression that defines the tie bing format.

## Terroir

Yunnan province. Blue Seal production was typically associated with the larger state-run factories. The iron cake format was popular for its durability during transport and the distinctive aging profile that extreme compression produces.

## Processing

Hydraulically pressed sheng pu-erh (iron cake format). The blue seal green label designation indicates a specific production classification within the state system. Thirty-five years of aging in the extremely dense format have produced a concentrated, slowly transforming tea.

## Experience

The seal on the wrapper tells a story of state-era tea production. The tea inside tells its own. Dense and concentrated, still carrying structured bitterness that is slowly, deliberately converting to sweetness. A patient tea from a patient format.
