A 1996 Menghai 'bullet' tuo, named for its small, dense shape.

Bullet tuo cha is a sub-format of the traditional tuo, smaller and more compressed, designed for intense aging in a compact form. Nearly thirty years have produced a concentrated, intense character.

## Terroir

Menghai county, Xishuangbanna, Yunnan. The Menghai Tea Factory and surrounding producers were the primary source of tuo cha production in this era.

## Processing

Sun-dried maocha compressed into bullet tuo form under high pressure. The extreme compression and small format create aging dynamics different from standard cakes or larger tuo. Thirty years of aging in this dense format produce a concentrated, intense character.

## Mood

concentrated force

## Experience

Dense and powerful. The bullet format has concentrated thirty years of transformation into a small, intense package. Breaking off a piece releases aromas that fill the room. Strong, sweet, and deeply satisfying. Not a gentle tea.
