A 1998 Menghai high-mountain sheng from Yunnan's Xishuangbanna region.

Teas from this era were pressed without ceremony and stored without expectation. Twenty-five years later, the leaf has transformed into something the original pressers never imagined.

## Terroir

Menghai county in southern Yunnan sits at the heart of pu-erh country. High-altitude gardens above 1,400 meters produce leaves with pronounced mineral character and a sweetness that only emerges after years of patient storage.

## Processing

Sun-dried maocha from high-altitude Menghai gardens, stone-pressed into cake form. Over two decades of natural aging have fully converted the original green astringency into deep, layered complexity.

## Mood

quiet persistence

## Experience

It brews a deep amber with surprising clarity. The first steeps are all camphor and wood, then a dried fruit sweetness builds underneath. A tea that has outlived its own anonymity.
