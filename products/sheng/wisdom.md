Wisdom (智慧) is a name that implies not cleverness but the deeper knowing that comes from experience and stillness.

This sheng carries that word as its identity, chosen by someone who tasted something in the cup that went beyond flavor into understanding.

## Terroir

Yunnan province. The provenance is unknown. The naming suggests a tea selected for its depth and contemplative quality rather than its intensity or power.

## Processing

Raw sheng pu-erh. The name and the price point suggest carefully chosen material with a character that earned a significant word. Specific origin and vintage are unrecorded.

## Mood

deep knowing

## Experience

The name is not casual. The tea is not either. There is a measured quality here, a bitterness that does not rush and a sweetness that does not force itself. It asks for attention and rewards patience. Whether that constitutes wisdom is between you and the cup.
