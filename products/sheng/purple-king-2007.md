Purple King (紫芽王) is a bold name for a bold tea. Purple-leaf sheng pu-erh from 2007 uses a rare genetic variant of the tea plant that produces anthocyanin-rich purple leaves.

The 'King' designation suggests exceptional material, likely from old trees that produce the most concentrated purple buds. Nearly twenty years of aging have begun to tame the distinctive bitterness.

## Terroir

Yunnan province, likely from old-growth forests where purple-leaf variants occur naturally among standard populations. These trees are scattered rather than cultivated, making true purple-leaf material inherently limited in supply.

## Processing

Sun-dried purple-leaf maocha from select old trees, compressed into 357-gram cake form. The elevated anthocyanin content creates a fundamentally different aging trajectory than standard green-leaf sheng. Eighteen years have softened the initial intensity while preserving the distinctive purple character.

## Mood

sovereign intensity

## Experience

This tea does not ease into anything. The first steep is intense, bitter in a way that is specific to purple leaf, more wild berry than tannic. By the third steep the sweetness arrives and transforms everything. Eighteen years have added depth without removing the wildness. A tea that demands your full attention.
