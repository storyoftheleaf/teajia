This tea arrived already named.

Courage (勇气) carries meaning from the hands that stored it, a private collection in mainland China where the practice of naming stored teas is common among serious collectors. The name marks not just what a tea is but what it means to the one who kept it.

## Terroir

Yunnan province, though the precise mountain and village are unrecorded. This is common for teas from private collections. The provenance lives in the cup, not the label.

## Processing

Raw sheng pu-erh. The specific processing details are unknown, but the leaf character suggests careful sun-drying and hand-pressing from an era when production was less standardized and more personal.

## Mood

quiet resolve

## Experience

It opens with a directness that earns its name. Bitter on the front, then a slow sweetness that rises through the throat and stays. Not gentle, not aggressive. Present. The kind of tea that meets you where you are.
