A 2010 sheng from an unspecified 'river town' origin.

Yunnan's tea regions are defined by their river systems, the Lancang (Mekong) in particular, and many villages sit along waterways that have served as tea trade routes for centuries. Fifteen years of aging.

## Terroir

A river town in Yunnan's pu-erh producing region. River valleys in Yunnan create their own microclimates, with the water moderating temperatures and contributing humidity that affects both tea growth and post-harvest aging.

## Processing

Sun-dried Yunnan maocha, stone-pressed. At fifteen years, this sheng is in early transition. The river-influenced terroir may contribute a particular mineral quality to the leaf.

## Mood

riverside patience

## Experience

Gentle and still developing. The honey note is emerging from underneath the remaining green character. There is a mineral quality that may be the river terroir expressing itself. A modest tea with quiet potential.
