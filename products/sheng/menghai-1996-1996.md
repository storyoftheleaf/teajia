A 1996 Menghai sheng pu-erh, thirty years into its aging journey.

Teas from the Menghai region pressed in the mid-1990s are highly valued because they represent the last era before pu-erh prices skyrocketed and production expanded dramatically. This is old-school Menghai: honest leaf, clean storage, and three decades of patient transformation.

## Terroir

Menghai county, Xishuangbanna, Yunnan. One of the two great pu-erh production centers (alongside Yiwu). Menghai teas tend toward a more robust, powerful profile compared to Yiwu's sweetness, with a distinctive bitterness that transforms into hui gan (returning sweetness) over time.

## Processing

Sun-dried Yunnan maocha from the Menghai area, stone-pressed into cake form. Thirty years of natural aging have fully transformed the original green character. The tea has crossed the threshold from 'aging sheng' into 'aged sheng.'

## Mood

aged authority

## Experience

Fully aged and drinking beautifully. The camphor and dried fruit are vivid, the body is thick, and the bitterness has completely converted to sweetness. Thirty years have done their work. This is what patience tastes like.
