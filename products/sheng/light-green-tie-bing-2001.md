A 2001 sheng iron cake with lighter-colored leaf than its dark green counterpart.

The lighter color suggests younger, more tender leaf material or earlier spring picking. Comparing this alongside the 2000 Dark Green Tie Bing reveals how leaf grade affects aging in the same compressed format.

## Terroir

Yunnan province. The lighter leaf color suggests higher-altitude or earlier-harvest material with more buds and young leaves.

## Processing

Sun-dried Yunnan maocha from lighter, more tender leaf material, hydraulically pressed. Twenty-four years of aging in the dense iron cake format. The lighter leaf grade produces a more refined, less aggressive aging profile.

## Mood

gentle strength

## Experience

Lighter and more refined than its dark green counterpart but still carrying the concentrated character of the iron cake format. The sweetness is brighter, the camphor more delicate. A good comparison piece for understanding how leaf grade affects aged sheng.
