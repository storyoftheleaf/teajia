Old Rainbow (旧彩虹) sheng, the elder version of the New Rainbow.

The 'old' designation and higher price suggest greater age and depth. The rainbow imagery carries forward: a wide spectrum of flavor refined by time into something more unified but no less varied.

## Terroir

Yunnan province. Same unknown origin as New Rainbow, but with additional years of transformation. The aging has shifted the spectrum from bright primary colors toward deeper, more blended tones.

## Processing

Raw sheng pu-erh, the aged counterpart to New Rainbow. More time has unified what the younger version presents as diversity. The rainbow of flavors has been blended by aging into something more cohesive.

## Mood

deepened spectrum

## Experience

Where New Rainbow is bright and varied, Old Rainbow is deep and unified. The same breadth of flavor is present but it has been blended by time into something more harmonious. The rainbow has aged into a sunset.
