Puerh comes from Yunnan; sheng is the raw style, kill-green and sun-dried, meant to age naturally over decades with no shortcut piling.

## Terroir

Yunnan province, southern China, the historical heart of puerh production.

## Processing

Raw sheng puerh from the 1980s. Sheng at this age moves quietly, the edges of youth long gone. Carries the mellowed character of proper humid aging in a tropical place. Expertly stored in Guangzhou.
