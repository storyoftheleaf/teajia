Yaobao (芽苞) are wild tea buds harvested before they open, tiny and densely packed with concentrated flavor. They grow on wild tea trees in Yunnan's forests and are picked in early spring when the buds are still tightly furled.

This is not technically tea in the processed sense. It is the raw beginning of tea, dried and preserved in its most primal form.

## Terroir

Yunnan's wild tea forests, likely at moderate to high elevation. Yaobao comes from wild trees that are not cultivated or managed. The buds are foraged rather than farmed.

## Processing

Minimal processing: wild buds are harvested and dried, often sun-dried. There is no kill-green step, no rolling, no oxidation control. The buds dry as they are, preserving whatever chemistry they carried at the moment of picking.

## Mood

pure beginning

## Experience

Unlike anything else in the collection. These are not tea leaves but tea buds, wild and unprocessed. The flavor is delicate, sweet, and faintly floral with a wildness underneath. Brewing them feels less like making tea and more like steeping a piece of the forest itself.
