The name Qing Mao Hong Cha (青毛红茶) combined with '1000 Year' is intriguing.

Despite being classified as green in the inventory, this appears to be a red tea (hong cha) made from old-tree material. The '1000 Year' likely refers to the age of the trees rather than the tea.

## Terroir

Ancient tea forests of Yunnan. Trees described as '1000 year' grow in protected old-growth forest areas at moderate to high elevation.

## Processing

Likely sun-dried or lightly processed red tea from ancient tree material. The '1000 Year' tree age designation suggests leaf from very old arbor trees in Yunnan's ancient tea forests.

## Mood

deep roots

## Experience

Full-bodied and rich with the distinctive depth that only ancient-tree material provides. The honey and wild herb notes carry a complexity that young-tree red teas cannot match. A tea that tastes like the forest it grew in.
