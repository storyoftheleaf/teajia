A 2018 version of the same ancient-tree red tea.

Comparing alongside the 2016 provides insight into year-to-year variation and how even a couple of years of aging affects this style.

## Terroir

Ancient tea forests of Yunnan. The '1000 Year' designation points to the same old-growth tree source as the 2016 version.

## Processing

Same ancient-tree processing as the 2016 vintage but from a different harvest year. Two years younger, it may show a slightly brighter, less integrated profile.

## Mood

living history

## Experience

Slightly brighter than the 2016 version with a freshness that the older vintage has begun to trade for depth. Side by side, they tell a story about what two years of aging does to ancient-tree red tea.
