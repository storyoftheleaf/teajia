Dong Ding is Taiwan's original oolong, rooted in Lugu Township since the 19th century.

This 1996 pressing carries the hallmark of competition-grade processing: tight roll, careful oxidation, and a charcoal roast that was built to age. Nearly thirty years have proven the maker right.

## Terroir

Lugu Township in Nantou County, Taiwan, sits at moderate elevation in a valley known for its fog and fertile soil. Dong Ding mountain itself is small but its terroir is distinctive: clay-heavy earth, consistent humidity, and cool nights that slow the leaf's growth.

## Processing

Medium-to-heavy oxidation with traditional charcoal roasting, likely re-roasted periodically over its life to maintain stability. The tight ball-roll preserves internal moisture and enables a long, slow transformation.

## Mood

settled wisdom

## Experience

The roast hits first, warm and clean. Then underneath, a fruit sweetness appears, something like ripe plum left in the sun. It has the kind of depth that only comes from tea made with skill and left alone long enough to prove it.
