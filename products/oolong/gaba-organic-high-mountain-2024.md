GABA oolong is one of tea's genuine innovations. Discovered in 1987 by Japanese researchers, the process involves oxidizing tea leaves in a nitrogen-rich, oxygen-free environment.

This triggers the plant to convert glutamic acid into gamma-aminobutyric acid (GABA), a compound associated with relaxation. This organic high-mountain version represents the premium end of the category.

## Terroir

High-mountain gardens in Taiwan, likely in the Nantou or Chiayi regions above 1,000 meters. The high-altitude growing conditions contribute complexity that standard lowland GABA oolongs lack. Organic cultivation adds further dimension from healthier soil biology.

## Processing

The leaf is first withered normally, then sealed in nitrogen chambers during oxidation. This anaerobic step is what produces the elevated GABA content. After nitrogen processing, the tea is finished with a light roast to stabilize the flavors. The organic certification ensures no chemical inputs during cultivation.

## Experience

Noticeably smooth and sweet with a ripe, almost tropical character. The GABA effect is subtle but real. There is a loosening quality to this tea, a physical settling that goes beyond flavor. Good for evening drinking or when you need to wind down.
