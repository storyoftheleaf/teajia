Muyun (Mother Rhyme) Da Hong Pao.

Da Hong Pao is the most famous of all Wuyi rock oolongs, and 'Muyun' suggests this is made from mother bush cuttings or in a style that honors the original cultivar's character. Da Hong Pao's name means 'Big Red Robe,' a reference to the legend of a grateful scholar who draped his red robe over the tea bushes that cured his illness.

## Terroir

Wuyi Mountains, Fujian province. The original Da Hong Pao bushes grow on a cliff face in the scenic area, but all commercial Da Hong Pao today comes from cuttings planted across the Wuyi rock tea region. The 'zhengyan' (true rock) terroir of these mountains imparts the mineral depth known as yan yun.

## Processing

Heavy charcoal roasting in the traditional Wuyi yancha method. The strip-rolled leaf undergoes multiple rounds of roasting over weeks or months. Da Hong Pao processing is considered one of the most labor-intensive and skill-dependent in all of Chinese tea.

## Mood

deep reverence

## Experience

Dark and commanding. The mineral note is immediate, almost geological. Underneath the roast there is a sweetness that surfaces slowly, like water rising through stone. A tea with authority. Best when you have time and attention to give it.
