A 1979 roasted oolong from Nantou County, the heartland of Taiwanese oolong production.

Nantou sits in central Taiwan and encompasses the Lugu and Sun Moon Lake tea-growing areas. A heavily roasted oolong from this era represents the peak of traditional Taiwanese processing before lighter styles took over the market.

## Terroir

Nantou County, central Taiwan. This is the most productive tea region in Taiwan, with elevation ranging from lowland to high-mountain.

## Processing

Heavy charcoal roasting in the traditional Taiwanese style. Over forty years of post-roast aging have fully integrated the charcoal character. The original roast profile is no longer a separate element but part of the tea's fundamental structure.

## Mood

fire memory

## Experience

Dark and deeply sweet. The roast is no longer something you taste on top of the tea. It is the tea. Caramel and dried fruit and a warmth that sits in the chest. A forty-year-old reminder of how Taiwanese oolong used to be made.
