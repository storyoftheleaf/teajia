Organically grown GABA oolong from Taiwan.

Organic tea cultivation in Taiwan is challenging due to the humid subtropical climate, which makes pest management without chemicals difficult. Farmers who achieve it produce leaf with a different character: often rougher in appearance but more complex in flavor.

## Terroir

Taiwan's highland tea regions. Organic farms tend to sit at higher elevations where cooler temperatures naturally reduce pest pressure. The resulting leaf grows more slowly and develops greater concentration of flavor compounds.

## Processing

Nitrogen-chamber anaerobic oxidation with organic-grade leaf. The processing is identical to conventional GABA oolong, but the leaf itself carries more variability and character from organic growing conditions.

## Mood

gentle unwinding

## Experience

A touch more complexity than its EU-certified counterpart. There is a slight edge, a mild astringency that gives the cup structure. The GABA relaxation effect is present and noticeable. Good for late afternoon.
