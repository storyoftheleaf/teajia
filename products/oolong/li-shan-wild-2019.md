Li Shan (Pear Mountain) is one of Taiwan's highest and most prestigious tea-growing regions, with gardens reaching above 2,000 meters.

Wild-grown oolong from this altitude represents the extreme end of Taiwanese gaoshan production, where thin air, intense UV, and cold nights force the plant to concentrate everything it has into fewer leaves.

## Terroir

Li Shan sits in the central mountain range of Taiwan, towering above most other tea regions. At 2,000+ meters, the gardens are frequently above the cloud line. The dramatic temperature swings between day and night produce amino acids and aromatic compounds at levels that lower-elevation teas cannot match.

## Processing

Lightly oxidized high-mountain oolong, processed with minimal intervention to preserve the terroir expression. The 'wild' designation suggests trees growing without intensive cultivation, producing lower yields with more concentrated flavor.

## Mood

alpine clarity

## Experience

Crystal clear in the cup with a fragrance that is almost overwhelming before you taste it. Floral and mineral on the tongue, then a sweetness that stays. Extremely clean finish. A tea that tastes like altitude.
