Red Edge (红边) oolong refers to a traditional Taiwanese processing style where the edges of the leaves are allowed to oxidize more heavily, turning red, while the center remains green.

This technique was common in older Taiwanese oolong production but has largely given way to lighter styles. A 2024 production in this style represents a deliberate return to traditional methods.

## Terroir

Taiwan. The red-edge processing style is historically associated with Nantou and other central Taiwan tea regions.

## Processing

Traditional Taiwanese oolong processing with extended edge oxidation. The red edges create a partially oxidized tea with more body and warmth than modern light-style oolongs, bridging the gap between green and roasted styles.

## Mood

warm tradition

## Experience

The extra oxidation shows. There is more body and warmth here than in light gaoshan oolongs, with a honey sweetness and a roasted grain note that recall how Taiwanese oolong tasted a generation ago. A welcome return.
