Universe (宇宙) takes the cosmic naming one step further than Galaxy. A small quantity (75g) at a very high price for an oolong without documented provenance.

Someone tasted this tea and reached for the biggest word they could find. That is either pretension or genuine awe.

## Terroir

Unknown. At this price point and with this name, the tea could be from any of China's greatest oolong regions. The absence of documentation makes the cup the only authority.

## Processing

Oolong of unknown origin, cultivar, and vintage. The extreme price and small quantity suggest a truly exceptional tea, whether through age, rarity, or quality. The cosmic naming is a testament to the impression it made.

## Mood

infinite depth

## Experience

The name is either the most honest or the most ambitious in the collection. The tea will have to answer for it. What a name like Universe promises is everything, and no tea can deliver that. What it can deliver is a cup worth the price of entry. Whether it does is between you and seventy-five grams of mystery.
