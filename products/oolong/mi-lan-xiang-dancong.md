Mi Lan Xiang (Honey Orchid Fragrance) is the most classic of all Phoenix Mountain dancong cultivars.

It defines the category for many drinkers: the combination of honey sweetness and orchid floral that gives this tea its name is one of the most recognizable profiles in Chinese tea.

## Terroir

Phoenix Mountain, Chaozhou, Guangdong. The old tea trees that produce Mi Lan Xiang grow on steep, rocky slopes where root systems penetrate deep into mineral-rich granite. Altitude varies but the best examples come from above 800 meters.

## Processing

Traditional dancong processing with careful oxidation to develop the honey-orchid fragrance. Medium roast level preserves the cultivar's natural aromatics while adding a warm base note. Single-bush processing ensures the distinct character of each tree is expressed.

## Mood

sweet warmth

## Experience

A tea that smells exactly like its name. Honey and orchid together, clear and unmistakable. The body is smooth and the sweetness persists through many steepings. Approachable and generous. A good first dancong for someone new to the style.
