A 1990 Tie Guan Yin from Fujian province.

This sits in the final generation of traditionally processed Tie Guan Yin before the industry shifted toward lighter, greener styles in the mid-1990s. Thirty years of aging have deepened what was already a substantial tea into something extraordinary.

## Terroir

Anxi county, Fujian province. The iron-rich laterite soil and misty mountain environment of this region are inseparable from the Tie Guan Yin cultivar's identity. No other origin produces the same combination of mineral depth and aromatic complexity.

## Processing

Traditional Anxi-style processing with full oxidation and heavy charcoal roasting. The 1990 vintage represents late-era traditional methods before the market demanded lighter, more floral profiles. Three decades of natural aging.

## Mood

warm depth

## Experience

Rich and enveloping. The roast has fully integrated after thirty years, leaving behind a smooth, honeyed sweetness with dried fruit running through it. The body is thick and the finish lingers. A tea that fills the room.
