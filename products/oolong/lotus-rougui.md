Rougui (Cinnamon) is one of Wuyi's flagship rock tea cultivars, named for its distinctive cinnamon-bark aroma.

The 'Lotus' designation may refer to the growing area or a specific processing variation. Rougui has become one of the most popular and widely produced Wuyi yancha varieties, prized for its bold, spicy character.

## Terroir

Wuyi Mountains, Fujian. The rocky, mineral-dense growing areas between Wuyi's sandstone cliffs are essential to rougui's character. The cultivar expresses its cinnamon note most clearly when grown in 'zhengyan' (true rock) areas.

## Processing

Wuyi yancha processing with heavy charcoal roast. Rougui responds well to intense roasting, and the cinnamon character becomes more pronounced with heat. The strip-rolled leaf is roasted in stages over multiple sessions.

## Mood

bold warmth

## Experience

The cinnamon hits immediately and does not apologize. It is warm and slightly sharp, then the mineral base rises to meet it. The throat feel is thick and the warmth spreads through the chest. Not a subtle tea. Best for drinkers who want something that announces itself.
