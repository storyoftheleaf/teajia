Alishan is one of Taiwan's most celebrated high-mountain tea regions.

The cool mist that wraps these gardens above 1,200 meters slows the leaf's growth and concentrates flavor into something that lowland tea simply cannot replicate.

## Terroir

The Alishan mountain range in Chiayi County, Taiwan, sits between 1,000 and 2,600 meters. Tea gardens here are often shrouded in fog, with dramatic temperature swings between day and night that stress the plant in ways that deepen flavor.

## Processing

Lightly oxidized and gently rolled in the traditional Taiwanese gaoshan style. Minimal roasting preserves the delicate floral character and the natural sweetness of slow-grown high-altitude leaf.

## Mood

morning clarity

## Experience

Light gold in the cup with a fragrance that fills the room before you sip. Buttery and floral on the tongue, with a clean finish that invites you to pour again immediately.
