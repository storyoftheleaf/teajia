Wuyi rougui in quantity.

Rougui has overtaken Da Hong Pao as the most widely produced Wuyi rock tea, and for good reason: the cultivar is reliable, expressive, and rewards both careful processing and casual drinking.

## Terroir

Wuyi Mountains, Fujian province. The rocky, mineral-dense environment that produces legendary oolongs gives this rougui its essential character.

## Processing

Standard Wuyi yancha processing with charcoal roasting. Rougui is one of the more forgiving cultivars to process, but the best versions still require careful management of the multiple roasting stages.

## Mood

daily fire

## Experience

The everyday rougui. Cinnamon warmth, clean roast, enough mineral to remind you where it comes from. A reliable cup that works morning or afternoon. Not the most complex Wuyi tea in the collection, but the one you reach for most often.
