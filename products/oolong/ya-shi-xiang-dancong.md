Ya Shi Xiang (Duck Shit Aroma) dancong from Phoenix Mountain in Guangdong. The name was coined by a farmer who told curious neighbors his soil smelled of duck droppings, hoping to keep them from stealing cuttings of his exceptionally fragrant bushes.

The ruse failed. The name stuck.

It is now one of the most sought-after dancong cultivars in the world.

## Terroir

Phoenix Mountain (Fenghuang Shan) in Chaozhou, Guangdong. The mountain's unique microclimate and mineral-rich soil support old tea trees, some centuries old, that produce leaves with aromatic intensity unmatched in the oolong world.

## Processing

Single-bush dancong processing: careful withering, controlled oxidation, and a moderate roast that preserves the cultivar's extraordinary fragrance while adding depth. Each bush produces tea with slightly different character, making dancong one of the most individualistic tea categories.

bright delight

## Experience

The fragrance fills the room before the first sip. Gardenia and something like warm pastry. Each cup is bright and clean with a sweetness that lifts rather than settles. The kind of tea that makes an ordinary afternoon feel like an occasion.
