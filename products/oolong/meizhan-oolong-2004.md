Meizhan is a cultivar originally developed in Wuyi but widely planted across Fujian and Taiwan for its versatility and distinctive plum-blossom aroma.

This 2004 example has had twenty years to develop the kind of aged character that transforms a good oolong into something memorable.

## Terroir

The Meizhan cultivar adapts to a range of growing conditions but produces its most distinctive character in the rocky, mineral-rich soils of Fujian's mountain regions. Whether grown in Wuyi or elsewhere, the plum-blossom aroma is a signature of the cultivar itself.

## Processing

Medium oxidation with traditional charcoal roasting. Meizhan cultivar responds well to aging due to its naturally robust leaf structure. Twenty years of post-roast transformation have deepened the flavor profile significantly.

## Mood

quiet bloom

## Experience

The plum note is subtle but unmistakable. It sits behind the roast and the grain, surfacing in the aftertaste rather than the first impression. A tea that reveals itself slowly across many steepings.
