Oriental Beauty (Dong Fang Mei Ren) is Taiwan's most unique oolong.

Its character depends on a natural phenomenon: small green leafhoppers bite the leaves during growth, triggering the plant to produce aromatic compounds as a defense response. This insect-bitten quality is the signature of genuine Oriental Beauty and cannot be manufactured.

## Terroir

Hsinchu and Miaoli counties in northern Taiwan are the traditional centers of Oriental Beauty production. The warm, humid conditions of these regions attract the jassid leafhoppers whose bites are essential to the tea's character. No bites, no beauty.

## Processing

Heavy oxidation (60-70%) of leafhopper-bitten leaf. Oriental Beauty is one of the most heavily oxidized oolongs, approaching red tea territory. The processing preserves the muscatel character created by the insect bites while developing honey and spice notes through extended oxidation.

## Mood

warm elegance

## Experience

Honey and muscatel fill the cup with a sweetness that is natural, not manufactured. The heavy oxidation gives it body and warmth while the leafhopper character adds an aromatic complexity that no other tea possesses. A genuinely unique experience.
