A fresh 2024 Meizhan from the Wuyi Mountains.

Young Meizhan processed as yancha (rock tea) carries the cultivar's characteristic plum-blossom aroma at its most vivid. Before aging rounds and deepens the profile, fresh Meizhan is bright and floral with an assertiveness that older versions mellow into.

## Terroir

Wuyi Mountains, Fujian province. The mineral-rich soil and the unique microclimate between sandstone cliffs create the terroir essential to yancha character.

## Processing

Wuyi yancha processing with charcoal roasting. Fresh production means the roast character is still prominent and will integrate over the coming years. The Meizhan cultivar's natural plum-blossom aroma is at its most expressive in young tea.

## Mood

bright fire

## Experience

Fresh and lively with a roast character that still sits on top of the tea rather than within it. The plum blossom is vivid and the mineral note is sharp. Give it a year or two for the roast to integrate, or enjoy it now for its energy.
