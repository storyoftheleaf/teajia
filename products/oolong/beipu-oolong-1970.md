Beipu is a Hakka township in Hsinchu County, Taiwan, historically known for its Oriental Beauty (Dong Fang Mei Ren) oolong and its Hakka-style tea culture.

That this 1970 oolong was packaged in tea bags is remarkable. Tea bag packaging of quality oolong was uncommon in this era and suggests these were prepared for a specific market or occasion.

## Terroir

Beipu Township, Hsinchu County, Taiwan. A Hakka tea region at moderate elevation known for warm, humid conditions that attract the leafhoppers essential for Oriental Beauty production. The general oolong from this area tends toward heavier oxidation and roast.

## Processing

Taiwanese oolong processing with medium roast, packaged into bags. The tea bag format from 1970 is unusual and may represent institutional or hospitality packaging from the era. Fifty-plus years of aging, even in bag form, have developed significant depth.

## Mood

unexpected depth

## Experience

The tea bag is a surprise. What comes out of it is not. Aged and smooth with a roasted sweetness that defies the packaging. A reminder that good tea can survive almost anything if given enough time.
