Chaozhou clay teapots are the other great Chinese teapot tradition alongside Yixing.

Where Yixing pots are often collector's pieces, Chaozhou pots are built to brew. The Su Zhang style references a specific regional form optimized for gongfu tea preparation.

## Terroir

Chaozhou city, Guangdong province. The local clay and kiln traditions have developed in parallel with the region's gongfu tea culture for centuries.

## Processing

Chaozhou clay body, typically fired at higher temperatures than Yixing, producing a thinner wall and faster heat transfer. This makes Chaozhou pots ideal for teas that need quick, hot steeps.

## Mood

focused purpose

## Experience

Thinner walls than Yixing, faster pour. Built for the quick, intense steeps that Chaozhou gongfu demands. Ideal for dancong, yancha, and any oolong that needs heat.
