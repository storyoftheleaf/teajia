A cup referencing the Hutian kilns of Song Dynasty Jingdezhen.

The bamboo-node form (竹节) is a classic Chinese ceramic motif, representing integrity and resilience. Song Dynasty aesthetics valued restraint, and this cup honors that tradition.

## Terroir

Hutian kiln tradition, Jingdezhen, Jiangxi province. The Hutian kilns operated during the Song Dynasty and are among the most important historical kiln sites in Chinese ceramic history.

## Processing

Kiln-fired ceramic in the bamboo-node form. Song Dynasty ceramic aesthetics emphasized simple, elegant forms with subtle glazes.

## Mood

Song restraint

## Experience

The shape is the statement. A bamboo node in ceramic, simple and clean. Drinks well with any tea and reminds you that the best designs are the oldest ones.
