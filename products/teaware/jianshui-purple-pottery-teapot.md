Jianshui purple pottery (建水紫陶) is one of China's four famous ceramics, alongside Yixing zisha, Qinzhou nixing, and Rongchang pottery.

The distinctive technique involves carving designs into the clay surface, filling with contrasting colored clay, then polishing to a high sheen after firing.

## Terroir

Jianshui county, Yunnan province. The local clay deposits produce a distinctive fine-grained purple body that polishes to a mirror finish. Jianshui pottery has been produced here since the Song Dynasty.

## Processing

Jianshui purple clay, hand-built and carved. The clay is polished after firing rather than glazed, producing a smooth, almost metallic surface. The fine-grained clay structure is less porous than Yixing, making it more neutral in how it affects tea flavor.

## Mood

polished depth

## Experience

Smoother and denser than Yixing. The fine-grained clay does not absorb as aggressively, making it more versatile across tea types. The polished surface feels like stone in the hand.
