Copper kettles have been used in Chinese tea culture for centuries.

Copper heats evenly and quickly, and traditional Chinese medicine associates copper vessels with health benefits. The longevity character (寿) stamped on this pot connects it to that tradition.

## Terroir

Copper kettle craft is widespread across China and Southeast Asia. Hand-hammered examples carry the mark of individual making.

## Processing

Hand-hammered copper with traditional character engraving. Copper develops a natural patina over time that does not affect water quality but adds visual character.

## Mood

warm craft

## Experience

Heats water with an evenness that electric kettles cannot match. The copper adds a subtle softness to the water that experienced brewers notice.
