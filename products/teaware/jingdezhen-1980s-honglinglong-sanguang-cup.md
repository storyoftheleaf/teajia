Hong Linglong (红玲珑) is the red-glazed version of the rice-grain perforation technique.

The red glaze over the translucent windows creates a warmer, deeper glow than the white version.

## Terroir

Jingdezhen, Jiangxi province. 1980s factory production.

## Processing

Jingdezhen porcelain with rice-grain perforations under red overglaze. The combination of techniques requires multiple firings at different temperatures.

## Mood

warm glow

## Experience

The red version of the Linglong cups. Warmer in tone and feeling. Same craft, different mood.
