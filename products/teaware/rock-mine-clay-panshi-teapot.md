Panshi (盘石) means 'flat stone,' describing the low, wide shape of this pot.

The form maximizes surface area for heat dissipation, suitable for teas that should not be over-steeped.

## Terroir

Chinese mineral clay deposits.

## Processing

Rock mine clay in a low-profile form. The wide, flat shape allows faster cooling than tall pots.
