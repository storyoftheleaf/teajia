Red clay (红泥) teapots are among the most affordable and practical brewing vessels.

The clay heats quickly and pours clean. In Chaozhou gongfu tradition, inexpensive red clay pots are often preferred because they can be dedicated to a single tea without financial concern.

## Terroir

Southern Chinese red clay deposits, common across Guangdong and Fujian provinces.

## Processing

Red clay body, porous and quick to heat. Less refined than Yixing or Jianshui clays but perfectly functional for daily brewing.

## Mood

humble daily

## Experience

A pot you use without worrying. Dedicate it to one tea and brew every day. At this price, you can have several, each assigned to a different tea type.
