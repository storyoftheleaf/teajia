A gongdao bei (公道杯, fairness cup) is used to decant tea from the brewing vessel before pouring into individual cups.

This ensures everyone receives tea of equal strength. Glass lets you see the liquor color before serving.

## Terroir

Chinese gongfu tea tradition.

## Processing

Heat-resistant glass. The transparency serves a purpose: it lets you judge concentration and color before pouring.
