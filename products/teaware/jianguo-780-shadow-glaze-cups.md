Yingqing (影青, shadow celadon) is one of the oldest Chinese glaze styles, dating to the Song Dynasty.

The pale blue-green glaze pools in carved recesses, creating subtle shadow patterns that give the style its name. 'Jianguo 780' likely refers to a specific production series.

## Terroir

Chinese celadon tradition, historically centered in Jingdezhen and Longquan.

## Processing

Porcelain body with yingqing (shadow celadon) glaze. The glaze is translucent, pooling in carved or impressed patterns to create depth. This technique has been practiced for nearly a thousand years.

## Mood

ancient light

## Experience

The subtlest cups in the collection. The shadow glaze reveals itself slowly as you drink, the pattern changing with the angle of light and the level of liquid in the cup.
