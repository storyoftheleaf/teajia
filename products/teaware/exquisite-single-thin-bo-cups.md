Botai (薄胎) means 'thin body.

' These cups are made with walls so thin they approach translucency. The craft required to throw or cast porcelain this thin without it collapsing is considerable.

## Terroir

Chinese thin porcelain tradition, typically Jingdezhen.

## Processing

Ultra-thin porcelain body. The walls are thin enough to feel the heat of the tea through them, connecting the drinker physically to the temperature of the brew.

## Mood

delicate heat

## Experience

You feel the tea's temperature through the wall before you taste it. That connection between hand and brew is what thin porcelain gives you.
