Side-handle (侧把) teapots are an alternative to overhead-handle designs, offering a different pouring ergonomic suited to small-cup gongfu service.

The rock mine clay gives this pot a rough, mineral texture.

## Terroir

Chinese mineral clay deposits. Rock mine clay is less refined than Yixing zisha, giving it a raw, earthy quality.

## Processing

Rock mine clay (矿石泥) is a coarser, more textured clay than refined zisha. It retains heat well and develops a distinctive rough patina over use.

## Mood

rustic warmth

## Experience

The side handle changes how you pour. More wrist, less arm. The rough clay texture feels honest in the hand. Good for shou and dark teas.
