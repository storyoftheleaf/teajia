Pure silver tea vessels have a long history in Chinese and Japanese tea culture. Silver ionizes water slightly during boiling, producing a softer, sweeter quality that experienced brewers describe as 'silky.

' The 999 designation means 99. 9% pure silver, the highest standard.

## Terroir

Silver teaware craft exists across China and Japan. Hand-hammered silver kettles are particularly valued in Japanese sencha and Chinese gongfu traditions.

## Processing

Hand-hammered 999 pure silver. The hammer marks are not decorative but structural, strengthening the thin silver walls. Pure silver is naturally antibacterial and does not corrode or leach harmful compounds.

## Experience

Silver changes water. The difference is subtle but real: a softness and sweetness that other materials do not produce. Reserve this for your finest teas. The vessel deserves leaf that matches its quality.
