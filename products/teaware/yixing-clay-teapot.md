Yixing zisha (purple sand) teapots are the most revered brewing vessels in Chinese tea culture.

The tradition dates to the Song Dynasty, with the craft reaching its peak during the Ming and Qing periods. Every serious tea drinker eventually arrives at Yixing.

## Terroir

Yixing city, Jiangsu province. The clay deposits around Dingshu town, particularly from Huanglongshan mountain, produce the three classic zisha clays: zini (purple), zhuni (vermillion), and duanni (golden). Each clay type interacts differently with tea.

## Processing

Yixing zisha clay is mined from deposits in Jiangsu province and processed into a workable material through months of weathering, crushing, and sieving. The clay's unique double-pore structure absorbs tea oils while allowing micro-ventilation, enhancing flavor over hundreds of uses.

## Experience

Dedicate each pot to a single tea type. Over months, the clay remembers what you brew. A well-seasoned Yixing pot adds a depth to the cup that no other vessel can replicate.
