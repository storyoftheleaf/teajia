Tang Ji is a Taiwanese pottery name with roots in traditional Chinese teapot craft.

Taiwanese clay teapots occupy a space between Yixing formality and Chaozhou utility, made for daily gongfu brewing without pretension.

## Terroir

Taiwan. Local clay traditions developed alongside the island's oolong culture, producing pots designed for the specific demands of high-mountain and roasted Taiwanese oolongs.

## Processing

Taiwanese clay body, hand-thrown or mold-formed. The clay absorbs tea oils over time, developing a patina specific to whatever tea is brewed in it most often.

## Mood

steady companion

## Experience

A working pot. Brew oolong in it daily and it will repay you with a surface that improves every session.
