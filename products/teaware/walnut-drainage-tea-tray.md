A drainage tea tray (排水茶盘) collects overflow water during gongfu brewing, where rinsing and warming vessels is part of the ritual.

Walnut wood adds warmth to the tea table.

## Terroir

## Processing

Solid walnut with integrated drainage system. The wood is sealed to resist water damage while maintaining the natural grain and warmth of walnut.
