The 'three non-jump' (三不跳) refers to a lid that stays perfectly seated during pouring from three different angles.

This is a mark of precise craftsmanship in Chaozhou teapot making.

## Terroir

Chaozhou, Guangdong province.

## Processing

Chaozhou clay with precision-fitted lid. The three-way lid stability test is a traditional quality benchmark for Chaozhou pots.

## Mood

precise craft

## Experience

The lid test tells you everything about the maker's skill. A pot this precise pours clean and seals tight between steeps.
