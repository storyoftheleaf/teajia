Douli (斗笠) cups are shaped like an inverted conical hat, wide at the rim and narrow at the base.

This form exposes more tea surface area to air, cooling the liquor faster and releasing aroma more effectively than cylindrical cups.

## Terroir

Jingdezhen, Jiangxi province.

## Processing

Jingdezhen porcelain in the classic douli (hat) form. The wide opening and shallow depth are functional: they cool the tea and concentrate the aroma.

## Mood

open aroma

## Experience

The wide mouth changes how you experience tea. More aroma reaches your nose before you sip. The shape is ancient and the reason is practical: it makes tea smell better.
