The Chaoshan wind stove (风炉) is one of the essential tools of traditional Chaozhou gongfu tea.

This style of kettle sits atop a small charcoal or alcohol stove, keeping water at temperature throughout a session. The wind stove setup is the original slow tea practice.

## Terroir

Chaoshan region (Chaozhou, Shantou, Jieyang) in Guangdong province. The gongfu tea ceremony originated here, and the wind stove setup is its defining equipment.

## Processing

Stainless steel body designed for use with traditional Chaoshan-style charcoal wind stoves. The form follows a specific regional tradition where the kettle, stove, and pot work as an integrated system.

## Mood

ritual precision

## Experience

Part of the traditional Chaoshan four treasures of gongfu tea. Best used with a charcoal stove for the full experience.
