A traditional Chaozhou red clay charcoal stove (炭炉).

In the Chaoshan gongfu tradition, charcoal heat is preferred over electric because it produces a steadier, more even temperature. The red clay body retains and radiates heat gently.

## Terroir

Chaozhou, Guangdong province. Part of the traditional gongfu four treasures.

## Processing

Red clay body designed for charcoal burning. The clay insulates the fire and radiates heat evenly to the kettle above.

## Mood

slow fire
