Bone china is the thinnest, most translucent ceramic material available.

Adding bone ash to the porcelain formula creates a body that can be thrown incredibly thin while remaining strong. The result is cups that glow when held to light.

## Terroir

Bone china originated in England but is now produced across China and Japan. The Chinese bone china industry has grown substantially in the past two decades.

## Processing

Bone china (骨瓷) with ultra-thin walls. Bone ash content typically 30-50% creates the characteristic translucency and warmth of tone that standard porcelain cannot achieve.

## Mood

translucent warmth

## Experience

Light passes through these walls. The tea glows from within the cup. Bone china also feels warmer to the touch than standard porcelain, adding a subtle comfort to the drinking experience.
