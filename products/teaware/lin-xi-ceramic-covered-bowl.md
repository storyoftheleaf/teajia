The gaiwan (盖碗, covered bowl) is the most versatile brewing vessel in Chinese tea.

Unlike a teapot, a gaiwan does not absorb flavor, making it suitable for any tea type. It is the tool of choice for professional tea tasters and the default vessel for anyone who wants to taste tea as it truly is.

## Terroir

Chinese ceramic tradition. Gaiwans are produced across China's major ceramic centers.

## Processing

Ceramic body with lid and saucer. The three-piece design allows precise control of steeping time and pour angle. A well-made gaiwan has a lid that fits snugly without being too tight, and a lip thin enough for comfortable handling.

## Mood

essential clarity

## Experience

The gaiwan hides nothing. Whatever is in the leaf comes through without the clay's influence. Every tea drinker needs at least one.
