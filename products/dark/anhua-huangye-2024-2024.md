Wild Anhua dark tea from 2024.

The 荒野 (huangye) designation indicates uncultivated, naturally growing tea plants. Wild material in Anhua typically comes from old mountains where tea was once cultivated and has since been abandoned, with the plants reverting to a semi-wild state.

## Terroir

Anhua county, Hunan province. Wild tea mountains in the region where formerly cultivated plants have grown without human management for years or decades.

## Processing

Anhua dark tea processing applied to wild-harvested material. Young production with active microbial development still in its early stages.

## Mood

wild freshness

## Experience

Fresh and herbaceous with the distinctive breadth that wild-harvested material brings. Young and still developing. The wild character sets it apart from cultivated Anhua tea even at this early stage.
