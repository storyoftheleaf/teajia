A 1990 liu bao carrying over thirty years of quiet transformation.

Younger than the 1980 version but still well into aged territory. The additional decade of youth shows in slightly more pronounced flavor and less of the ethereal quality that extreme age brings.

## Terroir

Guangxi province, southern China. Liu bao production is centered in Cangwu county where the warm, humid climate and specific soil conditions support the microbial cultures that define this tea's character.

## Processing

Standard liu bao processing with extended basket aging. Thirty-plus years have mellowed the fermentation character while developing the characteristic cooling notes and aged sweetness.

## Mood

easy stillness

## Experience

Smooth and approachable with a gentle earthiness. The cooling sensation is present but more subtle than in older examples. A good daily tea for someone who appreciates depth without intensity.
