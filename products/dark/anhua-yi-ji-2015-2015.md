First Grade (一级) Anhua dark tea from 2015, ten years into its aging journey.

Grade classifications in Chinese tea refer to leaf size and quality, with First Grade indicating the finest, most tender material. A decade of aging has brought this into comfortable drinking territory.

## Terroir

Anhua county, Hunan province. First-grade material typically comes from spring harvests when the buds and young leaves are most tender and concentrated in flavor.

## Processing

Anhua dark tea processing with first-grade leaf material. The finer leaf grades undergo the same fundamental processing as coarser grades but develop differently during aging due to the smaller leaf surface area and higher bud content.

## Mood

smooth maturity

## Experience

Ten years have rounded the edges. The mushroom and wood notes are fully integrated and the astringency is gone. A comfortable, satisfying dark tea that rewards the patience of proper aging. Approaching its prime drinking window.
