Liu An basket tea from 1990, a distinctive dark tea from Anhui province that is often confused with liu bao but follows its own tradition entirely.

Packed in small bamboo baskets lined with bamboo leaves, liu an was historically exported to Southeast Asia as a medicinal and daily drinking tea.

## Terroir

Qimen county in Anhui province, better known for its red tea (Keemun). Liu An tea comes from a parallel tradition in the same region, using different cultivars and processing. The foggy mountains and rich soil of southern Anhui give the leaf a clean, mineral quality.

## Processing

Liu An processing involves a light roast followed by packing into bamboo baskets and sealing for long-term aging. The bamboo lining creates a unique micro-environment that flavors the tea over decades. This is not a wo dui tea. The fermentation is entirely natural and slow.

## Experience

A gentle tea with a distinctive bamboo note that you will not find in any other category. The flavor is quiet and clean, the body is light, and there is a warmth that settles without heaviness. Best in the evening.
