Liu bao is a dark tea from Guangxi. San He (Three Cranes) is the house brand of the Wuzhou Tea Factory, founded in 1953 and the best-known name in the category.

## Terroir

Wuzhou city in Guangxi province, where the factory sits along the Xijiang river. The region's subtropical heat and humidity support the slow post-fermentation that liu bao requires.

## Processing

Factory-processed liu bao with wo dui pile fermentation, then aged in basket form. Factory work at this level runs cleaner and more refined than village production. This 1990s lot was expertly stored in Guangzhou.
