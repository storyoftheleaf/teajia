A liu bao (六堡) dark tea from Guangxi, made by the San He brand of the Wuzhou Tea Factory.

## Lore

San He, meaning Three Cranes, is the house brand of the Wuzhou Tea Factory, founded in 1953. Wuzhou is where modern liu bao production was concentrated, and the factory became the best-known name in the category, its mark widely respected across southern China and Southeast Asia.

## Terroir

The tea comes from Wuzhou, a city in Guangxi province on the Xijiang river. The area is subtropical and warm-humid, the kind of climate that supports the slow fermentation dark tea depends on. Only Wuzhou-grown leaf made in the traditional way may carry the liu bao name.

## Processing

Liu bao is a hei cha, a dark tea, and not a puerh. It is made through wet piling, where leaves are heaped so heat, moisture, and microbes can work on them over weeks. This 1990s lot was pressed into basket form and later held in Guangzhou, where the humid air pushes aging along. Aged liu bao from this style tends to drink dark, smooth, and gently sweet.
