A 2022 wild Anhua dark tea, three years into its aging.

This sits between the fresh 2024 and the more developed cultivated first-grade teas, offering a snapshot of how wild material ages differently from garden-grown leaf.

## Terroir

Anhua county, Hunan province. Wild tea mountains at various elevations in the Anhua region.

## Processing

Wild-harvested Anhua dark tea with three years of natural aging. The microbial transformation is underway and the initial rawness has begun to mellow.

## Mood

settling earth

## Experience

Three years have begun to smooth the wild edges. The mushroom note is emerging more clearly and the astringency is fading. An intermediate stage that shows where this tea is heading.
