A 2020 first-grade Anhua dark tea, five years old.

Still young by dark tea standards but beginning to show the character that aging develops. Comparing this alongside the 2015 version provides a clear lesson in what five additional years of transformation look like.

## Terroir

Anhua county, Hunan province. Spring-harvested first-grade material from cultivated Anhua tea gardens.

## Processing

First-grade Anhua dark tea processing. At five years, the microbial transformation is well underway but not yet complete. The golden flower fungus has had time to establish but the full integration of flavors is still developing.

## Mood

developing patience

## Experience

Noticeably younger than the 2015 vintage. There is still some astringency and the flavors have not yet fully merged. But the foundation is clear: good material, proper processing, and the promise of depth to come. Drink some, save the rest.
