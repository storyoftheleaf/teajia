San He (Three Cranes) is the most recognized liu bao brand, produced by the Wuzhou Tea Factory in Guangxi since 1953.

A 1999 pressing from this factory carries the institutional weight of China's most established liu bao producer. Twenty-five years of aging have brought it into its prime drinking window.

## Terroir

Wuzhou city in Guangxi province, where the factory sits along the Xijiang river. The factory's aging warehouses benefit from the region's consistent subtropical heat and humidity, creating ideal conditions for the slow post-fermentation that liu bao requires.

## Processing

Factory-processed liu bao with controlled wo dui fermentation and compressed into cake form. The Three Cranes factory is known for a cleaner, more refined fermentation profile than village-produced liu bao.

## Mood

steady presence

## Experience

Clean and reliable. No surprises, no rough edges. It does what liu bao is supposed to do: settle you, cool you, and leave a clean sweetness in the throat. A tea you can trust every day.
