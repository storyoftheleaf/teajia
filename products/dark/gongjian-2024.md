Gongjian (贡尖) means 'tribute tip' and represents one of the three traditional grades of loose Anhua dark tea (the others being Tianjian and Shengjian).

Gongjian uses the finest, most tender tips and is historically the most prized grade. A 2024 production means this is very young and will develop substantially over the coming years.

## Terroir

Anhua county, Hunan province. Gongjian-grade material comes from the earliest spring picking, selecting only the most tender shoots. The Anhua terroir and microbial environment are essential to the character.

## Processing

Gongjian-grade Anhua dark tea processing: the finest tips and young leaves, killed green, rolled, piled for fermentation, and dried. The tender material produces a more refined, lighter-bodied dark tea compared to coarser grades.

## Mood

refined beginning

## Experience

Lighter and more delicate than you expect from dark tea. The young age means it is still developing, but the gongjian grade gives it a refinement from the start. Sweet, clean, and promising.
