An aged 1980 loose-leaf pu-erh (san cha) carrying the Liao Fu name — a Hong Kong tea house that began importing border-region tea from the Yunnan/northern Vietnam line in the 1960s. 'Wan Yan' (万言, 'Ten Thousand Words') Triple-A designates the premium classification within the Liao Fu line, indicating select leaf.

## Terroir

Wild tea trees in the north of Vietnam, on the border with Yunnan. This is the large-leaf assamica family (Shan Tuyet) that grows at high altitude in northern Vietnam's Ha Giang, Lao Cai, Yen Bai, and Son La provinces — the same large-leaf stock as Yunnan pu-erh. The Liao Fu name (廖福, Hong Kong house Liao Fu San) denotes this Vietnamese, Yunnan-border leaf puer traded through Hong Kong.

## Processing

Loose-leaf (san cha) wild-tree puer from 1980 at the premium Wan Yan AAA grade, in the raw/sheng tradition, held in dry storage. The AAA designation selects the finest leaf within the Liao Fu line. Over four decades of aging in storage have developed aged depth in the preserved leaves.
