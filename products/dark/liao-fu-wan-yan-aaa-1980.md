Liao Fu 'Ten Thousand Words' (万言) Triple-A grade represents the highest classification within the Liao Fu dark tea lineage.

The AAA grading indicates premium leaf selection and processing. A 1980 production at this grade level is genuinely rare, representing over four decades of transformation on already exceptional material.

## Terroir

Guangxi province, southern China. Liao Fu is a respected name in Guangxi dark tea production. The AAA grading suggests leaf sourced from established, high-quality gardens. The subtropical climate and specific microbial environment of Guangxi are essential to this tea's character.

## Processing

Premium-grade Liao Fu dark tea from 1980. The AAA designation and 'Ten Thousand Words' name suggest the finest leaf selection within an already distinguished producer. Over four decades of aging have developed extraordinary depth and the characteristic cooling sensation of aged Guangxi dark tea.

## Mood

silent depth

## Experience

The forest in the name is accurate. There is something vast and quiet about this tea. The flavors are deep and layered, the cooling sensation is pronounced, and the body is substantial. It fills a room with its presence while remaining completely still.
