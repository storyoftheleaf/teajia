Wild-harvested Anhua dark tea from 2023.

Anhua county in Hunan province is the birthplace of Chinese dark tea (hei cha), with a production tradition stretching back centuries. Wild (荒野) tea refers to plants growing without cultivation, often on abandoned tea mountains or in naturally occurring populations.

## Terroir

Anhua county, Hunan province. The mountainous terrain, rivers, and specific fungal ecology of this region create conditions that support the unique microbial cultures (especially Eurotium cristatum, the 'golden flower' fungus) essential to Anhua dark tea production.

## Processing

Anhua dark tea processing: kill-green, rolling, piling for microbial fermentation, then drying. Wild-harvested material brings a broader flavor profile than cultivated plants. As a 2023 production, this is young for dark tea and will continue developing character over years.

## Mood

fresh earth

## Experience

Young and vibrant for a dark tea. The woody and mushroom notes are clear but not yet deep. The wild-harvested character adds a breadth of flavor that cultivated Anhua tea does not have. A tea to drink now and also to set aside for future comparison.
