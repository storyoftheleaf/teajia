Jia Ji (Grade A) tuo cha from the Menghai factory, pressed in 1998.

Tuo cha is one of the oldest compressed tea formats, shaped like a bird's nest for ease of transport along ancient trade routes. This piece uses higher-grade leaf than standard production, reflected in the Jia Ji designation.

## Terroir

Menghai county, Xishuangbanna, Yunnan. The Menghai Tea Factory has been a center of pu-erh production since 1940. Leaf sourced for Jia Ji grade typically comes from established gardens at moderate elevation with consistent quality.

## Processing

Sun-dried Yunnan large-leaf maocha, compressed into tuo form. Despite the 'sheng' label, twenty-five years of aging in warm storage have brought significant transformation. The tea sits in the space between young sheng and fully aged.

## Mood

patient transition

## Experience

Somewhere between bright and deep. The original sheng character is still faintly present, but age has rounded it considerably. Sweet, woody, with moments of camphor that surface and fade across steepings.
