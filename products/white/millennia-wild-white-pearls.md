White tea hand-rolled into dragon pearl form from wild, ancient tea trees.

Dragon pearl rolling is labor-intensive, requiring each pearl to be shaped individually while the leaf is still pliable. The combination of wild ancient-tree material with this careful shaping represents significant craft investment.

## Terroir

Yunnan's old-growth forest tea regions. Wild ancient trees produce leaf with the complexity and depth that cultivated gardens cannot match. The dragon pearl format concentrates this character into each individual piece.

## Processing

Wild ancient-tree leaf processed as white tea (withered and dried) then hand-rolled into individual pearl shapes. Each pearl unfurls over multiple infusions, releasing flavor gradually. The rolling process slightly bruises the leaf, adding a faint oxidation layer to the white tea base.

## Mood

unfolding grace

## Experience

Watch the pearls open. Each one unfurls slowly in the hot water, releasing waves of sweetness and floral notes. The first infusion is delicate; by the third, the full depth of the ancient-tree material is expressed. A tea that rewards patience and attention.
