Purple bud white tea from wild trees in Yunnan.

Purple-leafed tea varieties contain elevated anthocyanins, the same compounds found in blueberries and red wine. When processed as white tea with minimal intervention, the natural chemistry of these unusual leaves creates a profile unlike any standard white tea.

## Terroir

Yunnan's wild tea forests. Purple bud tea trees are a natural genetic variant found among old-growth populations. They grow in mixed forest environments at moderate to high elevation, their purple coloring a response to UV exposure at altitude.

## Processing

Minimal processing: sun-withered and air-dried. The purple bud material is handled gently to preserve its natural anthocyanin content and distinctive color. The 'tender' designation indicates young, early-spring buds were selected.

## Mood

wild sweetness

## Experience

A genuinely unusual tea. There is a berry-like quality that you do not find in any other white tea. The body is light but the flavor is more complex than its appearance suggests. A tea that challenges assumptions about what white tea can be.
