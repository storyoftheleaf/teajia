Zhenghe is the other great white tea city alongside Fuding, and the two produce noticeably different styles.

Zhenghe white tea tends toward a fuller, more robust character with deeper flavors, while Fuding leans delicate and floral. This 2008 example has had nearly two decades of aging to develop.

## Terroir

Zhenghe county, northern Fujian province. Zhenghe sits at higher elevation than Fuding with a cooler, more continental climate. The mountain environment and different cultivar produce a white tea with more structure and body, designed to age well.

## Processing

Zhenghe-style white tea processing, which typically involves more indoor withering in longer sessions compared to Fuding's methods. The Zhenghe Da Bai cultivar produces a larger leaf with more body. Eighteen years of aging have transformed this significantly.

## Mood

deep calm

## Experience

This has the weight that aged Zhenghe white tea is known for. It brews darker than you expect from white tea, with a honeyed, woody sweetness and a warmth in the body. Almost closer to an aged oolong in character. Serious and satisfying.
