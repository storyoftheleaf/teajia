A 2016 Fujian white tea pressed into cake form.

Cake-pressed white tea is a relatively modern development that allows the tea to age in a controlled, compact format. Fujian is the birthplace of white tea, and this eight-year-old cake sits in the early stages of the transformation that white tea undergoes over time.

## Terroir

Fujian province, the original and still primary source of white tea. The specific microclimate of Fujian's coastal mountains, with their marine influence and cool highland temperatures, produces leaf with a delicate sweetness and floral quality unique to this region.

## Processing

Minimal processing: withered and dried with almost no human intervention. White tea is the least processed of all tea categories. Pressing into cake form after initial drying creates conditions for slow, natural transformation over the years.

## Mood

gentle patience

## Experience

Light gold in the cup with a softness that makes you slow down. The hay and rose notes are present but not assertive. Eight years of aging have begun to add depth, but this tea is still young by white tea standards. It will continue to change.
