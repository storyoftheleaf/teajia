A 2009 Fuding white tea, likely processed as Shou Mei or Bai Mu Dan grade.

Fuding city in Fujian is one of the two major white tea production centers (alongside Zhenghe). At fifteen years old, this tea has entered the aged white tea category where traditional Chinese medicine practitioners value it for its warming properties.

## Terroir

Fuding city, northeastern Fujian province. Fuding's coastal mountain terroir, influenced by Pacific winds and consistent fog, produces white tea with a distinctive clarity and sweetness. The Da Bai (Big White) cultivar that grows here is the foundation of most commercial white tea.

## Processing

Fuding-style white tea processing: long outdoor withering under controlled conditions, then gentle drying. The 'eco' designation suggests environmentally conscious cultivation. Fifteen years of natural aging have darkened the leaf and deepened the flavor profile significantly.

## Mood

settled warmth

## Experience

This has moved beyond young white tea territory. The delicacy has given way to warmth. Dried dates and honey with a smoothness that aged white tea develops after a decade. In Chinese tradition, this is the age where white tea transitions from a beverage to a tonic.
