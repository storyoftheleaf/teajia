Yun Shen Chu (云深处) translates to 'deep in the clouds,' a name that evokes the high-altitude, mist-shrouded forests where this tea originates.

It is a poetic name in the Chinese tradition of naming teas for the feeling of their origin rather than their technical specifications.

## Terroir

High-altitude tea forests in Yunnan, above the cloud line. Gardens at these elevations are frequently immersed in mist, which filters sunlight and creates growing conditions that produce exceptionally delicate, aromatic leaf.

## Processing

White tea processed with minimal intervention. The name suggests high-altitude origin where fog and cloud cover slow leaf growth and concentrate aromatic compounds. Withered and dried naturally.

## Mood

above the clouds

## Experience

The name tells you how it drinks. There is a lightness and clarity that feels like being at elevation. The sweetness is present but never heavy, the floral notes drift rather than insist. A tea for quiet mornings.
