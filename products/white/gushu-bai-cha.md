Ancient-tree white tea from Yunnan's mountain forests.

The combination of old-growth tree material processed as white tea is a relatively recent practice that produces some of the most complex and age-worthy white teas available. The ancient trees contribute a depth and body that young garden bushes cannot provide.

## Terroir

Yunnan's mountain tea forests, likely in Xishuangbanna, Lincang, or Pu-erh prefecture. Ancient trees at moderate to high elevation in mixed forest environments. The deep root systems and old-growth ecosystem contribute mineral complexity impossible to replicate in cultivated gardens.

## Processing

Minimal processing of ancient-tree leaf: extended sun-withering and natural drying. The large, mature leaves from old trees require longer withering than garden material. The result is a white tea with more body and structure than standard production.

## Mood

mountain stillness

## Experience

Full-bodied for a white tea, with a thickness in the liquor that coats the tongue. The honey and dried fruit notes are vivid and the finish is exceptionally long. This will age beautifully over the coming decades.
