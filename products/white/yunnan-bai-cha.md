A simple Yunnan white tea.

Without a tree age or specific origin designation, this is everyday white tea from Yunnan's broad tea-growing regions. Yunnan white tea differs from Fujian white tea in its leaf size, body, and flavor profile, tending toward a fuller, more robust character.

## Terroir

Yunnan province. White tea production in Yunnan is widespread across the major tea-growing prefectures. The warm climate and large-leaf cultivar create a white tea with more body than its Fujian counterparts.

## Processing

Minimal processing: withered and dried. Yunnan white tea uses the province's large-leaf cultivar, which produces a bigger, more substantial white tea than Fujian's Da Bai variety. The simplicity of the processing lets the leaf speak.

## Mood

gentle ease

## Experience

Soft, sweet, and easy. A white tea you can brew without fuss and drink without analysis. The Yunnan large-leaf base gives it more body than you might expect from white tea. Good for any time of day when you want something light but not empty.
