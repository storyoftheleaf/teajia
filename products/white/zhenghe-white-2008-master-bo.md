A 2008 Zhenghe white tea cake from Master Bo's collection.

Zhenghe is the other great white tea city alongside Fuding, and the two styles are distinct. Zhenghe white tends fuller, more robust, with deeper body and less of Fuding's delicate floral character. Compressed into cake form and aged nearly two decades, this has crossed well into aged white tea territory where the Chinese medicinal tradition considers it most valuable.

## Terroir

Zhenghe county, northern Fujian province. Higher elevation and cooler climate than Fuding, with a more continental character. The Zhenghe Da Bai cultivar produces larger, more structured leaves than Fuding's equivalent. The mountain environment — fog, rain, and acidic soil — builds a white tea designed for long aging.

## Processing

Zhenghe-style white tea: extended indoor withering in longer sessions than Fuding methods, then gentle drying and compression into cake. Eighteen years of aging have darkened the leaf significantly and developed the complex sweetness that aged white tea is prized for.

## Mood

settled warmth

## Experience

This has left young white tea far behind. Dark honey and dried jujube dominate, with a woody warmth and a hint of the medicinal quality that old white tea develops. It brews darker than you expect, closer to aged oolong in color and body. The Zhenghe structure gives it more backbone than aged Fuding white.
