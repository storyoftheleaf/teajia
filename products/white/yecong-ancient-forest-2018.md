Yecong (wild bush) ancient forest white tea from 2018.

This style uses leaf from wild or semi-wild tea trees growing in old-growth forest conditions rather than cultivated gardens. The resulting tea carries a broader, wilder flavor profile than garden-grown white tea.

## Terroir

Ancient tea forests in Yunnan or Fujian province. Wild bush (yecong) tea trees grow without human management in forest ecosystems, their character shaped entirely by the surrounding environment: forest canopy, natural soil biology, and the absence of agricultural inputs.

## Processing

Minimal processing applied to ancient forest leaf. The wild-growing trees produce larger, more robust leaves that require slightly longer withering. The 'ancient forest' designation means these trees grow in mixed forest rather than managed gardens.

## Mood

wild clarity

## Experience

There is something distinctly 'forest' about this tea that garden white teas do not have. A green, herbaceous quality underneath the expected sweetness. It tastes like the place it grew, not just the processing it received.
