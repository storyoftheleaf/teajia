Yue Chen Yue Xiang (越陈越香) is a phrase used across Chinese tea culture meaning 'the older, the more fragrant.

' It is typically associated with pu-erh but applies equally to well-made white tea. Using this phrase as a name signals that this tea was made with aging in mind, processed and stored to improve over years and decades.

## Terroir

Yunnan province. The phrase 'the older, the more fragrant' implies confidence in the raw material's quality. Only leaf from established trees with sufficient complexity can sustain and develop over extended aging periods.

## Processing

White tea processed for long-term aging potential. The specific techniques that make white tea age-worthy include proper moisture content at the time of storage and sufficient leaf maturity to sustain decades of slow transformation.

This is a tea with a future. Whatever it tastes like now is a starting point. The dried date and honey notes will deepen. The body will thicken. In five years it will be different. In ten, unrecognizable. A tea to buy, store, and revisit.
